# Research interests for arXiv digest scoring

## High priority
- Strong gravitational lensing detection, especially with ML/foundation models
- Multimodal foundation models for astronomy (AION-1 and similar)
- Vision Transformers and CNNs applied to survey imaging
- Self-calibration and image mosaicking methods
- Euclid and SPHEREx mission science and methods

## Medium priority
- Cosmology: large-scale structure, weak lensing, clustering
- Anomaly detection / rare object discovery in survey data
- UMAP and representation learning on astronomical embeddings
- Photometric redshifts, deblending

## Low priority / context
- General ML methods papers (only if directly applicable to imaging)
- Cosmological simulations (only if novel methodology)

## Skip
- Pure theory cosmology with no observational hook
- Stellar physics, exoplanets, solar physics
- ML papers on NLP, audio, or non-imaging domains
