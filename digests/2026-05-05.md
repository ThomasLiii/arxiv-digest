## High priority (1)

**Euclid preparation. CosmoPostProcess: A simulation calibrated framework for weak lensing selection bias in richness-selected galaxy clusters** · Euclid Collaboration, R. Ingrao, S. Borgani, M. Costanzi et al. · [arXiv:2605.02723](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.02723v1)
- Why it matters: Direct Euclid mission methodology paper on weak-lensing selection bias for the cluster cosmology pipeline — squarely on the high-priority list.
- TL;DR: The team builds a forward-modelling pipeline that paints galaxies onto N-body halos, mimics Euclid's richness selection (no colour cut), and emulates miscentring to deliver corrections for stacked lensing profiles. They quantify projection-induced selection bias as a 20–40% boost near the one-halo / two-halo transition, growing with redshift, and fold in baryonic corrections that stay sub-2% outside the core. Output is a calibrated radial-profile correction with uncertainties intended for Euclid DR1 cluster cosmology.

## Medium priority (4)

**Tracing Primordial Gravitational Waves via non-Gaussian Signatures of Halo Bias** · M. Abdelaziz, P. Bari, S. Matarrese, A. Ricciardone · [arXiv:2605.02882](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.02882v1)
- LSS as a new probe of primordial gravitational waves: a tensor-induced bispectrum gives a scale-dependent halo-bias correction reaching order-unity for rare z=7 halos, distinct from standard PNG templates.

**Beyond collective fluctuations: probing micro-image swarms in lensed quasars with intensity interferometry** · A. K. Meena, P. Saha · [arXiv:2605.02181](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.02181v1)
- Proposes Hanbury Brown–Twiss intensity interferometry on the brightest strongly lensed quasars (QSO 2237+0305, PS J0147+4630) to resolve the swarm of micro-images that current photometric monitoring averages over.

**Using Lyα Transmitted Spectrum to Probe IGM Transmission and Identify Ionized Structures in Cosmic Reionization** · W. Hu, C. Papovich, L. Shen, P. Arrabal Haro et al. · [arXiv:2605.02539](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.02539v1)
- Uses 143 JWST/NIRSpec galaxy sightlines from CAPERS+JADES to map IGM transmission and flag a ~110 cMpc ionized bubble in GOODS-S coincident with a known z≈5.75–6 overdensity.

**PDRS: A Linear O(N) Algorithm for Segmentation of High-Activity Regions in Irregularly Sampled Time Series** · A. Agrawal · [arXiv:2605.02843](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.02843v1)
- A peak-seeded, breadth-first segmentation method that replaces the O(N²) Bayesian-Blocks + hill-climbing combo for transient detection in ZTF/LSST-scale irregularly sampled light curves.
