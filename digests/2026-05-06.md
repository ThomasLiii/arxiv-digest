## High priority

**High-Redshift Gravitational Lens Discoveries in JWST NIRCam Using AnomalyMatch** · Julia Dima, David O'Ryan, Sandor Kruk et al. · [arXiv:2605.03442](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03442v1)
- Why it matters: Direct hit on the top-priority interest — ML-based strong-lens detection in survey imaging, using a semi-supervised anomaly-detection pipeline on JWST NIRCam data.
- TL;DR: Starting from only eleven known lenses, the authors run AnomalyMatch (a semi-supervised iterative human-in-the-loop classifier) over ASTRODEEP and COSMOS-Web JWST imaging and surface 58 lens candidates, 37 of them previously uncatalogued. Expert grading yields 16 Grade A, 16 Grade B, and 26 Grade C systems extending to photometric redshift z ≈ 2.1, demonstrating that anomaly-style learners can mine JWST archives for rare lensed objects with very little training data.

**Euclid preparation: Baryon acoustic oscillations extraction techniques — comparison and optimisation** · Euclid Collaboration, E. Sarpa, A. Veropalumbo et al. · [arXiv:2605.03446](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03446v1)
- Why it matters: Euclid mission methods paper validating the end-to-end BAO pipeline that DR1 cosmology will rely on.
- TL;DR: The team runs the full Euclid BAO chain — density-field reconstruction, two-point correlation, parameter inference — on Flagship-I mock catalogues spanning 0.9 ≤ z ≤ 1.8, comparing the RecSym and RecIso Zel'dovich reconstruction schemes. They introduce an emulator + HMC inference stack (>500× faster than MCMC) and a semi-analytic covariance estimator, showing both reconstructions are unbiased, triple the BAO figure of merit, and yield ~10% precision on Ωₘ and ~3% on H₀ rₛ when redshift bins are combined.

## Medium priority

**Mitigating effects of telescope jitter through differentiable forward-modeling** · Max Charles, Louis Desdoigts, Benjamin Pope et al. · [arXiv:2605.03445](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03445v1)
- Why it matters: Differentiable optical forward models are increasingly the backbone of modern self-calibration / PSF inference for survey imaging, with explicit applicability to JWST NIRISS.
- TL;DR: A JAX-based differentiable optics framework (dLux) is used to model and remove low-, medium-, and high-frequency pointing jitter, evaluated on the TOLIMAN CubeSat astrometric mission.

**VERSUS: An excursion-set-inspired void-finder for the Stage-IV era** · Nathan Findlay, Seshadri Nadathur · [arXiv:2605.03779](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03779v1)
- Why it matters: Void cosmology tooling tuned to Stage-IV (Euclid/DESI) galaxy samples — large-scale-structure relevant.
- TL;DR: VERSUS finds spherical underdensities consistent with theoretical excursion-set void-size-function predictions over 25–61 h⁻¹ Mpc on AbacusSummit-based mocks, with no post-processing required.

**StreakMind: AI detection and analysis of satellite streaks in astronomical images** · Rafael Carrillo Navarro, René Duffard, Pablo García-Martín et al. · [arXiv:2605.03429](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03429v1)
- Why it matters: ML-based detection of rare/anomalous features (streaks) in survey imaging, with cross-matching to orbital catalogues — relevant to anomaly/contamination handling in modern surveys.
- TL;DR: A YOLO oriented-bounding-box model trained on 2,335 frames reaches 94% precision and 97% recall, with downstream geometric refinement and satellite cross-identification feeding a relational database.

**The JWST early galaxy crisis resolved by a reionization degeneracy** · Zihan Wang, Huanyuan Shan · [arXiv:2605.03635](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03635v1)
- Why it matters: Reframes the JWST high-z bright-galaxy tension within ΛCDM, relevant for cosmological inference using JWST UV LFs.
- TL;DR: They argue global observables only constrain f_esc × f★,0, and use JWST UVLF shapes to break the degeneracy and exclude the proposed crisis threshold at 4.5σ.

**New constraints on primordial non-Gaussianity from CMB lensing × CIB cross-correlations** · Joseph Thornton, Fiona McCarthy, Carmen Embil Villagra et al. · [arXiv:2605.03783](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03783v1)
- Why it matters: Lensing × LSS cross-correlation cosmology, methodologically adjacent to weak-lensing analyses.
- TL;DR: Dust-cleaned CIB maps cross-correlated with Planck CMB lensing yield f_NL^local = 43 ± 23, doubling prior CIB-based precision.

**Microlensing time-scales and flux magnification probabilities of 204 lensed quasars** · F. Ávila-Vera, V. Motta, E. Mediavilla · [arXiv:2605.03905](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03905v1)
- Why it matters: A homogeneous statistical treatment across the full known lensed-quasar sample — relevant context for any lensing-detection or H₀ work.
- TL;DR: Automatic lens modelling of 204 systems gives a mean Einstein-radius crossing time of 11.3 yr and a ~9% per-image probability of strong-magnification events.
