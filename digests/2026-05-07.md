## Medium priority

**A CNN–Transformer Denoiser for low-S/N Galaxy Spectra: Stellar Population Recovery in Synthetic Tests** · Suk Kim, Joon Hyeop Lee, Soo-Chang Rey · [arXiv:2605.04434](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04434v1)
- Why it matters: A hybrid CNN–Transformer applied to galaxy IFU survey data — adjacent to ViT/CNN-on-imaging interest, with implications for low-surface-brightness recovery without spatial binning.
- TL;DR: The Enhanced U-Net Transformer is trained on 90k synthetic SAMI-like spaxels (MILES SSP basis with on-the-fly noise injection) and reduces full-spectrum RMS residuals by ~94–96% across S/N = 5–20, tightening pPXF mass-weighted age scatter from ~0.41 to ~0.25 dex at S/N = 5 while preserving line profiles around Ca II H, Hδ, Hβ, Mg b and Na D.

**Efficient estimators for power spectrum and bispectrum multipole measurements** · Yunchen Xie, Ruiyang Zhao, Gan Gu et al. · [arXiv:2605.04864](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04864v1)
- Why it matters: Scalable anisotropic clustering estimators are the workhorse for Stage-IV LSS analyses — relevant to weak-lensing × clustering and Euclid/DESI cosmology pipelines.
- TL;DR: Conjugation/parity symmetries of spherical-harmonic-weighted FFTs halve the cost of Yamamoto multipoles, a Legendre-recasting trick further reduces high-order even multipoles to low-ℓ field operations, and a new TripoSH bispectrum estimator plus analytic shot-noise treatment ship in the open-source CosmoNPC package.
