## High priority (≤5)

**Joint probabilistic inference of galaxy redshifts and rest-frame spectra from photometric fluxes with latent diffusion** · Han-Yue Guo, Martin Eriksen · [arXiv:2605.10753](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10753v1)
- Why it matters: A multimodal generative model bridging broadband photometry and spectroscopy is squarely in the AION-1-style foundation-model territory, and the photo-z PDFs are directly useful for Euclid/SPHEREx-type analyses.
- TL;DR: The authors pre-train a spectral autoencoder (SPENDER) on 5M DESI DR1 spectra to build a compact latent space for rest-frame spectra, then learn a latent diffusion model that samples this space conditional on broadband photometric fluxes. The result is a single framework that yields well-calibrated photometric-redshift PDFs and plausible rest-frame spectra per galaxy, recovering the main spectral features that drive redshift information.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**Machine Learning Techniques for Astrophysics and Cosmology: Simulation-Based Inference** · Leander Thiele · [arXiv:2605.10719](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10719v1)
- TL;DR: A pedagogical review of posterior, likelihood, and ratio estimation flavours of SBI, including learned summaries, sequential variants, and diagnostics, with pointers to recent cosmology applications.

**Application of Machine Learning to 21 cm Cosmology** · Hayato Shimabukuro · [arXiv:2605.10105](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10105v1)
- TL;DR: Review of how ML tackles foregrounds, RFI, calibration, and inference bottlenecks for SKA-Low cosmic-dawn and EoR analyses, framed around the non-Gaussian 21 cm signal.

**Demonstrating the Use of the Spherical Fourier Bessel Basis for Large Scale Clustering Systematics Discovery and Mitigation with eBOSS** · Sean Bruton, James R. Cheshire, Olivier Doré et al. · [arXiv:2605.10935](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10935v1)
- TL;DR: The SFB decomposition is used on eBOSS LRG/QSO samples to localize and excise systematics-contaminated modes while preserving fNL information, flagging a likely systematic in the QSO sample.

**Mitigating residual foregrounds and systematic errors in SKA1-Low AA* EoR observations via Bayesian Gaussian Process Regression** · Samit Kumar Pal, Abhirup Datta, Aishrila Mazumder et al. · [arXiv:2605.10928](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10928v1)
- TL;DR: Extends the LOFAR/NenuFAR ML-GPR foreground-removal pipeline to synthetic SKA1-Low AA* EoR datasets and quantifies how residual systematics propagate into the 21 cm power spectrum.

**An agentic framework for gravitational-wave counterpart association in the multi-messenger era** · Yiming Dong, Yacheng Kang, Junjie Zhao et al. · [arXiv:2605.10584](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10584v1)
- TL;DR: Introduces GW-Eyes, an LLM-driven agentic pipeline that triages and associates electromagnetic candidates with GW events to scale multi-messenger follow-up for next-generation detectors.

**From Large Telescopes to the MUltiplexed Survey Telescope (MUST)** · Zheng Cai, Song Huang, Yu Liu et al. · [arXiv:2605.10102](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10102v1)
- TL;DR: Reviews the case for Stage-V multiplexed spectroscopy and presents MUST as a wide-field follow-up facility for the imaging surveys that will soon dominate the data landscape.

**Breaking Free from the Swampland of Impossible Universes through the DESI Portal** · Luis A. Anchordoqui, Dieter Lust · [arXiv:2605.10476](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10476v1)
- TL;DR: Argues that DESI BAO hints of evolving dark energy may relieve the de Sitter swampland tension with ΛCDM and sketches string-theoretic completions that match the data.

**Milky Way Dynamics Favor Dark Matter over Modified Gravity Models** · Zheng-long Wang, Yue-Lin Sming Tsai, Lan Zhang et al. · [arXiv:2605.10857](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10857v1)
- TL;DR: Joint modelling of the MW rotation curve, Gaia vertical phase-space spirals, and the stellar disk excludes MOND at >13σ and STVG at >4σ, with standard halo models fitting all three constraints simultaneously.
