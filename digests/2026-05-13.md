## High priority (≤5)

**Unveiling Hidden Lyman Alpha Emitters in the DESI DR1 Data** · Jui-Kuan Chan, Ting-Wen Lan, J. Xavier Prochaska et al. · [arXiv:2605.12503](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12503v1)
- Why it matters: A CNN doing rare-object discovery in a flagship Stage-IV spectroscopic survey is exactly the imaging/survey-ML overlap I follow, and the LAE catalogue feeds the same z>2 selection problems Euclid/SPHEREx will face.
- TL;DR: The authors train a convolutional classifier and redshift regressor on visually inspected DESI spectra to find Lyman-alpha emitters that the standard pipeline misidentifies because it is not built for z>2 sources. Running on ~2M emission-line-galaxy spectra, the model achieves ~95% purity and completeness and recovers nearly 20,000 bright LAEs in 12 minutes on a single GPU, producing a labelled set that can train future DESI-II redshift pipelines.

**Marginal multi-object multi-frame blind deconvolution** · A. Asensio Ramos · [arXiv:2605.11980](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11980v1)
- Why it matters: Self-calibration / image-reconstruction methodology that bypasses joint MAP estimation is directly relevant to my interest in atmospheric-aberration correction and mosaicking pipelines.
- TL;DR: Conventional ground-based MFBD reconstructions estimate object pixels and atmospheric wavefronts jointly, which tends to leak noise into the inferred PSF. This work integrates out the object analytically and shows that the resulting marginal estimator adds only a log-determinant term to the existing merit function, gains a free regularisation against PSF noise contamination, and is much less sensitive to PSD hyperparameters — enabling plug-and-play deployment in the open-source torchmfbd pipeline.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**Trajectory-Agnostic Asteroid Detection in TESS with Deep Learning** · Brian P. Powell, Jorge Martinez-Palomera, Amy Tuson et al. · [arXiv:2605.12391](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12391v1)
- TL;DR: Two stacked 3D U-Nets ("W-Net") segment moving-object pixels in TESS image cubes with no shift-and-stack priors on speed or direction, with an "Adaptive Normalization" head learning the per-cube data scaling; the framework is pitched as portable to Roman and NEO Surveyor.

**Impact of Calibration Systematics on Dark Energy Constraints from LSST Type Ia Supernovae** · Jonah Medoff, Christopher W. Stubbs, Dillon Brout · [arXiv:2605.12401](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12401v1)
- TL;DR: Quantifies how linear and quadratic LSST passband tilts propagate through SN distance moduli into shifts and contour-area inflation of (w0, wa), setting a concrete calibration-accuracy target for Rubin's dark-energy programme.

**GI BAO as a cosmological consistency check** · Kwan Chuen Chan, Yin Li, Jamie McCullough · [arXiv:2605.11658](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11658v1)
- TL;DR: Presents the first photometric measurement of the density-shear (GI) BAO, using DES Y3, and argues that agreement between GI BAO and density BAO simultaneously validates the shear catalogue, the linear alignment model, and the density BAO itself — a clean systematics diagnostic for Stage-IV weak lensing surveys.

**Measuring cosmic bulk flow with kinetic Sunyaev-Zel'dovich velocity reconstruction** · Suroor Seher Gandhi, Matthew C. Johnson, Jordan Krywonos et al. · [arXiv:2605.12499](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12499v1)
- TL;DR: Combines WISE/unWISE galaxy maps with Planck CMB temperature to reconstruct large-scale peculiar velocities in six tomographic bins out to R ~ 2 Gpc, placing ΛCDM-consistent bulk-flow upper limits that are in tension with the CatWISE quasar-dipole bulk-flow interpretation.

**Unveiling f(R) Gravity with Void-Galaxy Cross-Correlation Multipoles** · Yue Nan · [arXiv:2605.12482](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12482v1)
- TL;DR: Semi-analytic computation of monopole, dipole, and quadrupole of the redshift-space void-galaxy cross-correlation in Hu-Sawicki f(R), predicting a void-size-dependent deviation from ΛCDM (up to ~30% in small voids) that DESI, PFS, Euclid, and Roman should be able to test.
