## High priority (≤5)

**Machine-learning applications for weak-lensing cosmology** · Masato Shirasaki · [arXiv:2605.12877](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12877)
- Why it matters: A topical review on ML applied to weak lensing — directly central to my interests in cosmology, dark matter from imaging surveys, and the methods that will feed Euclid/Rubin-era analyses.
- TL;DR: Shirasaki surveys the state of ML-augmented weak-lensing pipelines, starting from how cosmic-shear estimators are built from current galaxy-imaging surveys and where traditional analyses run out of room. The review then walks through where neural-network methods are easing those bottlenecks — shape measurement, summary statistics beyond two-point, systematics mitigation — and sketches what is needed for ML pipelines to fully exploit upcoming weak-lensing datasets.

**From DES to KiDS: Domain adaptation for cross-survey detection of low-surface-brightness galaxies** · Hareesh Thuruthipilly, Krzysztof Lisiecki, Junais et al. · [arXiv:2605.13842](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13842)
- Why it matters: A concrete deployment of CNN + transformer ensembles for diffuse-object discovery in survey imaging, with explicit framing as a precursor to LSST and Euclid — squarely in the survey-ML / rare-object discovery sweet spot.
- TL;DR: The authors train a three-model ensemble (one CNN, two transformers) on DES cutouts of low-surface-brightness galaxies, then apply domain-adaptation tricks so the same models run on KiDS DR5 imaging without retraining. The pipeline yields 20,180 LSBG and 434 ultra-diffuse-galaxy candidates whose sizes, colours, and environments are characterised against existing DES and HSC samples, and the authors argue the same recipe scales to homogeneous catalogues for LSST and Euclid.

**UNIONS-3500 Weak Lensing: I. A Galaxy Shape Catalogue in the Northern Sky** · F. Hervas-Peters, S. Guerrini, M. Kilbinger et al. · [arXiv:2605.13549](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13549)
- Why it matters: The reference shape catalogue underpinning a new Stage-IV-precursor weak-lensing programme in the north, with detailed PSF and shear-calibration methodology that's directly relevant to my Euclid/Rubin imaging interests.
- TL;DR: This paper documents the construction of the first UNIONS cosmic-shear catalogue from 3,500 deg² of CFHT/MegaCam r-band imaging at 0.7 arcsec median seeing. It covers masking, source and star selection, PSF modelling, shape measurement, and the leakage tests that bound PSF-induced ellipticity contamination, ending with a 62-million-galaxy catalogue at 4.96 arcmin⁻² effective density and σ_ε = 0.27 — the foundation for the three companion cosmological analyses released at the same time.

**A New PSF Deconvolution Algorithm: Simultaneous Spatial Resolution Enhancement and Point Source Removal for Morphological Analysis of AGN Host Galaxies** · Ren Kawase, Takatoshi Shibuya, Kazunori Matsuda · [arXiv:2605.13735](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13735)
- Why it matters: A new PSF-deconvolution / point-source-subtraction method explicitly targeted at upcoming Rubin, Euclid, and Roman wide-field imaging — directly on my image-reconstruction and self-calibration interests.
- TL;DR: The algorithm splits an AGN-hosted galaxy image into a smooth extended-host component and a sparse point-source component, with a new "point-source balance" constraint based on the pixelwise product of the two that prevents over- or under-subtraction of the bright nucleus. Tests on synthetic images and Subaru/HSC observations of z ~ 0–1 AGN show the method recovers host-galaxy morphology at HST-comparable resolution while cleanly removing the central source, opening the door to statistical AGN-host morphology with Rubin, Euclid, and Roman data.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**UNIONS-3500 Weak Lensing: IV. 2D cosmological constraints in harmonic space** · S. Guerrini, L. W. K. Goh, F. Hervas-Peters et al. · [arXiv:2605.13543](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13543)
- TL;DR: A pseudo-Cℓ analysis of the UNIONS cosmic-shear signal yields S₈ = 0.891⁺⁰·⁰⁵⁷₋₀.₀₈₄, consistent with Planck within ~0.8σ and bracketing other cosmic-shear surveys at the 0.87–1.51σ level.

**UNIONS-3500 Weak Lensing: III. 2D Cosmological Constraints in Configuration Space** · L. W. K. Goh, S. Guerrini, C. Daley et al. · [arXiv:2605.13547](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13547)
- TL;DR: The two-point-correlation-function analysis of the same UNIONS catalogue gives S₈ = 0.831⁺⁰·⁰⁶⁷₋₀.₀₇₈, in agreement with Planck and prior shear surveys within 1σ, with detailed coverage of redshift-distribution estimation, shear calibration, and B-mode systematics.

**Comparative analysis of missing data imputation methods for CSST survey: Impact on photometric redshift estimation performance** · Ling Wang, Zhu Chen, Zhijian Luo et al. · [arXiv:2605.13219](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13219)
- TL;DR: Benchmarks ML/DL imputation methods on CSST mock photometry, finding KNN best under MCAR but the attention-based SAITS more robust under realistic mixed missingness, and warning that all of them fail on MNAR cases driven by flux-limit non-detections.

**COOL-LAMPS IX: A Rare Duo of Quasars Each Lensed by a Single Massive Galaxy Cluster** · Erik Solhaug, Michael D. Gladders, Andi M. Kisare et al. · [arXiv:2605.13514](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13514)
- TL;DR: Reports COOLJ1153+0755, the eighth known wide-separation cluster-lensed quasar system and the first in which a single cluster lenses two distinct quasars (a broad-line Type I and a dust-obscured Type II) into four images each, found via non-point-source DECaLS selection that conventional morphology cuts would have missed.

**Probing nonlinear structure formation beyond ΛCDM with the LSS bootstrap: a joint power spectrum and bispectrum analysis** · Giorgia Biselli, Marco Marinucci, Guido D'Amico et al. · [arXiv:2605.13298](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13298)
- TL;DR: First MCMC constraints on symmetry-based deviations from ΛCDM in the linear growth rate and quadratic perturbation-theory kernel, using a joint EFTofLSS one-loop power spectrum + tree-level bispectrum analysis of BOSS DR12 LRGs and the PT Challenge simulation suite.

**The Galaxy Luminosity Functions in ASTRID: Predictions for LSST** · Fatemeh Hafezianzadeh, Tianqing Zhang, Paul Rogozenski et al. · [arXiv:2605.13843](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13843)
- TL;DR: Forward-models galaxy luminosity functions from the ASTRID hydrodynamic simulation with a metal-surface-density-based dust prescription calibrated to SDSS, and delivers an LSST-ready mock catalogue of ~378M galaxies with Schechter fits and number-count predictions for ugrizy from Year 1 to Year 10.

**DEFROST: Detecting Excess in Faraday Rotation thrOugh Sophisticated analysis Techniques** · Valentina Vacca, Sebastian Hutschenreuter, Andrea Cabriolu et al. · [arXiv:2605.13605](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13605)
- TL;DR: Introduces a Bayesian algorithm that simultaneously separates Galactic and extragalactic contributions to rotation-measure catalogues, with synthetic tests showing the extragalactic magnetic-field parameters can be recovered to within ~5σ when restricted to |b| > 45°.

**No evidence for phantom crossing: local goodness-of-fit improvements do not persist under global Bayesian model comparison** · Bikash R. Dinda, Roy Maartens, Shun Saito · [arXiv:2605.13546](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13546)
- TL;DR: Combines DIC and Bayesian evidence to argue that recent w₀wₐ hints of dynamical dark energy and phantom crossing dissolve once the full prior volume is folded in, leaving ΛCDM, w₀wₐ, and thawing quintessence statistically indistinguishable.
