## High priority (≤5)

**Denoising Interferometric Observations Using Visibilities-Informed Neural Networks** · Jason P. Terry, Cassandra Hall, Sergei Gleyzer · [arXiv:2605.14971](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14971v1)
- Why it matters: Physics-informed neural denoising for radio interferometry sits squarely in my self-calibration / survey-imaging ML interest, and the method is pitched as broadly applicable across SKA-era instruments.
- TL;DR: The authors build a U-Net denoiser (VIREO) that ingests the instrument's point-spread function both as an extra input channel and as a term in its loss, training on synthetic protoplanetary-disc interferograms. The PSF-aware model beats classical CLEAN and PSF-agnostic deep baselines on the synthetic benchmark and, when run on archival ALMA data, suppresses background noise while preserving — sometimes sharpening — substructure, suggesting the visibility-informed recipe should generalise to other interferometers.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**Strong Gravitational Lensing with the James Webb Space Telescope** · Adi Zitrin · [arXiv:2605.15189](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15189v1)
- TL;DR: A perspective article surveying how JWST's resolution and depth are already extending strong-lensing science — magnified high-redshift sources, transient detections, and tighter mass models — and previewing what the next few years of cluster and galaxy-scale lensing should look like.

**Beyond AI as Assistants: Toward Autonomous Discovery in Cosmology** · Licong Xu, Thomas Borrett · [arXiv:2605.14791](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14791v1)
- TL;DR: Demonstrates two LLM-driven agent systems for cosmology: CMBEvolve, which uses code-evolution plus tree search to improve weak-lensing-map out-of-distribution detection, and CosmoEvolve, a multi-agent "research lab" that runs an end-to-end ACT DR6 reanalysis and surfaces non-trivial pair- and scale-dependent features.

**KiDS+VIKING-450 cosmology with Bayesian hierarchical model redshift distributions** · George T. Kyriacou, Arrykrishna Mootoovaloo, Alan F. Heavens et al. · [arXiv:2605.15078](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15078v1)
- TL;DR: Reanalyses KiDS+VIKING-450 cosmic-shear data by sampling tomographic redshift distributions from a template-based Bayesian hierarchical model, with PCA-compressed templates; the resulting S₈ rises modestly, easing the Planck tension from 2.3σ to 1.9σ and providing a cleaner photo-z prescription for Stage-IV shear surveys.

**Identifying group galaxies merging with massive clusters using machine learning** · Rhys Jordan, Meghan E. Gray, Alfonso Aragón-Salamanca et al. · [arXiv:2605.14930](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14930v1)
- TL;DR: Supervised classifier trained on mock cluster-infall observations labels galaxies as cluster, group, or field from position, line-of-sight velocity, and stellar mass, reaching 81% accuracy overall and outperforming Friends-of-Friends and Gaussian Mixture baselines — a practical pre-processing tool for upcoming spectroscopic surveys of cluster outskirts.
