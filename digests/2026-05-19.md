## High priority

**Photometric classification of quasars from DES and photo-$z$ estimation with Machine Learning** · Motta, Abdalla, Abdalla et al. · [arXiv:2605.18218](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18218v1)
- Why it matters: ML applied to DES survey imaging for quasar selection and photometric redshifts — sits squarely at the intersection of survey imaging, photo-z, and large-scale structure preparation.
- TL;DR: The authors cross-match DES DR2 photometry against SDSS DR16 spectroscopic labels and train a KNN classifier on PSF magnitudes in griz to separate quasars from stars and galaxies, reaching 0.77 recall at 0.99 precision. A hybrid boosted-tree plus decision-tree regressor estimates redshifts out to z≈4, and a stacked outlier classifier reduces catastrophic failures. The cleaned catalog of ~676k quasars is positioned as a resource for cosmological large-scale-structure work in 0<z<3.

**Self-Supervised Conformal Prediction with Equivariant Bootstrapping for Image Uncertainty Quantification** · Aldridge, Liaudat, Pereyra, McEwen · [arXiv:2605.18655](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18655v1)
- Why it matters: A calibration-data-free uncertainty quantification method demonstrated on weak-lensing mass-mapping — relevant to weak lensing science and to ML imaging pipelines that need trustworthy error bars without simulation priors.
- TL;DR: The method generates heuristic coverage sets via equivariant bootstrapping that exploits image symmetries, then refines them through conformal prediction calibrated in a self-supervised way, sidestepping the need for ground-truth or simulated calibration sets. The authors demonstrate the approach by reconstructing convergence fields from galaxy shear, where simulated calibration data are expensive and can imprint cosmological-model bias. The result is image-level UQ that avoids known failure modes of existing methods on ill-posed inverse problems.

## Medium priority

**Faster CMB lensing with control variates** · Namikawa, Sherwin · [arXiv:2605.18659](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18659v1)
- Differencing realistic masked simulations against analytically tractable isotropic ones cuts the realization-dependent bias compute cost by ~5x for ACT/Simons-Observatory-like noise, with a drop-in path into existing CMB lensing pipelines.

**Primordial power spectrum reconstructions from BOSS + eBOSS** · Martínez-Somonte, Gil-Marín, Marcos-Caballero, Martínez-González · [arXiv:2605.18615](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18615v1)
- A non-parametric knot-based reconstruction of P(k) from BOSS LRG and eBOSS QSO catalogs finds no evidence for primordial features out to k=0.3 h/Mpc and yields ns=0.976±0.021, consistent with Planck.

**Probing Reionization up to the Mark: The Marked Power Spectrum to unveil the HI 21-cm signal from the EoR** · Pandya, Noble, Majumdar et al. · [arXiv:2605.18301](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18301v1)
- Tailored marked-field statistics capture non-Gaussian information in simulated 21-cm EoR signals more cheaply than the bispectrum, tightening Fisher-matrix constraints on reionization parameters.

**Gravitational lensing time delay beyond the Shapiro/geometry split** · Teodori, Blum, Bai · [arXiv:2605.18182](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18182v1)
- Working from exact Schwarzschild–de Sitter null geodesics, the authors show the leading correction to the standard geometry-plus-Shapiro time-delay formula is intrinsic to the Schwarzschild part and introduces no new cosmological dependence at that order.

**Assessing the Impact of Source Confusion for GREX-PLUS based on Deep JWST NIRCam Imaging** · Ono, Inoue, Sugahara et al. · [arXiv:2605.17882](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17882v1)
- Convolving deep JWST NIRCam fields with simulated GREX-PLUS PSF+ghost kernels quantifies how blending from faint unresolved sources erodes effective depth and detection completeness, with implications for planning the GREX-PLUS deep survey.
