## High priority

**ComPACT: Mass-Redshift Properties of the galaxy cluster catalogue** · Voskresenskaia, Lyskova, Zaznobin et al. · [arXiv:2605.20027](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20027v1)
- Why it matters: A CNN applied to combined ACT+Planck microwave maps to detect SZ clusters, with photometric-redshift follow-up using DESI Legacy Imaging — sits at the intersection of CNNs on survey imaging, rare-object catalog building, and photo-z, all high-priority themes.
- TL;DR: The authors validate the ComPACT catalog of 2,962 CNN-detected SZ cluster candidates, confirming roughly 60% as real clusters through DESI-LIS photometric redshifts and literature cross-matches, and recovering SZ masses for 56% of the sample via ACT+Planck scaling relations. Simulated injection tests quantify completeness, and the catalog extends the SZ cluster population at high redshift and high mass, including five newly identified massive systems at z>0.7. The work demonstrates that deep-learning detection on existing CMB maps materially expands SZ-selected cluster samples beyond Planck-derived catalogs.

**A Value-added Physical Properties Catalog for Low-redshift Galaxies from DESI Legacy Imaging Surveys DR10** · Wei, Li, Zhang et al. · [arXiv:2605.19520](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19520v1)
- Why it matters: Multimodal deep learning (ResNet CNN on imaging + MLP on photometric features) producing a homogeneous catalog for ~547M galaxies from a major imaging survey — a direct hit on the multimodal-foundation-models / ViTs-and-CNNs-on-survey-imaging interests.
- TL;DR: The authors train a dual-branch model that fuses ResNet-based image features from DESI Legacy DR10 multi-band cutouts with a photometric-catalog MLP, supervised on MPA-JHU DR8 spectroscopic measurements of SFR, stellar mass, and gas-phase metallicity. Inference across the LS DR10 footprint yields a catalog of physical properties for ~547M low-redshift galaxies (z≤0.5), validated against independent catalogs and scaling relations. The authors position the catalog as a population-statistics resource rather than a per-object precision tool, aimed at supporting ensemble galaxy-evolution studies and cross-comparisons with upcoming spectroscopic surveys.

## Medium priority

**Reassessing the Statistical Necessity of Stellar Velocity Anisotropy in Strong-Lensing Cosmology with Lens-by-Lens Photometric Constraints** · Hu, Liu, Hu et al. · [arXiv:2605.19615](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19615v1)
- Cross-matching 130 SGL systems with Pantheon+ SNe Ia and folding in lens-by-lens luminosity-density slopes from high-resolution imaging, the authors show the orbital anisotropy parameter is statistically required as free in SGL cosmology and recover a mild negative redshift evolution of the lens density slope.

**Prospect of Measuring the Cosmic Dipole by Strongly Lensed Gravitational Waves Associated with Galaxy Surveys** · Chen, Zhang · [arXiv:2605.19476](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19476v1)
- Forecasts that strongly lensed GW events combined with galaxy-survey redshifts can deliver an independent cosmic-dipole measurement at the 10⁻³ level after ten years of Einstein Telescope and Cosmic Explorer operation, jointly with background cosmology.

**CMB lensing imprints of cosmic voids in DESI Legacy Survey DR9 LRGs with photometric redshift calibration** · Sartori · [arXiv:2605.19763](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19763v1)
- Stacks Planck 2018 CMB lensing against 3D voids identified in DESI-LIS DR9 LRGs, with Buzzard mocks tuned to the data's sparsity and photo-z error model, and finds full ΛCDM consistency (A_κ=1.016±0.054) at ~17σ in both void-in-void and void-in-cloud regimes.

**Correlation between baryonic process and galaxy assembly bias** · Xiao, Hua, Zhai et al. · [arXiv:2605.19323](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19323v1)
- Using the Galacticus SAM on UNIT to vary cooling, star-formation, and feedback prescriptions, and a Random Forest importance analysis, the authors identify gas cooling and stellar feedback as the dominant drivers of stellar-mass-selected assembly bias, with star formation taking over for SFR-selected samples.
