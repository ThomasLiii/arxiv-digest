## High priority

**Euclid preparation: Testing multi-field inflation with galaxy power spectrum and bispectrum** · Euclid Collaboration, Linde, Moradinezhad Dizgah et al. · [arXiv:2605.21436](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21436v1)
- Why it matters: A Euclid-collaboration preparation paper validating a joint power-spectrum + bispectrum pipeline against Euclid-like mocks — a direct hit on Euclid mission methods and on large-scale-structure / clustering methodology.
- TL;DR: The authors test a one-loop $P_\ell$ + tree-level $B_\ell$ redshift-space pipeline on Abacus-PNG mocks with HOD tuned to Euclid Flagship 2, exploring PNG-bias parametrisations, priors, and scale cuts under Gaussian and local-PNG initial conditions. They demonstrate that the bispectrum alone reduces $\sigma(f_\mathrm{NL})$ by ~29–46% relative to $P_\ell$, with a further ~8–13% improvement when combined, and propose a physically motivated $b_\phi$ prior that yields unbiased $f_\mathrm{NL}$ recovery. The strongest forecast sensitivity (2.35σ on $f_\mathrm{NL}$ at $z=1.7$) sets up Euclid's spectroscopic sample as a competitive multi-field inflation probe pending end-to-end systematics validation.

**Detection of a dark matter subhalo in the strongly lensed system PJ011646** · Amvrosiadis, Nightingale, He et al. · [arXiv:2605.21212](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21212v1)
- Why it matters: A high-significance strong-lensing detection of a dark-matter substructure perturber — squarely in the high-priority strong-lensing detection theme, with a sensitivity floor that lets ALMA imaging discriminate cold vs warm dark matter.
- TL;DR: The authors model PJ011646 with an elliptical power-law plus $m=3,4$ multipoles and external shear, fitted to ~0.1″ ALMA dust-continuum data, then perform a grid-based NFW subhalo search in the image plane. They detect a $M_{200} \approx 2.8 \times 10^{10} M_\odot$, $c_{200}\approx 30$ subhalo at 5.8σ, with the projected mass within 2 kpc tightly pinned at $3.6 \times 10^9 M_\odot$. Non-detection cells set a 3σ sensitivity floor near $M_{200} \approx 8 \times 10^8 M_\odot$ along the arcs, demonstrating that sub-arcsecond ALMA continuum lensing probes the substructure mass range where CDM and WDM diverge.

**Robustness Analysis of USmorph: II. Optimizing Feature Extraction, Dimensionality Reduction, and Clustering for Unsupervised Galaxy Morphology Classification** · Fang, Yin, Zheng et al. · [arXiv:2605.20871](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20871v1)
- Why it matters: A direct combination of CNN feature extraction + UMAP + clustering on survey galaxy imaging for unsupervised morphology discovery, intersecting three high/medium-priority themes (CNNs on imaging, UMAP/representation learning, morphology as a precursor to anomaly detection).
- TL;DR: The authors sweep five ImageNet-pretrained backbones for galaxy-image feature extraction, finding AlexNet most discriminative, and pair it with UMAP for dimensionality reduction on stability/efficiency grounds. They introduce a Bagging-based multi-cluster voting scheme over K-means, Birch, and Agglomerative clustering at $K=16$ that improves label consistency and cluster purity, with t-SNE projections showing compact, well-separated morphology groups. The resulting unsupervised classifications align with galaxy-evolution expectations, positioning USmorph as a deployable pipeline for upcoming surveys such as CSST.

**Euclid: Early Release Observations -- The formation of peanutty dwarf galaxies in Perseus** · Kapoor, Smith, Zuiderwijk et al. · [arXiv:2605.21342](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21342v1)
- Why it matters: A Euclid-collaboration ERO science paper that develops a low-surface-brightness-tailored isophote measurement method on Euclid imaging and benchmarks against $N$-body mocks — both Euclid mission science and a transferable low-SB measurement technique.
- TL;DR: The authors introduce a cumulative light fraction estimator for the $c_4$ isophotal shape parameter robust in the low-surface-brightness regime, applied to ~190 cleanly selected early-type dwarfs from a Euclid ERO catalog of ~1100 Perseus dwarfs. They identify 13 significantly boxy ($c_4 < -0.0175$) systems sitting on the cluster red sequence with no preferred spatial distribution, and find an anticorrelation between $c_4$ and effective radius that is reproduced in mock-Euclid observations of $N$-body tidally-stripped progenitors. They interpret the population as orientation-selected, tidally transformed dwarfs whose boxy appearances trace inner box-orbit-dominated triaxial peanut structures seen face-on.

## Medium priority

**Velocityformer: Broken-Symmetry-Matched Equivariant Graph Transformers for Cosmological Velocity Reconstruction** · Tröster, Mirkovic, Oehl, Thomsen · [arXiv:2605.21483](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21483v1)
- An equivariant graph transformer whose inductive bias is matched to the line-of-sight broken symmetry of spectroscopic surveys improves galaxy-velocity reconstruction correlation by ~35% over the linear baseline, generalising zero-shot across cosmologies and yielding the same SNR gain on simulated kSZ measurements.

**SpectralEarth-FM: Bringing Hyperspectral Imagery into Multimodal Earth Observation Pretraining** · Ait Ali Braham, Banze, Albrecht et al. · [arXiv:2605.21075](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21075v1)
- A hierarchical transformer with sensor-specific encoders and cross-sensor fusion jointly pretrained on ~25M co-located hyperspectral (EnMAP, EMIT, DESIS), Sentinel-2/Landsat optical, LST, and Sentinel-1 SAR patches with a JEPA objective — a close methodological analog to AION-style multimodal foundation models for imaging surveys.

**OlmoEarth v1.1: A more efficient family of OlmoEarth models** · Tseng, Zhang, Bastani et al. · [arXiv:2605.20804](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20804v1)
- An efficiency update to the OlmoEarth Earth-observation foundation models that cuts training GPU hours by 1.7× and Sentinel-2 inference MACs by 2.9× while preserving downstream performance, with open training code — a useful efficiency reference for survey-imaging foundation-model builders.
