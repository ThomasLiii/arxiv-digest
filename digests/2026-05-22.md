## High priority

**Self-Supervised ConvLSTM for Fermi Large Area Telescope Transient Detection** · Garinei, Speziali, Vispa et al. · [arXiv:2605.22112](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22112v1)
- Why it matters: A CNN-based model (ConvLSTM) applied directly to all-sky survey imaging in a self-supervised regime for transient and anomaly detection — sitting at the intersection of CNNs-on-survey-imaging (high) and survey anomaly detection (medium) priorities.
- TL;DR: The authors generate ten years of synthetic Fermi-LAT observations with gtobssim, format them as daily all-sky counts/exposure maps, and train a ConvLSTM to reconstruct the expected nominal gamma-ray sky while preserving spatial locality. Transient excesses are flagged via per-pixel mean-squared residual maps with statistically motivated thresholds and a local-coherence filter to suppress isolated noise, and the trained model is then deployed as a predictor on real Fermi-LAT daily maps. The pipeline surfaces variable sources, flares, and GRB-like events and provides a controlled benchmark for evaluating anomaly-detection strategies on long-duration survey imaging.

## Medium priority

**Hostless extragalactic transients in Fink: Results from the ELEPHANT pipeline** · Durgesh, Pessi, Ishida, Peloton · [arXiv:2605.22407](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22407v1)
- A stamp-based real-time hostless-transient filter inside the Fink broker, evaluated on 877 candidates flagged between Sept 2023 and Dec 2025, recovers 67 confirmed hostless events with 84% pipeline accuracy and is now operating on the Rubin alert stream — a direct anomaly-detection deployment on next-generation survey data.

**Machine Learning applications to Galaxy Clusters** · Yepes, de Andrés · [arXiv:2605.21991](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21991v1)
- Review chapter on AI methods for cluster cosmology covering mass estimation from SZ, X-ray, optical, and dynamical tracers, baryonic-physics emulation, dynamical-state classification, and intracluster-light analysis, with emphasis on training-simulation requirements and uncertainty quantification for next-generation surveys.

**Machine Learning Techniques for Astrophysics and Cosmology: Lyman-α forest** · Chaves-Montero · [arXiv:2605.22489](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22489v1)
- Review of ML for Lyman-α forest analyses — absorption-system characterization, quasar continuum reconstruction, hydro-simulation emulation, simulation-based and field-level inference, and 3D matter-density reconstruction — positioning ML-driven pipelines as core infrastructure for upcoming spectroscopic surveys.

**Towards precision cosmology with Void x CMB correlations (II): Impact of mock catalogs on the Void x CMB lensing signal** · Pérez Sar, Hernández Monteagudo, Kovács, Pisani · [arXiv:2605.22630](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22630v1)
- Using validated Roman mocks, the authors forecast Void x CMB lensing S/N of ~13σ (Planck) up to ~31σ (CMB-S4) for 2D rescaled voids, and show the signal is far less sensitive to mock-catalog choices than galaxy or void statistics, indicating that future Roman-driven tensions would not be artifacts of mock construction.

**The impact of evolving dark energy on the Weyl potential measured from the Dark Energy Survey Year 3 data** · Rosatello, Ye, Berti et al. · [arXiv:2605.22599](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22599v1)
- Replacing ΛCDM with a w0waCDM background (DESI-preferred phantom-crossing dark energy) reduces the DES Y3 Weyl-potential tension with GR to 1.6–2.2σ, with the relief driven by the modified background rather than enlarged uncertainties.

**Spectra as Language: Large Language Models for Scalable Stellar Parameter and Abundance Inference** · Lu, Li, Li, Wang · [arXiv:2605.22162](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22162v1)
- A two-stage LLM framework treats stellar spectra as tokenized continuous sequences and infers Teff, log g, [M/H], and ~20 chemical abundances; scaling-law analyses show systematic gains with data, foreshadowing foundation-model-style pipelines for spectroscopic surveys.

**Signatures of Modified Gravity Below 𝒪(10) Mpc in a Dynamical Dark Energy Background** · Toda, Gómez-Valent · [arXiv:2605.22309](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22309v1)
- A combined Planck PR4 + DESI DR2 + Pantheon+/DES-Dovekie + RSD analysis with binned effective-Newton-constant deviations on top of a CPL background finds that any departures from GR needed to suppress low-z structure growth must act on comoving scales below ~10 Mpc.

**Blue-tilted Runnings and the JWST Early Galaxy Tension** · Kobayashi, Chiaki, Kimura et al. · [arXiv:2605.22161](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22161v1)
- A blue-tilted primordial spectrum with positive running αs ≈ 0.2 and βs ≈ 0.2 can reconcile JWST's massive z ≈ 6.5–9 galaxy abundance with CMB constraints at the 1σ level while remaining consistent with PBH formation on much smaller scales.
