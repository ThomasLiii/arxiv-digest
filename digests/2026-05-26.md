## Medium priority

**A Multimodal 3D Foundation Model for Light Sheet Fluorescence Microscopy Enables Few-Shot Segmentation, Classification, and Deblurring** · Scheinfeld, Zhang, Mu et al. · [arXiv:2605.26026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26026v1)
- Why it matters: It's a multimodal image–text 3D foundation model trained by jointly optimizing masked reconstruction and image–text alignment — the same recipe behind astronomy foundation models like AION-1 — so the architecture and few-shot transfer results map onto survey-imaging and deblending workflows despite the microscopy domain.
- TL;DR: A backbone pretrained on diverse unlabeled 3D microscopy volumes learns transferable volumetric representations that beat baselines on few-shot segmentation, classification, and deblurring under both standard metrics and expert review.
