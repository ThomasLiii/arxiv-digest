## Medium priority

**Constraints on Dynamical Dark Energy from Multiple Probes in the Full Dark Energy Survey** · DES Collaboration, Abbott, Adamow et al. · [arXiv:2605.27221](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27221v1)
- Why it matters: A flagship multi-probe result from the complete six-year Dark Energy Survey that combines weak gravitational lensing and galaxy clustering (3×2pt) with SNe and BAO — squarely in the large-scale-structure and weak-lensing cosmology theme.
- TL;DR: The full-DES growth-plus-geometry combination gives $w_0\approx-0.84$, $w_a\approx-0.44$ (the tightest single-survey dark-energy constraint to date), with a 2.2–3.0σ preference for evolving dark energy that strengthens to 3.0σ once DESI DR2 BAO and primary CMB are added.

**Cosmological Constraints from Bias-Robust Wavelet Scattering Statistics for Stage-IV Galaxy Surveys** · Jiang, Xiao, Yin et al. · [arXiv:2605.27087](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27087v1)
- Why it matters: A bias-robust non-Gaussian clustering statistic paired with simulation-based inference for Stage-IV galaxy surveys — a clustering/LSS methodology hit that is also ML-adjacent via its Gaussian-process emulator.
- TL;DR: Their $R^{\rm wst}$ statistic, built from $m$-mode ratios of the wavelet scattering transform and emulated with GP regression on $N$-body suites, tightens the $\Omega_m$–$\sigma_8$ degeneracy by roughly 2× over the two-point correlation function while staying stable across tracer-bias scenarios without explicit bias modeling.
