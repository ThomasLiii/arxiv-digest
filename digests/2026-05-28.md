## Medium priority

**Fewer simulations, sharper covariances: Reducing mock covariance noise with Zeldovich approximation control variates** · Hadzhiyska, White · [arXiv:2605.28817](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28817v1)
- Why it matters: A variance-reduction technique for power-spectrum covariance estimation that the authors show is directly deployable in DESI/Euclid/LSST/SPHEREx pipelines — core large-scale-structure clustering methodology.
- TL;DR: Pairing each expensive mock with a cheap Zeldovich realization sharing the same initial conditions cancels correlated sample variance, cutting covariance-matrix noise by roughly an order of magnitude on large scales and accelerating convergence 2–3× elsewhere.

**Exploring non-Poisson satellite occupation in HOD models and its impact on 2- and 3-point galaxy clustering** · Rocher · [arXiv:2605.28644](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28644v1)
- Why it matters: Refines the galaxy–halo connection that underpins clustering analyses — squarely in the large-scale-structure / clustering theme.
- TL;DR: A one-parameter Conway–Maxwell–Poisson extension to satellite occupation shifts small-scale clustering by up to ~10–30% (especially counts-in-cylinders) while leaving large-scale power-spectrum and bispectrum cosmology constraints largely intact.

**Dark Quest II: A Wide-Coverage Neural Network Emulator of the Nonlinear Matter Power Spectrum Across Extended Cosmologies** · Tanaka, Nishimichi, Kobayashi · [arXiv:2605.28596](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28596v1)
- Why it matters: A neural-network emulator for nonlinear clustering across a nine-parameter w₀wₐνCDM space — an ML-for-cosmology tool that feeds weak-lensing and clustering inference.
- TL;DR: DarkEmulator2 reaches subpercent accuracy out to the particle Nyquist scale by augmenting the cosmological inputs with the linear spectrum, resolution descriptors, and a low-dimensional initial-field summary, and by training a single network jointly across three simulation-resolution tiers.

**Unsupervised Morphological Characterization of Gravitational-Wave Glitches in LIGO O4a Using Frozen DINOv2 Features** · Cirfeta · [arXiv:2605.28572](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28572v1)
- Why it matters: A frozen DINOv2 Vision Transformer paired with UMAP and unsupervised clustering for label-free anomaly/novelty search on spectrogram imagery — directly aligned with foundation-model embeddings and representation learning for discovery.
- TL;DR: Zero-shot 384-dimensional DINOv2 embeddings of 188k+ Q-transform spectrograms, clustered via a Dirichlet-process mixture, find no morphologically novel O4a glitches (all map to known Gravity Spy classes), establishing a reproducible baseline for label-free glitch characterization.
