## High priority

**Generative Diffusion Priors for 3D Mapping of the Dark Universe** · Zhao, Scognamiglio, Doré et al. · [arXiv:2606.00803](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00803)
- Why it matters: Combines weak-lensing dark-matter reconstruction (a core cosmology problem) with a learned diffusion prior trained on simulations — sits at the intersection of weak lensing and ML/foundation-model methods.
- TL;DR: The authors build Conicus3D, a dataset of high-resolution cosmological simulations, and train a score-based diffusion model that captures the full non-Gaussian, filamentary 3D matter distribution as a generative prior. They plug this prior into a posterior-sampling scheme for the weak-lensing inverse problem, where only a single line-of-sight is observed through noisy galaxy shapes with uncertain redshifts. The resulting 3D maps recover cosmic-web structure more faithfully than handcrafted priors and provide calibrated Bayesian uncertainties.

**Euclid: Early Release Observations — Internal kinematics and the convective-transition gap of NGC 6397** · Griggio et al. · [arXiv:2606.02703](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02703)
- Why it matters: Introduces a Euclid-specific multiple-pass data-reduction tool (adapted from HST pipelines) that improves astrometry and photometry in crowded fields — directly relevant to Euclid methods and self-calibration/mosaicking of survey imaging.
- TL;DR: The team ports an HST-style multi-pass PSF-fitting pipeline to Euclid Early Release Observations of the globular cluster NGC 6397, combining it with archival HST imaging over a ~20-year baseline to derive high-precision proper motions, energy equipartition, anisotropy, mass segregation, and binary fraction profiles. They also detect, at >5σ, a subtle under-density in the cluster's color-magnitude diagram around 0.35 M_⊙, consistent with the Gaia M-dwarf convective-transition gap — its first detection in a globular cluster.

**Calibrating Self-Weighted Shear Estimation via Field Distortion** · Liu, Sun, Zhang et al. · [arXiv:2606.00553](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00553)
- Why it matters: Methodology for cosmic-shear measurement and bias calibration on real survey data — the kind of weak-lensing systematics work that underpins Euclid/CSST/LSST cosmology.
- TL;DR: The authors introduce SWSE, a self-weighting variant of the Fourier_Quad shear estimator that avoids the iterative cost of PDF-SYM by reweighting bright and faint galaxies to suppress shape noise, at the price of an inherent multiplicative bias. They then study whether the field-distortion test — which uses the known shear gradient across the focal plane — can recover and correct this bias from real data without simulation calibration, characterizing residual systematics as a function of PSF, noise, and galaxy population.

**COSMOS2025: Machine Learning Classification of Early- and Late-type Galaxies at 0 < z < 3** · Asadi, Sheikhi · [arXiv:2606.03224](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.03224)
- Why it matters: ML morphological classification on COSMOS2025 photometry without imaging-based labels — a low-cost alternative to CNN/foundation-model approaches that is directly applicable to wide-field surveys like Euclid.
- TL;DR: The authors train a CatBoost classifier on 66 broadband colors using mock galaxies from the Santa Cruz Semi-Analytic Model, with labels defined by bulge-to-total mass ratio and specific star-formation rate, and inject COSMOS2025-calibrated photometric noise to bridge the sim-to-real gap. Applied to 44k COSMOS2025 galaxies, the model recovers a sharp early/late bimodality (only ~6% of galaxies are ambiguous), matching the simulated distribution, and reaches ~70% agreement with bulge+disk decomposition validations. This suggests broadband colors carry most of the morphological signal without needing image cutouts or structural fits.

## Medium priority

**21cmEMUv3: a hybrid diffusion-LSTM emulator of 21cmFAST summary observables** · Breitman, Mesinger, Murray et al. · [arXiv:2606.00219](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00219)
- A score-based diffusion emulator for the cylindrical 21cm power spectrum plus LSTM emulators for six other reionization summary observables, conditioned on σ8 and ten astrophysical parameters.

**Euclid: Disky titans — surprisingly high star formation efficiency in two brightest group galaxies at z∼0.75** · Gentile et al. · [arXiv:2606.02687](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02687)
- Euclid first-data-release discovery of two massive star-forming disk galaxies at the centers of dense groups, with NOEMA follow-up showing large cold-gas reservoirs that defy standard quenching expectations.

**Decaying Dark Matter as a Possible Solution for Cosmological Tensions** · Acharya, Johnson · [arXiv:2606.00529](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00529)
- A neutrinophilic decaying-dark-matter model is propagated through DES Y1 redshift distributions and shown to produce scale-dependent clustering suppression that eases the S_8 weak-lensing tension while remaining consistent with shear and galaxy-shear angular spectra.

**CSST large-scale structure analysis pipeline IV: Cosmic Voids Identified from Galaxy Group Samples** · Song, Yang, Gong et al. · [arXiv:2606.01907](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.01907)
- Builds group-based void catalogs for the CSST mock survey and shows that void size functions and density profiles measured from groups (rather than individual galaxies) cleanly trace the halo-defined truth out to z=1, giving a more theoretically tractable LSS probe.

**A Cosmic Archipelago of lensed metal-poor galaxies at z∼6** · Bolamperti, Messa, Zanella et al. · [arXiv:2606.02720](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02720)
- JWST/NIRCam + VLT/X-Shooter + NIRSpec-IFU analysis of an ensemble of strongly lensed galaxies behind MACSJ0416, including a UV-faint, compact, metal-poor source with very high Lyα and LyC escape fractions relevant to reionization.

**Neutrino Fingerprints: Image-Based Encodings of IceCube Events for CNN Direction Reconstruction** · Tori, Verbeken, Ginis · [arXiv:2606.02788](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02788)
- Encodes sparse IceCube pulse data as dense 72×72×3 "fingerprint" images and trains a ResNet18 to reconstruct neutrino direction, reaching 1.10 rad mean angular error — a clean demonstration of CNNs on a non-survey but image-shaped detector readout.

**Bridging the UV Gap: HST Ultraviolet Foundation for Star Formation Science in the Era of Roman, Euclid, and HWO** · Majidi, Bayo, Biazzo et al. · [arXiv:2606.02746](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02746)
- White paper arguing that continued HST STIS/COS UV spectroscopy is required to complement the IR/optical surveys of Roman, Euclid, JWST and to pave the way for HWO, framing how UV diagnostics tie into upcoming Euclid-era science.

**Optimal Transport Reconstruction of Biased Tracers in Primordial Non-Gaussian Fields** · Nikakhtar, Sheth, Padmanabhan et al. · [arXiv:2606.01529](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.01529)
- Extends optimal-transport reconstruction of initial conditions from biased late-time tracers to non-Gaussian primordial displacement fields, a useful LSS analysis tool for surveys aiming to constrain primordial non-Gaussianity.
