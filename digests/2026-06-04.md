## High priority

**Identifying Gems from Roman RAPIDly** · Gandhi, Mahabal, Jencson et al. · [arXiv:2606.05103](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05103)
- Why it matters: An ML real/bogus classifier for the Roman Space Telescope's RAPID alert pipeline — directly in the "ML applied to next-generation survey imaging" sweet spot, alongside Euclid/SPHEREx methods work.
- TL;DR: With no real Roman data yet available, the authors train three variants of a real/bogus classifier ($RuBR_{comb}$, $RuBR_{loc}$, $RuBR_{DA}$) that combine locally injected transients with the OpenUniverse2024 simulated detections, using domain adaptation in one variant to bridge the synthetic-to-real gap. The framework is designed to drop into the RAPID pipeline so Roman can begin issuing trustworthy transient alerts as soon as it starts observing, and the authors discuss strategies for retraining $RuBR$ once on-sky data exists.

## Medium priority

**Full Nonlinear Velocity Reconstruction With Transformer and Ensemble Tree Machine Learning** · Gong, Bean · [arXiv:2606.05047](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05047)
- Trains a Transformer and a gradient-boosted tree to predict the residual between true galaxy peculiar velocities and the linear-theory estimate on AbacusSummit mocks matched to DESI LRG/ELG samples, including Rubin LSST photo-z uncertainties.

**Cosmic variance or galaxy bias? Disentangling finite-volume and galaxy formation effects in cosmological analysis** · Sinigaglia, Kitaura · [arXiv:2606.04830](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04830)
- Reformulates cosmic variance using Eulerian halo/galaxy bias to separate finite-volume noise from galaxy-formation-induced shifts of the BAO scale, validated against variance-suppressed N-body sims for DESI/Euclid-style analyses.

**Posterior sampling in the Age of Emulators** · Nygaard, Janken, Hannestad, Tram · [arXiv:2606.04895](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04895)
- Benchmarks MH, MALA, HMC, NUTS, and ensemble sampling against differentiable neural-network likelihood emulators (built with CLiENT) for ΛCDM and sterile-neutrino models, and releases the TensorFlow `BEST` package.

**On Out-of-sample Embedding in UMAP** · Islam, Fleischer · [arXiv:2606.04451](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04451)
- Diagnoses the "repulsion effect" that pushes new points to cluster peripheries in standard UMAP and proposes an in-graph optimization plus parameterized UMAP variant that places out-of-sample points correctly — relevant to embedding-based survey workflows.

**Steep Redshift Evolution of the Ionizing Escape Fraction at $z=5$–$12$: Empirical Constraints and Comparison with Simulations** · Wang, Shan · [arXiv:2606.04827](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04827)
- Constrains a three-parameter power-law model of $f_{\rm esc}(M_h,z)$ using HST+JWST UV luminosity functions, the Planck optical depth, and seven neutral-fraction measurements, finding $\langle f_{\rm esc}\rangle$ rising from ~2% at $z=5$ to ~9% at $z=12$.

**A Measurement of the Thermal and Ionization State of the IGM at $z<0.5$** · Hu, Khaire, Hennawi, Tripp et al. · [arXiv:2606.05006](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05006)
- Applies an ML-based inference of the joint $b$–$N_{\rm HI}$ Lyα distribution to 82 COS quasar spectra and finds the low-$z$ IGM is ~7σ hotter and the photoionization rate ~4σ lower than UV-background models predict.

**Cosmography of the Sloan Basin of Attraction and Neighborhood** · Pomarede, Tully, Valade, Libeskind et al. · [arXiv:2606.04578](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04578)
- Uses Hamiltonian Monte Carlo forward reconstruction of the Cosmicflows-4 velocity field to delineate the Sloan Great Wall's basin of attraction (~0.13c across), with probabilistic density and V-web filament maps in a ΛCDM framework.
