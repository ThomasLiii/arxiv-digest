## High priority

**Semi-supervised Source Detection in Astronomical Images: New Benchmark and Strong Baseline** · Feng, Cao, Luo, Guo et al. · [arXiv:2606.09219](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09219)
- Why it matters: A new benchmark plus semi-supervised detector for source detection in survey imagery — squarely in the "ML on astronomical survey images" track and a likely reference for any catalog-building or lensing-candidate pipeline.
- TL;DR: The authors release LAMOST-DET, a benchmark of 18,400 astronomical images with 728,898 source annotations, alongside Nova Teacher, a dual-teacher semi-supervised framework that augments source light, generates confidence-gated pseudo-labels, and mines cross-view complementary cues for sparse annotations. Nova Teacher beats prior semi-supervised detectors by 4–5 mAP points on LAMOST-DET, generalises to natural-image data, and the code is open-sourced — providing a ready building block for handling dense, faint, PSF-blurred sources in surveys.

## Medium priority

**Characterizing Stellar Streams with Error-Aware Machine Learning** · Pratsos, Dey, Li · [arXiv:2606.09576](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09576)
- Weakly-supervised stream finder (SCREAM) that adapts the particle-physics CATHODE method, uniquely propagates observational uncertainties into the training loss, and is validated on Gaia DR3 + DESI Legacy imaging — relevant as an uncertainty-aware anomaly/over-density discovery template for survey embeddings.

**A Meta-Learning Framework for Multitask Reverberation Mapping in Active Galactic Nuclei** · Raju, Kovačević, Ilić, Tombesi, Popović et al. · [arXiv:2606.09665](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09665)
- Uses Self-Organizing Maps plus Attentive Latent Neural Processes with Mixture Density heads to jointly learn AGN light-curve structure, SMBH parameters, and transfer functions for LSST-scale photometric reverberation mapping, demonstrating sim-to-real transfer onto ZTF data — a representation-learning recipe for time-domain survey embeddings.

**Towards a consistent framework of determining active galactic nucleus contribution fraction and host galaxy properties** · Liu, Yang, et al. · [arXiv:2606.09413](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09413)
- Combines two SED-fitting codes (CIGALE, GRAHSP) with a deep-learning imaging decomposition on JWST/NIRCam F150W COSMOS-Web data to break AGN/host degeneracies, showing that morphological information from imaging directly improves photometric inference.
