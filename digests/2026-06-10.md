## High priority (≤5)

**High-Resolution ALMA Imaging for a Gravitationally-lensed Quasar at z=6.5: Constraining the AGN Contribution to Galactic-Scale Dust Heating** · Minghao Yue, Xiaohui Fan, Anna-Christina Eilers et al. · [arXiv:2606.11084](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11084v1)
- Why it matters: Pixelated strong-lens modeling on visibility data for a z=6.5 lensed quasar — a concrete application of the strong-lensing science you track, with a published lens model others can reuse.
- TL;DR: Sub-arcsecond ALMA imaging of J0439+1634 is fit with a pixelated singular-isothermal-ellipsoid plus shear model, yielding a naked-cusp configuration and ~4.6× magnification. The reconstructed source has a sub-200 pc bright core and a radiative-transfer fit decomposes dust heating into an inner AGN-dominated core (within ~100 pc, contributing ~13% of the sub-mm flux) and an outer star-formation-heated envelope, implying prior FIR-based SFRs for high-z quasars are mildly overestimated.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**Balancing bias, baryons, and scale cuts in LSST 3x2pt analysis** · Ottavia Truttero, Maria Tsedrik, Joe Zuntz et al. · [arXiv:2606.10679](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10679v1)
- Why it matters: Stage-IV cosmology (LSST DESC) tradeoff study on galaxy-bias and baryon modeling for weak-lensing-plus-clustering, with an open-source pipeline.
- TL;DR: Uses BACCO emulators (HEFT for nonlinear bias, baryonification for feedback) to find scale cuts that keep LSST Y1/Y10 3×2pt constraints unbiased, finds a minimal-bias variant remains unbiased to k=0.7 h/Mpc in ΛCDM, and shows neutrino-mass inferences are sensitive to the chosen bias model.

**Synergy between the gravitational potential decay rate and other structure growth probes in testing gravity** · Shang Li, Pengjie Zhang, Fuyu Dong · [arXiv:2606.10597](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10597v1)
- Why it matters: Combines a newer LSS observable (DR) with CMB-lensing Σ8 and DESI fσ8 — relevant to multi-probe cosmology and modified-gravity tests.
- TL;DR: Joint analysis of tomographic decay-rate measurements with CMB-lensing and DESI DR1 full-shape and peculiar-velocity data tightens modified-gravity parameter constraints by ~1.5–2× over Σ8+fσ8 alone in both phenomenological and EFT α-basis parameterizations.

**Optical-morphology-based assessment of astrometric quality in Gaia-CRF3 quasars** · Qiqi Wu, Shilong Liao, Zhaoxiang Qi et al. · [arXiv:2606.10655](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10655v1)
- Why it matters: Multi-survey ML fusion (XGBoost on DESI/SDSS/SkyMapper/PS1 morphology) to characterize a survey systematic — touches survey-imaging ML and Gaia-CRF reliability.
- TL;DR: A composite point-source score derived by fusing four survey-based morphology classifiers covers 99.6% of Gaia-CRF3 and reveals that extended-morphology AGNs introduce a parallax zero-point bias near −43.7 μas and inflate the all-sky proper-motion frame spin by ~16%.

**On-sky demonstration of reinforcement learning for adaptive optics control** · Jalo Nousiainen, Vincent Chambouleyron, Benoit Neichel et al. · [arXiv:2606.10771](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10771v1)
- Why it matters: First on-sky validation of an RL adaptive-optics controller — relevant for ML-driven image quality in survey/imaging instrumentation.
- TL;DR: The PO4AO reinforcement-learning controller, deployed on the Papyrus AO system at OHP, beat a standard integrator across nights and seeing conditions with a single hyperparameter set, learning to suppress vibrations and remaining robust to noise despite Python-induced latency.
