## Medium priority (≤8)

**KiDS-Legacy: Joint analysis of second- and third-order cosmic shear** · L. Linke, L. Porth, P. Burger et al. · [arXiv:2606.12389](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12389v1)
- Why it matters: Final-release KiDS weak-lensing analysis that combines COSEBIs with third-order aperture-mass moments — a concrete demonstration that 3-pt cosmic shear is now mature enough for Stage-IV surveys.
- TL;DR: Combining 2- and 3-pt statistics on KiDS-Legacy more than doubles the figure of merit in the Ω_m–S_8 plane relative to 2-pt alone, gives S_8 = 0.806 consistent with Planck within 1σ, and finds no S_8 tension.

**Towards Practical Field-Level Inference for Weak Lensing** · Yuuki Omori, Justine Zeghal, Chihway Chang · [arXiv:2606.12255](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12255v1)
- Why it matters: Apples-to-apples comparison of field-level inference vs. power-spectrum inference using the same forward model — informs how much information upcoming lensing surveys leave on the table by sticking with 2-pt.
- TL;DR: Using 8M-parameter LPT+particle-mesh forward models, both implicit and explicit FLI return consistent posteriors and outperform 2-pt analyses, particularly when small scales are pushed into the PM regime.

**Bounding the Effect of HOD Assumptions on Small-Scale Clustering Constraints** · Nick Magnelli, Zachery Brown, Lado Samushia · [arXiv:2606.12405](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12405v1)
- Why it matters: Quantifies how much small-scale clustering cosmology depends on galaxy–halo connection priors — directly affects how to interpret DESI-era LRG constraints.
- TL;DR: Using AbacusSummit LRG mocks, fixing HOD parameters rules out ~81% of the tested cosmologies at 3σ while a fully marginalized "floor" treatment rules out only 25%, showing HOD priors carry most of the constraining power on small scales.

**A Unified Halo Mass Function Across Dark Matter Models from High-Resolution Multi-Scale Simulations** · Andrew J. Benson, Ethan O. Nadler, Xiaolong Du et al. · [arXiv:2606.12137](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12137v1)
- Why it matters: Calibrated HMF spanning ten orders of magnitude in halo mass and many DM scenarios — a reusable ingredient for substructure/lensing forward models.
- TL;DR: A unified fitting function calibrated against MultiDark plus Group/MW/LMC zoom-ins (CDM and non-CDM) reaches ~12% precision down to 10^7 M_sun, captures small-scale cut-offs and oscillations, and includes a working environmental-density model.

**Recalibration of SDSS photometric zero-points based on the InfraRed Flux Method temperature scale** · Zenghua Zhou, Luca Casagrande, Heran Xiong · [arXiv:2606.11587](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11587v1)
- Why it matters: Revised SDSS zero-points anchored via IRFM across ~6000 stars — a methodology relevant to broadband photometric calibration for survey imaging.
- TL;DR: Inverting IRFM with benchmark T_eff for FGK stars finds the SDSS r band well-standardized but i, z, g offset by a few hundredths of a magnitude and u depending strongly on the filter curve adopted, with CALSPEC and Gaia XP independently corroborating the offsets.

**Deep Learning Calibration of the Quasar X-ray/UV Luminosity Relation for Cosmological Applications** · Jiaze Gao, Yun Chen, Lixin Xu et al. · [arXiv:2606.12265](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12265v1)
- Why it matters: Tests redshift evolution of a high-z standard-candle relation using a learned Hubble-diagram reference — touches ML-driven cosmological probe calibration.
- TL;DR: The LADDER deep-learning reconstruction of the Pantheon+ Hubble diagram is used as a reference to detect a non-linear redshift dependence of the quasar UV–X-ray scaling and to flag the z<0.7 sample as likely contaminated and unsuitable for cosmological use without further screening.
