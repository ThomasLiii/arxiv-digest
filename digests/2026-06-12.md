## Medium priority (≤8)

**SIDM and CDM interpretations of the million-solar-mass lensing perturber JVAS B1938+666-V** · Xingyu Zhang, Hai-Bo Yu · [arXiv:2606.12909](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12909v1)
- Why it matters: Direct follow-up on a strong-lensing-detected dark substructure — exactly the kind of object next-gen ML lens-finders are aiming to mass-produce, with implications for the dark-matter physics that would be read off such detections.
- TL;DR: Gravothermal fluid simulations show the dense-inner / extended-envelope perturber inferred from gravitational imaging is naturally produced by a deep core-collapsing SIDM halo; CDM can reproduce it only if an intermediate-mass black hole sits inside a heavily tidally stripped progenitor.

**Cluster Mass Inference from Galaxy Kinematics** · Bonny Y. Wang, Leander Thiele, Matthew Ho · [arXiv:2606.12938](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12938v1)
- Why it matters: A permutation-invariant ML pipeline for cluster mass posteriors from member-galaxy phase-space — directly relevant to upcoming wide-field spectroscopic + photometric surveys that will feed cluster cosmology.
- TL;DR: Pairing a Deep Sets encoder with normalizing-flow neural posterior estimation on Uchuu-UniverseMachine mocks halves the M–σ scatter to ~0.1 dex in the clean case and remains robust to cylindrical interloper contamination at the high-mass end.

**Machine Learning Does It and Does It Better: Unearthing Primordial Dark-Matter Velocities from the Matter Power Spectrum** · Keith R. Dienes, Jessica N. Howard, Fei Huang, Yuan-Zhen Li et al. · [arXiv:2606.13527](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13527v1)
- TL;DR: A 1D CNN reconstructs the primordial DM phase-space distribution from P(k) more accurately than the prior analytic mapping, and extends to non-thermal / multi-modal velocity distributions where the analytic ansatz breaks.

**A local Universe catalogue of structures and voids dynamically identified using Cosmic-Flows4++ZOA peculiar velocities** · A. M. Hollinger, H. M. Courtois · [arXiv:2606.13538](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13538v1)
- TL;DR: Applying the V-web algorithm to CF4++ZOA peculiar-velocity reconstructions yields a robust catalogue of 37 voids (13–38 h⁻¹ Mpc) and 42 knots out to z = 0.1, with uncertainties propagated through Hamiltonian Monte Carlo realizations.

**Galaxy clusters in the VIDEO fields: detection and characterisation in the context of MOONRISE** · Pierre Galois, Christophe Benoist, Gianluca Castignani, Matteo Maturi et al. · [arXiv:2606.13359](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13359v1)
- TL;DR: Running AMICO and WaZP on ~4.5 deg² of VIDEO photo-z data yields 519 joint cluster candidates (74 at z > 1.5), shows red-sequence populations consistent with passive evolution since z = 1.5–2, and forecasts that even the shallowest MOONRISE strategy can spectroscopically confirm clusters out to z ~ 1.7.

**Geometric obstruction to resolving the Hubble tension: orthogonality of scale and shape in distance measurements** · Zhihuan Zhou, Zhuang Miao, Sheng Bi, Chaoqian Ai et al. · [arXiv:2606.12822](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12822v1)
- TL;DR: An MCMC fit to DESI DR2 BAO + Planck + Pantheon+ shows that the ΔΩ_m = 0.037 BAO–SN gap is invariant under sound-horizon rescaling and that reconciling them would require opposite-sign w(z) deformations (cos θ = −0.97), leaving an irreducible 3.2σ H₀ deficit relative to SH0ES.
