## High priority (≤5)

**Deep Learning Segmentation of Spiral Arms and Bars for 600,000 Galaxies in DESI** · Ashley Spindler, Mike Walmsley, Karen L. Masters, Tobias Géron et al. · [arXiv:2606.16507](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16507v1)
- Why it matters: A textbook CNN-on-survey-imaging pipeline — U-net trained on Galaxy Zoo 3D citizen-science masks, then run over the full DESI Legacy Survey to produce per-pixel structural maps at scale. The released catalogue is exactly the kind of value-added product downstream foundation-model / representation-learning work can consume.
- TL;DR: ZooBot:3D, a U-net trained on Galaxy Zoo: 3D pixel labels, produces soft segmentation maps of spiral arms and bars for 639,636 face-on DESI galaxies, generalises to a wide range of disk morphologies, and unexpectedly learns to highlight rings despite minimal ring training data. The authors release the full catalogue plus cross-matches with the MaNGA and SAMI IFU surveys.

**The Edge-on Galaxies in the DESI survey (EGIDE): sample building and photometry** · Alexander A. Marchuk, Sergey S. Savchenko, Dmitry I. Makarov, Vladimir P. Reshetnikov et al. · [arXiv:2606.16734](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16734v1)
- Why it matters: Another concrete example of survey-imaging CNNs at scale — Zoobot fine-tuned for a morphological niche on DESI imaging, with multi-band SExtractor photometry, stellar masses, and redshifts attached. The resulting 149k-galaxy catalogue is a useful labelled set for representation-learning experiments on disk geometry.
- TL;DR: Fine-tuning the Zoobot neural model on Galaxy Zoo annotations and running it across DESI Legacy DR10, the authors build a ten-times-larger sample of 149,215 edge-on galaxy candidates spanning more than half the sky, after manual vetting. Preliminary analysis recovers distinct flattening and thickness trends between blue-cloud and red-sequence edge-ons, including a high-mass q-vs-M⋆ relation seen in both observations and figures-of-revolution models.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**Scalable Bayesian data curation for next-generation radio experiments** · S. A. K. Leeney, E. de Lera Acedo, W. J. Handley, H. T. J. Bevins et al. · [arXiv:2606.16525](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16525v1)
- TL;DR: A JAX-based Bayesian pipeline marginalises a latent anomaly indicator inside the likelihood to score REACH radio observations without thresholds or manual flags, jointly recovering weather, instrument drifts, and RFI on a year of data — a template for SKA-era automated curation.

**Graph Neural Networks Trained on Null Signal for Angle Reconstruction in X-ray Polarimetry** · Vittorio Latorre, Victor Rodriguez-Fernandez, Alessandro Di Marco, Fabio La Monaca et al. · [arXiv:2606.16922](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16922v1)
- TL;DR: A GNN that operates directly on the hexagonal Gas Pixel Detector topology and is trained only on unpolarized simulations recovers photoelectron emission-angle structure competitively with CNN baselines, exposing a gap between per-track reconstruction accuracy and global polarimetric fidelity.

**Tomography of the gamma-ray sky from cross-correlation with DESI DR2 and unWISE galaxies** · Alex Krolewski, Neal Dalal, Will J. Percival, Elena Pinetti et al. · [arXiv:2606.16297](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16297v1)
- TL;DR: Cross-correlating Fermi-LAT point sources and the unresolved gamma-ray background against DESI DR2 and unWISE galaxy maps yields a ~10σ detection and constrains the redshift distribution and bias of UGRB sources, while leaving room for a high-z (z>2) component best probed via CMB lensing cross-correlations.

**Benchmarking Exact, GP-Emulated, and Simulation-Based Inference for Late-Time Cosmology** · Sai Swagat Mishra · [arXiv:2606.16248](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16248v1)
- TL;DR: On Cosmic Chronometer + DESI DR2 BAO + Pantheon+ combinations, exact MCMC, GP-assisted MCMC, and neural SBI agree on H0 and Ωm to within ~1.5σ in the worst case, with SBI delivering order-of-magnitude runtime savings via amortised posteriors — useful guidance as cosmological pipelines scale.
