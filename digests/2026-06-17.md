## Medium priority (≤8)

**Weak-lensing mass calibration of Planck Sunyaev–Zel'dovich clusters with HSC-SSP Year 3** · Andrés Alejandro Plazas Malagón, Hironao Miyatake, Surhud More, Nicholas Battaglia et al. · [arXiv:2606.17984](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17984v1)
- TL;DR: Stacked HSC-SSP Y3 shear around 19 Planck SZ clusters, fit with a forward model that folds in selection, Eddington bias and miscentering, gives a mass bias 1−b = 0.73 ± 0.10 at z ≈ 0.24, reinforcing the need for sizable hydrostatic corrections to reconcile cluster counts with primary CMB cosmology.

**Field-level vs summaries: convergence of information in non-Gaussian density fields** · Ivana Nikolac, Fabian Schmidt, Beatriz Tucci · [arXiv:2606.18227](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18227v1)
- TL;DR: In a controlled toy model with a single quadratic bias, field-level MCMC inference systematically beats power-spectrum-plus-bispectrum and even up-to-6-point composite-operator summaries as non-Gaussianity or signal-to-noise increases, quantifying how much information summary-based pipelines leave on the table.

**Joint reconstruction of H(z) and fσ8(z) with physics-informed neural networks** · Konstantinos F. Dialektopoulos · [arXiv:2606.17614](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17614v1)
- TL;DR: A shared-backbone PINN with two heads is trained on CC+BAO H(z) and RSD fσ8(z) data with the linear growth equation enforced as a residual loss; the joint reconstruction sits below ΛCDM in fσ8 across redshift and shows an Om(z) departure at low z, robust to the choice of local H0 anchor.

**Querying an astronomical database using large language models: the ALeRCE text-to-SQL system** · P. A. Estevez, J. Espejo-Moreira, S. Sanfeliu-Alvarez, F. Forster et al. · [arXiv:2606.18108](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18108v1)
- TL;DR: A four-stage prompting pipeline (schema linking, query classification, decomposition, self-correction) over 13 LLMs is benchmarked on 110 NL/SQL pairs for the ZTF/Rubin ALeRCE broker, with self-correction reliably cutting execution errors and Claude Opus 4.6 leading on perfect-match scores.

**Synergy between CSST and future gravitational-wave detectors: probing primordial black holes by cross-correlating dark sirens with galaxies** · Ya-Nan Du, Ji-Yu Song, Jing-Fei Zhang, Xin Zhang · [arXiv:2606.17617](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17617v1)
- TL;DR: Forecasts of CSST photometric galaxy clustering cross-correlated with mock dark-siren catalogues from ET+2CE and the multiband BDET+2CE network show the PBH merger fraction can be exposed via clustering-bias differences once it exceeds ~40% (ET2CE) or ~20% (BDET2CE), with sky-localization driving most of the gain.

**Effective description of lensed gravitational waves diffracted by stellar fields** · Miguel Zumalacárregui, Xikai Shan · [arXiv:2606.17765](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17765v1)
- TL;DR: An SVD of wave-optics simulations of microlensing by stellar fields yields an orthonormal basis plus a coefficient PDF (ROSD, e.g. SVD-stellar-I5-aLIGO) that can be slotted into Bayesian GW parameter estimation as priors or consistency checks, a methodological template that overlaps with how strong-lensing populations are characterised.
