## High priority (≤5)

**Onsite Calibration of the Shear-Shear Correlation** · Cong Liu, Jun Zhang, Zhenjie Liu · [arXiv:2606.18705](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18705v1)
- Why it matters: Direct hit on the self-calibration interest — uses per-galaxy field-distortion signals stored in the shear catalog to calibrate shear-shear correlations onsite rather than relying on image simulations, applied here to HSC PDR3.
- TL;DR: Applying the Fourier_Quad onsite calibration to HSCpdr3 reveals shear bias that varies with photo-z bin, SNR cut, optical band, and estimator form, yet after calibration the shear-shear correlations and S₈/Ω_m constraints become consistent across all selections. The fiducial r/i/z catalog with SNR>10 yields S₈ = 0.740 ± 0.030 and Ω_m = 0.383⁺⁰·¹²⁹₋₀.₁₃₂, suggesting the technique can absorb instrumental and selection-dependent biases that pre-built image sims may miss.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**Impact of lensing magnification on the power spectrum turnover** · Yolanda Dube et al. · [arXiv:2606.18908](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18908v1)
- TL;DR: Mock Euclid-Hα and MegaMapper LBG forecasts show that lensing magnification shifts the matter-radiation-equality turnover scale by up to ~0.4σ (Euclid) and ~3.6σ (MegaMapper), so high-z turnover-as-standard-ruler analyses must model the magnification contribution or restrict to z ≲ 2.9.

**Constraints on Cosmic Strings from the Curl-Mode CMB Lensing Power Spectrum measured by ACT DR6** · A. I. Lonappan et al. · [arXiv:2606.19337](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19337v1)
- TL;DR: Using ACT DR6's curl-mode CMB lensing reconstruction over 9400 deg², the authors push 2σ bounds on the cosmic string tension to GµP⁻¹ ≤ 3.5×10⁻⁵, nearly an order of magnitude tighter than the 2008 ACT analysis, and tighter still when combined with Planck 2013.

**From Evidence to Evident: Decisive Cosmological Evidence for the Normal Neutrino Mass Hierarchy** · Raul Jimenez et al. · [arXiv:2606.18987](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18987v1)
- TL;DR: Combining DESI DR2 clustering with Planck CamSpec drives Σm_ν below the inverted-ordering floor and yields Bayes factors K > 460 in favor of the normal hierarchy across a 2D grid of priors, marking the transition from prior-sensitive Bayesian preference to likelihood-dominated exclusion.

**Exploring Primordial Non-Gaussianity Measurements in the CSST Spectroscopic Survey** · Jiangnan Duan et al. · [arXiv:2606.18744](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18744v1)
- TL;DR: Jiutian-based ELG mocks for CSST at z = 0.3, 0.6, 0.9 show the bispectrum (especially squeezed-limit configurations) only marginally tightens f_NL beyond the power spectrum (5–6% gain), but the full spectroscopic survey should still improve current f_NL precision by up to an order of magnitude.

**The Chandra-Gaia Catalog of Counterparts: Resolving ambiguous Gaia matches to X-ray sources in the Chandra Source Catalog using Machine Learning** · V. Samuel Pérez-Díaz et al. · [arXiv:2606.19329](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19329v1)
- TL;DR: A LightGBM classifier trained on NWAY-derived high-confidence pairs and source-property features matches ~113k of 254k CSC v2.1 X-ray sources to Gaia DR3, reproduces 95% of NWAY's COUP matches without using positions, and flags ~10k likely chance coincidences in separation-only catalogs.
