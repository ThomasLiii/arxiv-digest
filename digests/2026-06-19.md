## High priority (≤5)

**The SPHEREx View of Galaxy Clusters: A Simulation-based Validation of the Forced Photometry Pipeline for Extended Sources** · Hyeonguk Bahk, Ho Seong Hwang, Lindsey Bleem et al. · [arXiv:2606.19875](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19875v1)
- Why it matters: Direct hit on SPHEREx mission methods and on deblending/forced-photometry in extended-source regimes, the kind of pipeline characterisation work that informs cluster-cosmology readiness.
- TL;DR: The authors run end-to-end mock SPHEREx observations (built from DESI Legacy + COSMOS ancillaries) on eight clusters across z ≈ 0.02–1.1 and use The Tractor for forced photometry. Photometry is essentially unbiased, but blended neighbours dominate catastrophic outliers; quality-selected members still yield σ_NMAD ≈ 0.003–0.01 photo-zs and |Δz|/(1+z) < 0.002 cluster redshifts at z ≲ 0.5 — adequate for cluster cosmology.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**Finite-Core Signatures in LISA-Band Wave-Optics Lensing by Low-Mass Dark Matter Halos** · Dejiang Li et al. · [arXiv:2606.19995](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19995v1)
- TL;DR: Comparing NFW and cored-NFW (plus SIDM-isothermal) lenses in the wave-optics regime, the authors show LISA GW amplification residuals peak for r_c/r_s ≈ 0.25–0.3 on halos of M_vir ≳ 10⁷ M_⊙ — a baseline for diffraction-based dark-matter halo core diagnostics.

**Lightstack: A Python Package for Creating Photometric Data Cubes** · Andressa Wille et al. · [arXiv:2606.20360](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20360v1)
- TL;DR: A small Python toolkit that crops regions of interest from multi-band mosaics, stacks them into a data cube, and PSF-matches across bands — aimed at HST/JWST and the Roman/Rubin era.

**VASTER: The ASKAP real-time fast-imaging pipeline — overview and discovery of two long period transients** · Yuanming Wang et al. · [arXiv:2606.20067](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20067v1)
- TL;DR: VASTER produces 15-minute images of most ASKAP survey data in real time, and in its first two weeks already pulled out two new long-period transients (6.48 h and 4.69 h) — a working real-time rare-object discovery pipeline on a wide-field survey.

**Multi-band Structural Analysis of KiDS-selected Low Surface Brightness Galaxies with Hyper Suprime-Cam Imaging** · Dipanjan Mitra, Kanak Saha · [arXiv:2606.19862](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19862v1)
- TL;DR: GALFIT Sérsic fits across HSC G/R/I imaging confirm 205 KiDS-selected LSBG candidates as a structurally homogeneous, diffuse (n ≈ 0.7), mostly red (87%) reference sample.

**ELMA: ELlipse-based bar MAjor axis estimator** · Bruna R. Bragança de Lima et al. · [arXiv:2606.20370](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20370v1)
- TL;DR: A pip-installable bar-length estimator that runs iterative elliptical-isophote fits on 2D imaging and reports physical bar lengths via WCS + redshift, demonstrated on JWST/NIRCam GOODS-South barred galaxies.

**Evolving Dark Energy Is Vacuum Energy After All** · Dong Ha Lee, Carsten van de Bruck, Eleonora Di Valentino et al. · [arXiv:2606.20036](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20036v1)
- TL;DR: A QCD-vacuum-driven dark-energy model with no new fields fits Planck+ACT+SPT-3G CMB, DESI DR2 BAO, and Pantheon+/DES-Dovekie SNe, reproduces DESI's preferred late-time w(z) evolution, and naturally yields an effective phantom crossing without scalar-field instabilities.

**The Hubble tension: A decade review** · Rong-Gen Cai, Shao-Jiang Wang · [arXiv:2606.20434](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20434v1)
- TL;DR: A review framing the H_0 discrepancy as Planck- and ladder-independent, surveying early-Universe sound-horizon fixes, late-Universe SN absolute-magnitude routes, and the remaining interacting-dark-energy and inhomogeneity-transition options.

**Enhancing the Detection Sensitivity of Primordial Parity Violation using Galaxy Spins** · Byoungjo Yu, Junsup Shim, Hyunmi Song · [arXiv:2606.19709](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19709v1)
- TL;DR: Using parity-asymmetric N-body sims, mass- and density-based halo selection boosts the SNR of the late-time spin-field parity signal beyond the full-sample baseline, and upcoming spectroscopic surveys can supply the needed statistics.
