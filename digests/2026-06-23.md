## Medium priority (≤8)

**Revisiting the 'Lensing is Low' Problem with UNIONS** · Martine C. T. Campbell, Jack Elvin-Poole, Michael J. Hudson et al. · [arXiv:2606.23651](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23651v1)
- Why it matters: Galaxy–galaxy lensing measurements from a wide-area weak-lensing survey (UNIONS) directly intersect the LSS/weak-lensing cosmology interest area.
- TL;DR: The authors stack background-shape catalogs from UNIONS around BOSS CMASS lenses across ~2650 deg² and jointly fit clustering and lensing with an HOD. Unlike earlier reports, they no longer see a clear lensing-is-low deficit, though a mild reduction in σ8 relative to Planck is preferred, and they argue large scales matter more for the discrepancy than previously thought.

**Field-level weak lensing cosmology with <100 simulations using multifidelity simulation-based inference** · Alex A. Saoulis, Kiyam Lin, Niall Jeffrey et al. · [arXiv:2606.23346](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23346v1)
- Why it matters: Methods for cheap, well-calibrated field-level weak-lensing inference are directly relevant to upcoming Stage-IV survey analyses.
- TL;DR: They pre-train neural compression and SBI networks on fast log-normal GLASS mocks of KiDS-Legacy-like shear fields, then fine-tune on a small batch of full N-body simulations. With only 60–100 high-fidelity sims they recover informative, well-calibrated cosmological posteriors, cutting simulation budgets by roughly an order of magnitude.

**Testing the Generalization and Domain Stability of Compact Feature Representations for Photometric Supernova Classification** · Anurag Garg · [arXiv:2606.22879](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22879v1)
- TL;DR: A 16-feature physically interpretable representation plus XGBoost classifies SPCC Type Ia SNe well (F1 0.84) and is stable under resampling and feature perturbations, but transferring SPCC-trained models to PLAsTiCC collapses because the class centroids sit in different regions of feature space — a clean illustration that "compact and interpretable" does not buy survey invariance.
