## High priority (≤5)

**Euclid preparation. First investigation of the impact of cross-contamination on spectroscopic redshift measurements with pixel-level simulations** · Euclid Collaboration · [arXiv:2606.24697](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24697v1)
- Why it matters: A Euclid mission methods paper assessing a known weak spot — NISP slitless-spectroscopy contamination — that directly impacts the redshift catalogs everyone downstream will rely on.
- TL;DR: Using pixel-level NISP simulations, the team injects controlled levels of overlapping-spectrum contamination and measures how spec-z recovery degrades. They quantify the regime in which contamination biases or breaks redshift measurements and identify where mitigation in the pipeline is most needed before Q1/DR1 data.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**A First Measurement of Circumgalactic Dust Reddening from Only 4.6 deg² of the Rubin Observatory's DP1** · (Rubin/LSST team) · [arXiv:2606.24159](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24159v1)
- Why it matters: First-light scientific use of Rubin DP1 photometric data with stacked photo-z foregrounds — a useful early benchmark of what LSST imaging can do at tiny footprint.
- TL;DR: Stacking background-galaxy colors around foreground galaxies on 4.6 deg² of ComCam DP1 yields a clear chromatic reddening profile out to ~1 Mpc, scaling roughly with the expected dust column.

**CHEX-MATE: AMALGAM weak-lensing analysis of 41 Planck Sunyaev-Zel'dovich-selected galaxy clusters** · CHEX-MATE/AMALGAM collaboration · [arXiv:2606.24142](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24142v1)
- Why it matters: Wide-field weak-lensing shear measurement on a uniform SZ-selected sample — the kind of mass-calibration backbone Stage-IV cluster cosmology needs.
- TL;DR: Subaru/Suprime-Cam and CFHT/MegaPrime shear catalogs around 41 Planck SZ clusters yield a median per-cluster S/N of 6.5, with cross-component nulls consistent with zero, supporting clean weak-lensing mass estimates.

**Probing Baryonic Feedback Effect with CSST Weak Lensing and Future FRB Measurements** · [arXiv:2606.24061](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24061v1)
- Why it matters: Forecasts for cross-correlating an upcoming wide imaging survey (CSST) with FRB DMs — relevant for getting baryonic-feedback nuisances under control in weak-lensing cosmology.
- TL;DR: A joint Fisher-style forecast shows CSST cosmic-shear plus SKA/DSA-2000 FRB dispersion-measure statistics break baryonic-feedback degeneracies that either probe leaves unconstrained.

**The 3D clustering of Lyman Alpha Emitters measured with DESI** · DESI Collaboration · [arXiv:2606.24852](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24852v1)
- Why it matters: A new high-z tracer (LAEs) added to DESI's clustering pipeline expands LSS science into a redshift range where existing samples are sparse.
- TL;DR: DESI spectroscopy of IBIS-selected LAE candidates yields the auto- and quasar-cross 2-point correlation and power spectrum, fit with HOD and analytic models to recover LAE bias and host-halo properties.

**Measuring local primordial non-Gaussianity from the clustering of DESI DR1 LRGs and QSOs** · DESI Collaboration · [arXiv:2606.24651](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24651v1)
- Why it matters: A first configuration-space fNL constraint from DESI DR1, with the kind of imaging-systematics mitigation directly relevant to LSST/Euclid cosmology pipelines.
- TL;DR: The authors apply a simulation-based clustering model and a new imaging-systematics mitigation to DR1 LRGs and QSOs to deliver a fresh fNL measurement from the 2-point correlation function.

**Weak Lensing with the SKAO: Radio Shear Measurement** · [arXiv:2606.24638](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24638v1)
- Why it matters: Radio weak-lensing is a complementary cross-check for optical cosmic shear and an obvious place where shape-measurement methods need to mature.
- TL;DR: A review/forecast of how SKA-Mid can finally bring radio cosmic shear to optical-survey-grade sensitivity, including the calibration and PSF-handling problems specific to interferometric shape measurement.

**Extragalactic test of General Relativity with time-delay gravitational lenses** · [arXiv:2606.24278](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24278v1)
- Why it matters: A direct observational use of strong-lensing systems, in this case for a GR/PPN test rather than H0, broadens the strong-lens science case beyond cosmography.
- TL;DR: Using H0LiCOW time-delay lenses and a model-independent distance treatment, the authors constrain the PPN γ parameter, finding agreement with GR within current systematics.

**The Impact of Host Galaxy Properties on Supernova Classification with Hierarchical Labels** · [arXiv:2606.24807](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24807v1)
- Why it matters: ML-driven photometric classification on Rubin-era data is the kind of survey-imaging pipeline work that touches anomaly detection and downstream rare-event triggers.
- TL;DR: Adding host-galaxy features to a hierarchical SN classifier trained on Rubin-like light curves measurably improves discrimination between SN subclasses, especially where light-curve information alone is ambiguous.
