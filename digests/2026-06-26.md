## Medium priority (≤8)

**Clustering of high-redshift quasars with DESI DR2** · M. Charles, P. Martini, A. J. Ross et al. · [arXiv:2606.27115](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27115v1)
- Why it matters: Large-scale-structure clustering from a flagship spectroscopic survey — directly relevant to LSS interest.
- TL;DR: From ~715,000 DESI DR2 quasars at 2.0 < z < 3.5 they measure a mean linear bias of 3.61 and fit its redshift evolution with a simple quadratic in (1+z), useful as a tracer-bias input for cosmological analyses at z>2.

**Methodological Frontiers in 21-cm Intensity Mapping: Treatment of Systematics and Foreground Contamination** · Marta Spinelli, Matilde Barberi-Squarotti, José Luis Bernal et al. · [arXiv:2606.27244](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27244v1)
- Why it matters: 21-cm intensity mapping is a major upcoming LSS probe and the foreground/systematics question is the practical bottleneck.
- TL;DR: A community review of the leading methods being developed for SKA-Mid single-dish 21-cm intensity mapping out to z~3, organising the toolbox for foreground removal, gain/beam systematics and noise treatment, and pointing to which approaches are converging.

**The SKA View of the Sunyaev-Zeldovich Effect from Massive Cosmic Halos** · Yvette C. Perrott, Luca Di Mascolo, Rémi Adam et al. · [arXiv:2606.26562](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26562v1)
- Why it matters: tSZ from clusters is a cornerstone LSS/cosmology probe and SKA band 5b opens a new high-resolution view.
- TL;DR: Forecasts and design considerations showing that SKA at 14 GHz can image thermal SZ pressure substructure in massive halos with high resolution, complementing existing mm-wave SZ surveys.

**Forward-modelling the Tolman and distance-duality tests with IllustrisTNG** · Harry Desmond, Tariq Yasin, Richard Stiskalek, Sebastian von Hausegger · [arXiv:2606.27182](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27182v1)
- Why it matters: A simulation-based forward model of a classic cosmography test — a methodology pattern worth tracking even if the science is theory-adjacent.
- TL;DR: They build a realistic mock pipeline from IllustrisTNG to predict observed Tolman and distance-duality signals, showing that selection and morphological-evolution effects can produce the "flat" scalings reported by JWST/ASTRODEEP without new physics.

**Quintom Model Perturbations** · L. W. K. Goh, A. N. Taylor · [arXiv:2606.27049](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27049v1)
- Why it matters: Adds LSS observables to a phantom-crossing dark-energy model — relevant for interpreting the w0wa results coming out of DESI.
- TL;DR: They derive linear perturbations for a two-field quintom model and show it can reproduce the w0waCDM matter-power suppression and late-ISW enhancement without ghost instabilities, giving a physical model for the recent phantom-crossing hints in the data.

**Distance-Ladder Measurements of the Hubble Constant: Recent Progress, Systematics, and Prospects** · Xiaodian Chen, Shu Wang · [arXiv:2606.26831](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26831v1)
- Why it matters: Useful synthesis of where the local-ladder side of the Hubble tension actually stands.
- TL;DR: A review framing the distance ladder as a covariance network from geometric anchors to Hubble-flow probes, summarising current systematic budgets across Cepheid/TRGB/JAGB/Miras and outlining what JWST/Roman/Rubin-era data will tighten.

**The SPOTLIGHT Multibeam Real-Time Transient Detection System** · Ujjwal Panda, Jayanta Roy, Kshitij Bane et al. · [arXiv:2606.27262](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27262v1)
- Why it matters: Real-time transient discovery is a concrete rare-object/anomaly pipeline at survey scale.
- TL;DR: Describes the GPU-backed (90×A100) commensal FRB/transient backend now running on the upgraded GMRT, with multibeam search, localisation, and high-resolution capture in a single real-time pipeline.
