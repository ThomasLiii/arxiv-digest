## High priority (≤5)

**GIGA-Lens 2.0: Strong-Lens Modeling on Multiple GPU Nodes** · Huang, Upson, Ratier-Werbin et al. · [arXiv:2606.30633](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30633)
- Why it matters: Scaling strong-lens modelling to thousands of systems is exactly the bottleneck for ML-driven detection pipelines feeding sample-scale science.
- TL;DR: The authors extend their GPU-accelerated Bayesian strong-lens framework to run across multiple nodes, demonstrating execution on 512 A100s spread over 128 nodes. They benchmark the throughput on 100 mock systems plus a DESI lens and report further framework-level performance tweaks.

**Gaussian processes on ray-guided transformed uniform grids for fast, flexible, and auto-differentiable adaptive source reconstruction in lens modelling** · Enzi, Krawczyk, Li, Collett · [arXiv:2606.30620](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30620)
- Why it matters: A differentiable, FFT-fast source reconstruction that plugs into gradient-based and ML lens pipelines, with the authors explicitly motivating it for the Euclid sample.
- TL;DR: Instead of Voronoi/Delaunay adaptive meshes, the source plane is modelled as a Gaussian process on a uniform grid that is then warped by the ray-traced cumulative distribution to concentrate resolution where it is needed. The construction keeps everything auto-differentiable and uses the FFT for speed, and on mocks it matches the fit quality of standard adaptive grids with roughly half as many source pixels per dimension.

**Streak detection in the VST/OmegaCAM archive using deep learning** · Rachith, Hellmich, Fiszbin et al. · [arXiv:2606.30286](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30286)
- Why it matters: A concrete CNN pipeline applied at archive scale to wide-field survey imaging — the kind of operational deep-learning-on-survey-data result that informs how to deploy similar tooling for science detections.
- TL;DR: The pipeline pairs a Hough-transform-lookup CNN for streak detection with a VGG6 classifier for false-positive rejection, trained on 384k augmented patches. Run over a year of OmegaCAM data (1.25M CCD frames), it finds ~25k streaks at precision 0.99 with 97% true-positive retention, with ~20% of detections uncorrelated to the space-track catalogue.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**Universal distance modes from DESI BAO and Type Ia supernovae: what do cosmological rulers actually measure?** · Zaldarriaga · [arXiv:2606.30527](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30527)
- TL;DR: An SVD of DESI BAO + three SN compilations shows that the dominant measured direction is essentially Ω_m h² and is where the CMB tension actually sits, with only w0-wa and curvature opening additional independent directions.

**Cosmology-dependent covariance in galaxy cluster number counts: consequences for parameter inference** · Lettieri, Penna-Lima, Vitenti · [arXiv:2606.30459](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30459)
- TL;DR: For LSST-like cluster-count analyses, fixing the SSC+shot-noise covariance at a wrong cosmology leaves point estimates unbiased but can substantially miscalibrate the S_8 confidence region; a single covariance re-evaluation at the best fit recovers correct errors.

**Cosmology with Multi-Wavelength Line Intensity Mapping Synergies in the SKAO Era** · Sarkar, Majumdar, Murmu et al. · [arXiv:2606.30224](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30224)
- TL;DR: A review-chapter forecast of cross-correlating SKAO 21-cm with [CII], [OIII], CO, Lyα, and Hα LIM experiments to suppress shared systematics and disentangle astrophysical from cosmological parameters via two-point and higher-order statistics.

**Cosmology with Intensity Mapping via Statistics Beyond the Power Spectrum in the SKAO Era** · Majumdar, Sarkar, Noble et al. · [arXiv:2606.30200](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30200)
- TL;DR: Companion chapter surveying non-Gaussian summaries (VID, bispectrum, ℓ1-norm, Minkowski functionals) for post-reionization HI intensity maps and forecasting their SKAO detectability under realistic noise and foreground assumptions.

**Modeling survey-window and integral-constraint effects on PNG in the galaxy power spectrum with light-cone mocks** · Nakano, Takada, Kurita, Terasawa · [arXiv:2606.29735](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29735)
- TL;DR: A linear-theory light-cone mock framework matches the BOSS estimator at the multipole level and shows that the standard analytic survey-window/integral-constraint treatment breaks down on PNG-relevant super-survey scales, motivating a mock-based correction for f_NL searches.

**Joint population and strong-lensing inference for resolved gravitational-wave events probes the black-hole merger rate beyond the peak of star formation** · Mould · [arXiv:2606.30144](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30144)
- TL;DR: A joint Bayesian analysis of the LVK BBH catalogue simultaneously infers per-event strong-lensing status, magnification, and the merger-rate population; no event exceeds 1% lensing probability, but the high-redshift rate upper limits tighten by an order of magnitude versus standard treatments.
