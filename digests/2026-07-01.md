## High priority (≤5)

**Joint inference of weak lensing convergence map and cosmology with diffusion models** · Benjamin Remy, Chihway Chang, Rebecca Willett · [arXiv:2606.31988](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31988)
- Why it matters: Hits the sweet spot of ML-driven weak lensing analysis — a transformer over pixel-space maps plus diffusion-based implicit inference, directly relevant to foundation-model style workflows for survey imaging.
- TL;DR: The authors train a pixel-space transformer that jointly treats cosmological parameters and the shear field as tokens, and use a diffusion process to sample from the posterior over convergence maps and parameters simultaneously. On log-normal wCDM mocks they recover MCMC-consistent cosmological posteriors and reconstructed maps that match both two-point and one-point statistics, and pass a MIRA calibration check on the joint posterior. It's pitched as a route to full field-level inference without needing a differentiable forward model.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**Constraining dark energy with complementary probes of large-scale structure** · Neel Shah, Kazuya Koyama, Johannes Noller et al. · [arXiv:2606.31977](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31977)
- TL;DR: They combine DESI DR1 RSD, DES Y3 3×2pt, and CMB×galaxy/CMB-lensing ISW cross-correlations in an EFT-of-Dark-Energy framework, gaining a ~2.7× improvement in the DE-perturbation figure of merit over expansion-history-only fits.

**Predisposition of galaxy clusters to producing exotic hyperbolic umbilic lensing configurations** · Quentin Basto, Johan Richard, David J. Lagattuta et al. · [arXiv:2606.31583](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31583)
- TL;DR: Using parametric mass models of 74 clusters, they map source-plane regions and comoving volumes where rare hyperbolic-umbilic strong-lensing configurations can form, and identify which clusters are most predisposed to host them.

**The Large-Scale Structure of the Universe through the SKA lenses** · Virginia Cuciti, Surajit Paul, Viral Parekh et al. · [arXiv:2606.31503](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31503)
- TL;DR: Review of how SKA (and precursors LOFAR/uGMRT/MeerKAT) will trace diffuse synchrotron emission from cluster outskirts and cosmic-web filaments, connecting recent megahalo/bridge detections to what full SKA sensitivity should reveal.

**An overview of stray light findings and interpretation during on-sky commissioning of LSSTCam** · Gabriele Rodeghiero, Alex Drlica-Wagner, Alessio Taranto et al. · [arXiv:2606.31945](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31945)
- TL;DR: A tour of the stray- and scattered-light artefacts encountered since LSSTCam saw first photon in April 2025, plus the observation/ray-tracing/mitigation workflow used to trace each feature to its opto-mechanical origin.

**Early Telescope Throughput Results from the Collimated Beam Projector at the Vera C. Rubin Observatory** · Nathan Amouroux, Parker Fagrelius, Thibault Guillemin et al. · [arXiv:2606.31822](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31822)
- TL;DR: First on-sky demonstration of Rubin's Collimated Beam Projector for in-situ photometric calibration, including spatially resolved LSST filter bandpass edge shifts of a few nm across the focal plane driven by ray-angle variation.

**Multi-Scale Contrastive Attention for Light-Curve Representation Learning** · Torsha Majumder, Konstantin Malanchev, Emille E. O. Ishida · [arXiv:2606.31627](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31627)
- TL;DR: Astra-CLR pretrains an attention-based, contrastive self-supervised model on ~2.1M ZTF light curves using asymmetric short/long temporal views and a late-fusion multi-filter head, hitting ~70% accuracy on a 12-class variability task and improving further with light fine-tuning.

**COSMOS-Web: does halo mass alone shape the clustering of star-forming and quiescent galaxies?** · Louise Paquereau, Clotilde Laigle, Henry Joy McCracken et al. · [arXiv:2606.31978](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31978)
- TL;DR: After matching halo-mass distributions with UniverseMachine, COSMOS-Web quiescent galaxies still cluster more strongly than star-formers by 0.5–1 dex out to z=5, with one-halo conformity signals suggesting environmental quenching dominates for low-mass systems at z≤2.

**DEVILS: Morphologically-selected galaxy merger fractions vs close-pair samples** · Melissa F. Fuentealba-Fuentes, Luke J. M. Davies, Aaron S. G. Robotham et al. · [arXiv:2606.31140](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31140)
- TL;DR: Using HST/ACS imaging of the COSMOS field, they compare visual, non-parametric (CAS-Gini-M20), and close-pair merger fractions from z=0.2–0.9 and find morphology-based estimates consistently exceed pair-based ones, with limited sample overlap between methods.
