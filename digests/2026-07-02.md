## High priority (≤5)

**Strong Lensing Tomography: Double and pseudo multi-source plane strong gravitational lensing to constrain dark energy** · Paras Sharma, Simon Birrer, Narayan Khadka et al. · [arXiv:2607.01005](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01005)
- Why it matters: A strong-lensing cosmology method purpose-built for the ~10^5 galaxy-galaxy lenses expected from LSST/Euclid/Roman — exactly the survey-scale SL regime I care about, with an honest treatment of the mass-sheet degeneracy.
- TL;DR: The authors argue that true double-source-plane lenses are too rare to drive precision dark-energy constraints, and instead pair up single-plane lenses with self-similar deflectors ("pseudo DSPLs") to reproduce the tomographic geometry without multi-plane ray tracing. Folding the deflector's mass-sheet degeneracy into a hierarchical forecast, they show the LSST 10-year photometric sample alone can reach σ(w0) ~ 0.45 (dropping to ~0.29 with an Ωm prior) while jointly pinning down the MSD and profile slope at the ~2% level. The result is that photometric statistical volume beats small samples with precise spectroscopic follow-up.

**Point spread function wavefront recovery from in-focus stellar observations** · Ezequiel Centofanti, Samuel Farrens, Jean-Luc Starck, Tobias Liaudat · [arXiv:2607.01032](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01032)
- Why it matters: WaveDiff is the Euclid-lineage differentiable PSF model, and pushing it from good pixel-space PSFs to accurate physical wavefronts is directly relevant to survey imaging calibration and self-calibration workflows.
- TL;DR: The paper extends WaveDiff — a semi-parametric, differentiable model that fits PSFs in wavefront space — by adding a projection step that couples its learnable non-parametric features back onto the physical wavefront basis. Where the original recovered pixel-space PSFs well but left the wavefront error itself ~30% off ground truth, the new optimisation cycle brings WFE error down to about 3% using only noisy, undersampled, in-focus polychromatic star cutouts. This makes it, per the authors, the first method to combine wide-field WFE recovery, in-focus-only polychromatic data, and non-parametric wavefront features in one framework.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**Galaxy Clusters Selected via the Sunyaev-Zel'dovich Effect in 5 year data from the SPT-3G Main Survey** · L. E. Bleem, M. Klein, K. Kornoelje et al. · [arXiv:2607.01175](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01175)
- TL;DR: New SPT-3G SZ catalog of 8,892 candidate clusters (7,190 optically/IR confirmed) spanning z ≈ 0.04–2 and 8×10^13 – 1.6×10^15 M⊙, with 1,780 at z>1 — a substantial jump in high-z cluster statistics for cluster cosmology.

**Cosmology from HI galaxy surveys with the SKA** · Ainulnabilah Nasirudin, Philip Bull, Ziad Sakr et al. · [arXiv:2607.00827](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00827)
- TL;DR: Forecasts for wide-field HI galaxy surveys with SKAO-MID, showing they can deliver competitive BAO and RSD measurements while sampling more of the halo mass function than optical surveys, with Tully-Fisher distances as a bonus probe.

**Assembly bias and the redshift evolution of intrinsic alignments for LRGs** · A. Herle, N. E. Chisari, H. Hoekstra, D. Neumann · [arXiv:2607.00785](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00785)
- TL;DR: Using the 2.8 Gpc FLAMINGO run, they show IA amplitude for LRG-like samples depends on halo formation time in addition to mass, and provide a mass-plus-redshift IA fit that weak-lensing analyses can adopt as a more physical prior.

**Tilting at the Turnover: Modeling the Faint-End of the UV Luminosity Function Behind Abell s1063 with JWST** · Caio Moreira Goolsby, Christopher J. Conselice, Duncan Austin et al. · [arXiv:2607.01129](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01129)
- TL;DR: JWST/NIRCam GLIMPSE imaging behind Abell S1063 is combined with a gradual quadratic turnover model of the z=6–11 UVLF, showing faint galaxies below M_UV = −17 supply at least half of the reionization-era ionizing budget.

**SPHEREx 0.75 to 5 μm Spectra for a Sequence of Nearby Brown Dwarfs** · Zafar Rustamkulov, J. Kirkpatrick, Rachel Akeson et al. · [arXiv:2607.00543](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00543)
- TL;DR: An early SPHEREx science result: R~40–100 spectra of 37 field brown dwarfs across L0–Y4 are compared against Sonora, Elf Owl, BT-Settl and ATMO grids, exposing systematic mismatches at the L/T transition and around the CO/CO2 features.

**The "dark dips" phenomenon in the LSST Camera on-sky images** · Claire Juramy, Pierre Antilogus, Pierre Astier et al. · [arXiv:2607.00925](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00925)
- TL;DR: On-sky ComCam/LSSTCam data reveal a new sensor effect on some ITL STA3800 CCDs where columns hosting a bright star sit up to ~7% below sky level, flanked by slight excesses — consistent with a lateral field distortion driven by saturated pixels and important to model for photometry.

**GAIA: Geometry-Adaptive Operator Learning for Forward and Inverse Problems** · Meenakshi Krishnan, Pranav Pulijala, Ke Chen et al. · [arXiv:2607.01128](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01128)
- TL;DR: A neural operator that encodes the domain boundary and interior field into "geometry tokens" and conditions integral-transform layers via cross-attention, letting a single model handle both forward PDEs and inverse boundary-value problems on arbitrary geometries.

**Foreground Characterization and Mitigation in the Observations of the CD/EoR with the SKA** · Jacob Burba, Philip Bull, Emilio Ceccotti et al. · [arXiv:2607.01048](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01048)
- TL;DR: A review of Galactic synchrotron / free-free / point-source foregrounds that outshine the 21-cm signal by 4–5 orders of magnitude, and how the SKA-Low AA* array design, calibration accuracy and modelling strategy are being tuned to peel them cleanly.
