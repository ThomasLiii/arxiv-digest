## Medium priority (≤8)

**Alleviating prior dependencies for DESI DR1 clustering fits through reparameterization** · Marco Bonici, Simone Paradiso, Glenn McGee et al. · [arXiv:2607.02498](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02498v1)
- TL;DR: The authors tackle prior-volume projection effects in EFTofLSS fits to DESI DR1 power spectrum multipoles using nonlinear orthogonalization plus an on-the-fly Jeffreys prior, which recovers late-time expansion parameters ($H_0$, $w_0$, $w_a$) that agree with profile-likelihood results — a useful methodological reference for full-shape clustering analyses.

**meer21cm: an Analysis Pipeline and Comprehensive Toolkit for HI Intensity Mapping** · Zhaoting Chen, Steven Cunnington, Daniel Tassie et al. · [arXiv:2607.01864](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01864v1)
- TL;DR: An open-source Python pipeline for single-dish HI intensity mapping targeted at MeerKLASS UHF data, covering foreground cleaning through parameter inference and demonstrating percent-level power-spectrum accuracy on mock multi-tracer analyses with overlapping optical galaxy catalogues.

**Assessing Ultra-Cool Dwarf Contamination in Photometrically Selected High-Redshift Galaxy Samples** · Onnalin Innala, Nicha Leethochawalit, Takahiro Morishita, Michele Trenti · [arXiv:2607.02354](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02354v1)
- TL;DR: FC-ENZO forecasts UCD contamination rates in photometric high-z galaxy searches across HST/COSMOS, Roman deep-tier and JWST, finding T- and early Y-dwarfs are the dominant interlopers at $z\sim8$ and that contamination clusters near each survey's limiting magnitude — relevant for Roman/Euclid photometric selection design.
