## Medium priority (≤8)

**CausticFlow: An Efficient Machine Learning Framework Combining Neural Differential Equations and Normalizing Flows for Binary Microlensing Parameter Inference** · Haibin Ren, Wei Zhu · [arXiv:2607.04955](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04955v1)
- Why it matters: Amortized ML inference targeted at Roman/CSST-scale lensing surveys — adjacent to your strong-lensing ML interest and a good look at how NCDEs + normalizing flows behave on irregular real survey light curves.
- TL;DR: CausticFlow couples a neural controlled differential equation encoder for gappy, irregularly sampled KMTNet-like light curves with a normalizing-flow head that emits full posteriors for the mass ratio and projected separation of binary microlenses in under a second per event. Used as a proposal for local refinement, it recovers the correct model for 7/10 real events at a few-percent parameter precision, positioning it as a triage engine ahead of the Roman/CSST/ET microlensing data deluge.

**Interpretable Human-Label-Free Deep Learning for Real-Bogus Classification with Uncertainty Quantification** · Raphaël Bonnet-Guerrini, Bruno Sanchez, Dominique Fouchez et al. · [arXiv:2607.05393](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05393v1)
- Why it matters: Directly relevant to anomaly/rare-object pipelines on survey imagery: shows that transient discovery can be trained end-to-end on injected sources plus a bogus-contaminated survey stream, avoiding human labels — a template that should transfer to Rubin/Euclid difference-image pipelines.
- TL;DR: The authors combine simulated transient injections with a contaminated survey class and train a dual-network real-bogus classifier via asymmetric co-teaching, adding a cheap hybrid MC-dropout/dual-net uncertainty estimator. The model stays stable under heavy label contamination, its latent space cleanly separates bogus subclasses, and the pipeline is designed to be re-run for each new survey.

**Weak Evolution of Cosmic Atomic Hydrogen over the Past 4.5 Billion Years** · Chuan-Peng Zhang, Hong Guo, Yizhou Gu, Amélie Saintonge et al. · [arXiv:2607.05326](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05326v1)
- Why it matters: A ~2.5 M-galaxy FAST × DESI stack — a concrete low-z clustering/gas benchmark that models of LSS gas physics and future intensity-mapping/photometric analyses will need to match.
- TL;DR: Combining FAST 21-cm spectra with DESI optical spectroscopy over ~12 000 deg², the authors measure Ω_HI out to z ≈ 0.41 and find only a 1.12 ± 0.10 raw decline (a factor of 1.35 before systematic corrections), far shallower than the factor-2.46 drop in cosmic star-formation rate density. HI gas fractions at fixed stellar mass barely evolve, arguing against HI depletion as the driver of the late-time SFRD fall and providing a stringent target for gas-accretion and phase-conversion models.

**Commensal image plane transient search methods with the SKAO** · Alex Andersson, Dougal Dobbie, Natasha Hurley-Walker, Hao Qiu, Kaustubh Rajwade et al. · [arXiv:2607.05118](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05118v1)
- Why it matters: A consolidated view of fast-imaging, artifact rejection, and triggered reprocessing for image-plane surveys — the kind of pipeline scaffolding that any ML anomaly/transient detector eventually plugs into.
- TL;DR: This SKAO chapter summarises the SKA-pathfinder-derived recipe for commensal image-plane transient searches: fast-imaging strategies, automated candidate pipelines, filtering to suppress imaging artefacts, and the triggered reprocessing tools that turn candidates into follow-up. The methods are designed to carry over to SKA-Mid and SKA-Low from AA* onward.
