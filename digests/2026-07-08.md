## High priority (≤5)

**Exploring Image-Text Alignment for Radio Galaxy Morphologies** · Erica Lastufka, Mariia Drozdova, Svyatoslav Volosynovskiy · [arXiv:2607.06305](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06305v1)
- Why it matters: A direct probe of whether general-purpose vision–language foundation models (SigLIP-2) transfer to survey morphology tasks — the kind of experiment that informs how AION-style multimodal astronomy backbones should treat text supervision.
- TL;DR: The authors hand-craft domain-specific captions for the MiraBest FR-I/FR-II radio galaxy dataset and score how well SigLIP-2 aligns those captions with the images, with and without LoRA fine-tuning. Caption-only classification matches image-only classification, and LoRA tightens local embedding structure but does not improve the global image–text alignment, suggesting text supervision can substitute for pixels on this morphology task but that off-the-shelf VLM alignment is the current bottleneck.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**Skipping the rungs! Calibrating distance indicators through their clustering with galaxies** · Madeline L. Cross-Parkin, Leonardo Giani, Cullan Howlett, Tamara M. Davis · [arXiv:2607.06567](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06567v1)
- Why it matters: Uses upcoming LSST × DESI cross-correlations as an independent handle on Hubble-tension systematics — a clustering-based check that sits squarely in the LSS/clustering theme.
- TL;DR: Fisher forecasts show angular cross-correlations between SN Ia and DESI-final galaxy samples can constrain a constant distance-calibration offset ΔM to ~0.05 and H₀ to ~1.8 km/s/Mpc, well below the ~0.13 offset that would be needed to explain the Hubble tension as a low-z calibration bias.

**One with HI: Modelling HI Intensity Mapping one-point statistics including systematics** · Bernhard Vos-Gines, Cora Uhlemann · [arXiv:2607.06526](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06526v1)
- Why it matters: A large-deviation-based PDF model for HI intensity maps that folds in foreground removal and beam effects — useful theory scaffolding for SKAO-era LSS analyses beyond the power spectrum.
- TL;DR: The paper builds an analytic PDF for HI in spherical cells from spherical collapse plus nonlinear bias and stochasticity, adds foreground and beam systematics validated against high-resolution sims, and shows the PDF adds non-Gaussian information that breaks the bias–amplitude degeneracy relative to the power spectrum.

**CMB Lensing Reconstruction Using Two Years of Temperature Data from the SPT-3G Summer Survey** · K. Levy, S. Raghunathan, F. Guidi, Y. Li et al. · [arXiv:2607.05784](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05784v1)
- Why it matters: A fresh wide-area CMB lensing reconstruction that will feed into cross-correlations with weak-lensing and galaxy surveys — relevant background for lensing-focused cosmology work.
- TL;DR: The SPT-3G collaboration reconstructs the CMB lensing potential over ~2640 deg² of the Summer survey from two years of temperature data and measures a lensing amplitude of 1.015 ± 0.053 relative to Planck 2018 ΛCDM over 50 < L < 2000, with the fields intended to combine with Main and Wide for a ~10 000 deg² SPT-3G footprint.

**A Face-on View of Interstellar Dust in the Galactic Plane** · Lin Zhang, Bingqiu Chen, Fei Qin, Guangxing Li et al. · [arXiv:2607.06016](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06016v1)
- Why it matters: A concrete CNN-on-survey-photometry pipeline — a U-Net inverts LOS extinction from red-clump-star photometry into 3D dust density, a template worth noting for anyone applying CNNs to survey imaging or building extinction priors.
