## High priority (≤5)

**The Subversive Role of Excessive External Shear in Concealing Lensing Anomalies** · Alfred, Singh, Lewis · [arXiv:2607.07709](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07709v1)
- Why it matters: Directly targets the lens-model degeneracy that governs whether flux-ratio anomalies in quadruply-lensed quasars can be attributed to dark matter substructure — a core ingredient of any ML-based substructure inference pipeline.
- TL;DR: The authors show that plugging in external shears far larger than what cosmic-shear surveys measure lets standard smooth lens models absorb position anomalies entirely and partially soak up flux-ratio anomalies in quad-lens NLRs. That inflation systematically hides the imprints of DM sub-halos, so inferences about DM particle mass/temperature drawn without disciplined shear priors are unreliable.

**Euclid Quick Data Release (Q1): Searching for radio-selected Euclid-dark galaxies in the EDF-N** · Euclid Collaboration · [arXiv:2607.07296](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07296v1)
- Why it matters: First look at the population that Euclid Q1 imaging alone misses — critical context for calibrating Euclid-driven ML detection and photometric pipelines, especially near the survey's faint limit.
- TL;DR: Starting from LOFAR 144 MHz sources with Spitzer/IRAC counterparts but no Euclid Q1 optical/NIR detection, the team isolates 88 isolated "Euclid-dark" galaxies and fits SEDs, finding photo-z from 0.4 to 5 with a dusty, high-mass population dominating the higher-z tail. Sub-arcsec radio imaging supports a mix of AGN and star-forming systems, and the sample suggests that Euclid's optical selection function drops sharply for heavily obscured or high-z sources.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**Beyond traditional emission-line diagnostics: using autoencoders to uncover AGN in DESI spectra** · Zaidouni et al. · [arXiv:2607.07329](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07329v1)
- TL;DR: A SPENDER autoencoder compresses 50k DESI spectra into a latent space where k-d-tree nearest-neighbour classification recovers AGN — including candidates missed by BPT-style line diagnostics — showing representation learning on survey spectra as a route to rare-object discovery.

**KAN-LSTM-Transformer Neural Networks, MFV and Cosmological Parameters** · Wei et al. · [arXiv:2607.06959](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06959v1)
- TL;DR: A hybrid Kolmogorov-Arnold + LSTM + Transformer network non-parametrically reconstructs the SN Ia distance modulus and, cross-checked against several ML baselines, is used to constrain cosmological parameters directly from Pantheon data.

**Constructing a Mock Galaxy Catalog for A-SPEC using a Machine-assisted Semi-Simulation Model** · Yao et al. · [arXiv:2607.06933](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06933v1)
- TL;DR: A model trained on IllustrisTNG predicts baryonic subhalo properties from DM-only features (R² up to 0.96 for stellar mass) and is applied to an N-body simulation to build a spectroscopic-survey mock — a template for ML-augmented mock generation for upcoming imaging/spectroscopic missions.

**Generalist Vision-Language Models for Fast Radio Burst detection: a zero-shot benchmark** · Zurlo et al. · [arXiv:2607.07382](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07382v1)
- TL;DR: Small locally-run VLMs are evaluated zero-shot on 2000 simulated L-band dynamic spectra against a specialized deep detector; the study probes how far off-the-shelf generalist multimodal models take us on structured astronomical image detection without retraining.

**Present Day Cosmic Acceleration from SDSS and DESI BAO** · Camarena, Marra · [arXiv:2607.07348](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07348v1)
- TL;DR: Reanalysing DESI DR2 vs SDSS BAO together with Planck and SNe Ia, the authors find the DESI-preferred present-day deceleration parameter lies on the decelerating side (q₀≈0.1) and argue the tension with SDSS traces to DESI's lowest-z bin — motivating finer tomographic slicing of the DESI Bright Galaxy Survey.

**Quantifying Environmental Effects on Galaxy Properties using Non-spherical Voids from SDSS DR7** · Duan et al. · [arXiv:2607.07268](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07268v1)
- TL;DR: Voronoi-tessellation + watershed void catalogs from SDSS DR7 are used to classify void vs non-void galaxies and quantify how colour, sSFR and quiescent fractions depend on the low-density LSS environment across stellar-mass bins.

**Prospect for Detection of Strongly Lensed Multi-messenger Signals of BNS Mergers** · Chen et al. · [arXiv:2607.07120](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07120v1)
- TL;DR: Combines lensed GW rates for Cosmic Explorer / Einstein Telescope with a pointed EM follow-up strategy on known galaxy-scale lens candidates to estimate detection rates for lensed kilonovae, afterglows and sGRBs — a lensing-driven multi-messenger probe of gravity and cosmology.
