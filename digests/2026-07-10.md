## High priority (≤5)

**Disentangling the dark and stellar mass through precise lens modelling of the JWST observation of lensed quasar WFI2033--4723** · Li, Collett, Krawczyk et al. · [arXiv:2607.08576](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08576v1)
- Why it matters: A concrete recipe for using JWST-quality imaging plus time delays to break the stellar/dark-matter degeneracy in a well-known quad — exactly the modelling regime any ML lens-inference pipeline has to reproduce before it can be trusted on Euclid-scale samples.
- TL;DR: The authors fit WFI2033--4723 with a composite model that decomposes the lens into a multi-Gaussian stellar component (with a free M/L gradient) plus a gNFW halo, anchored to JWST/NIRCam imaging and measured time delays. External cosmological priors (Planck+DESI and Pantheon+SH0ES) pin down the mass-sheet transformation that would otherwise wreck the decomposition, letting them recover distinct stellar and dark-matter mass distributions rather than a single power-law total.

**XShooter DESI Lens Program: Sample characterization** · Jullo, Boghossian, Chapel et al. · [arXiv:2607.08562](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08562v1)
- Why it matters: Directly follows up ML-detected strong-lens candidates from DESI Legacy Imaging (the Huang 2021 and Storfer 2022 deep-ResNet catalogs) with VLT spectroscopy — a rare check on the redshift and source-property biases of neural-network lens finders that upcoming Euclid/SPHEREx searches will inherit.
- TL;DR: The team observed 67 candidate lens systems with VLT/XShooter and measured spectroscopic redshifts for 58 lenses and 57 sources, finding no systematic redshift bias tied to the neural-network selection. They also flag a handful of sources with outflows or rotating [OII]/Hα disks, giving a first look at the source population that DESI-based ML lens searches are pulling out.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**The Three Hundred Project: validating H₀ inference from mock X-ray and millimetre analyses of galaxy clusters** · De Luca, Bourdin, Mazzotta et al. · [arXiv:2607.08613](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08613v1)
- TL;DR: Using a large cluster sample from The Three Hundred hydrodynamical simulations, the authors quantify how X-ray-only versus joint X-ray+mm ICM temperature reconstructions bias cluster-based H₀ estimates and show that morphology-informed priors let joint temperature profiles recover cosmological parameters with well-controlled systematics.

**Beyond ΛCDM with the SKA Observatory — II: Unveiling the Secrets of the Early Universe** · Fonseca, Bahr-Kalus, Ballardini et al. · [arXiv:2607.08593](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08593v1)
- TL;DR: A review of what SKAO large-scale-structure surveys (biased tracers on the largest scales, plus beyond-2-point statistics) will add to constraints on the inflationary power spectrum, primordial non-Gaussianity, and cross-survey synergies for probing the early Universe.

**HETDEX [OII] galaxies at z ≤ 0.48: Volume-limited samples and their power spectra** · Moon, Komatsu, Ciardullo et al. · [arXiv:2607.08453](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08453v1)
- TL;DR: The HETDEX PDR1 [OII] emitter catalog is sliced into volume-limited samples across three luminosity bins with number densities 5–10× typical spectroscopic ELG surveys, and the resulting monopole/quadrupole power spectra agree with Uchuu ΛCDM mocks — providing a new low-z clustering baseline.

**Lambda as a Probe of Lensing Consistency** · Shi, Cui, Giocoli · [arXiv:2607.08286](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08286v1)
- TL;DR: Turns the weak-lensing mass-sheet degeneracy from bug into feature: a spatially resolved parameter λ(r) tracks where strong- and weak-lensing reconstructions agree in clusters, giving a data-driven criterion for combining the two probes and testing joint mass models.

**Robust Heteroskedastic Matrix Factorization: A Generalization of PCA that Flags Outliers and Handles Missing Data** · Hilder, Hogg, Casey, Rix · [arXiv:2607.08081](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08081v1)
- TL;DR: RHMF (with a fast JAX implementation, Robusta-HMF) extends PCA with per-feature uncertainties, missing-data handling, and per-object/feature outlier flagging via an iterative reweighting equivalent to a Student-t likelihood — directly useful for building clean embeddings and anomaly catalogs from noisy survey data.

**Dimensionality Reduction Meets Network Science: Sensemaking on UMAP's kNN Graph** · Chau, Ren, Hohman, Moritz · [arXiv:2607.08746](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08746v1)
- TL;DR: Instead of only using UMAP's 2D projection, the authors run PageRank, k-core decomposition, and clustering-coefficient analysis on UMAP's internal high-dimensional kNN graph to surface representative points, dense cores, and tight neighborhoods — a lightweight recipe transferable to representation-learning workflows on astronomical embeddings.
