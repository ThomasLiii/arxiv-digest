## Medium priority (≤8)

**Can third- and fourth-order multipoles plus radial variation of iso-density ellipses explain the observed flux ratios in B1422+231? YES, and a lesson learned from a TNG100 lensing galaxy sample** · Feng, Xu, Sluse et al. · [arXiv:2607.11559](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11559v1)
- TL;DR: Extracting smooth (non-clumpy) $m_3$, $m_4$ multipoles and radial variations of ellipticity from a TNG100 strong-lens galaxy sample is enough to reproduce the flux-ratio and astrometric anomalies of B1422+231 without invoking dark substructure — a direct warning about the mass-model degeneracies any ML lens-modelling pipeline has to grapple with.

**Two Earliest Optical-UV Tidal Disruption Events Hidden in the SDSS DR7 Catalog Unveiled by the Transformer-Based Spectrum Classifier** · Zheng, Lin, Kong et al. · [arXiv:2607.11539](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11539v1)
- TL;DR: A PCA-augmented Transformer classifier trained on optical TDE spectra (precision 0.88, recall 0.99) is run over the SDSS DR7 spectral catalog and pulls out two previously unrecognised TDEs — including one predating the earliest confirmed optical-UV event — a nice proof-of-concept for transformer-based rare-object discovery in existing survey archives.

**Cosmology from Nx2pt Analyses of SKAO Wide-Area Surveys** · Harrison, Sakr, Piccirilli et al. · [arXiv:2607.11254](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11254v1)
- TL;DR: Forecasts the joint constraining power of galaxy clustering, cosmic shear, galaxy–galaxy lensing and 21 cm intensity mapping from SKA-Mid AA4, showing $\sim$1% precision on $\Lambda$CDM parameters plus useful handles on $w_0 w_a$, $\sum m_\nu$ and $\Omega_k$ from an $N\times2$pt combination.

**The Environmental Dependence of Halo Intrinsic Alignments: Stronger Signals in Underdense Regions** · Ichikawa, Shi, Kurita et al. · [arXiv:2607.11114](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11114v1)
- TL;DR: Using the Dark Quest $N$-body suite, haloes in underdense environments show intrinsic-alignment amplitudes 1.5–1.8× larger than mass-matched haloes in overdense regions, driven by both greater ellipticity and stronger tidal alignment — a systematic that upcoming weak-lensing analyses (and IA models used at the two-point and beyond) need to absorb.

**Direct Primary Beam Correction: Untangling Mutual Coupling in 21-cm Cosmological Experiments** · O'Hara, Gueuning, de Lera Acedo et al. · [arXiv:2607.11438](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11438v1)
- TL;DR: A regularised linear-inversion framework reconstructs SKA-Low far-field beams (including mutual-coupling structure) from stacked Jones matrices, but simulated EoR observations show that correcting only the main-lobe and near-sidelobes still leaves chromatic grating-lobe contamination that does not average down across snapshots.

**Constraints on Ultra-Light Axions from the DESI DR1 Full Shape, Planck and ACT** · Verdiani, Castorina, Salvioni et al. · [arXiv:2607.11876](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11876v1)
- TL;DR: DESI DR1 full-shape galaxy power spectra combined with Planck+ACT tighten ULA-as-dark-matter bounds by more than $2\times$ at $m_a\sim 10^{-32}$–$10^{-24}$ eV, restricting $m_a\sim 10^{-29}$ eV ULAs to <0.3% of the matter density.

**Breaking the Dark Sector Degeneracy with Nonparametric Expansion–Growth Reconstruction** · You, Cai, Yang · [arXiv:2607.11813](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11813v1)
- TL;DR: A Gaussian-process reconstruction of both the dark-energy equation of state and a dark-sector coupling is jointly fit to Pantheon+, CC, DESI DR2 BAO and RSD data, finding no significant departure from $\Lambda$CDM and providing a model-independent expansion–growth consistency test.
