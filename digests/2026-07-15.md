## High priority

**Demystifying image-recovery from radio interferometers: toward a multiscale predictive model** · Dan Miao, Guang-Xing Li · [arXiv:2607.12396v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12396v1)
- Why it matters: An analytical image-domain model of interferometric spatial filtering — directly relevant to self-calibration/mosaicking and to quantifying how much diffuse flux is lost in survey imaging.
- TL;DR: The authors introduce a "Constrained Diffusion Decomposition" that splits an image into continuous scale-space components and show that an interferometer's per-scale flux recovery follows a simple one-parameter error function. Combining the two lets them predict what an interferometer would see from a true sky map directly in the image plane, without running visibility simulations, and they validate this on simulated ALMA observations of the Perseus cloud.

## Medium priority

**Model-Independent Indication for a Localized Anomaly in the Late-Time Expansion History** · Shibendu Gupta Choudhury, Purba Mukherjee, Eleonora Di Valentino, Anjan A Sen · [arXiv:2607.13009v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13009v1)
- A spline reconstruction of distances from DESI DR2 BAO and DES-Dovekie supernovae shows a localized ~3.5σ departure from Planck ΛCDM near z≈0.47 that survives methodology and calibration variations.

**Overview: Cosmology with the SKAO** · Marta Spinelli, Ian Harrison, Richard A. Battye et al. · [arXiv:2607.12887v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12887v1)
- A synthesis of what SKAO HI intensity mapping and continuum galaxy surveys, combined with weak lensing and other probes, will deliver for large-scale-structure cosmology and ΛCDM tests.

**Exploring Hu-Sawicki-like modified gravity with Genetic Algorithms** · Chiara De Leo, Elisa Fazzari, Matteo Martinelli, Savvas Nesseris · [arXiv:2607.12745v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12745v1)
- Uses genetic-algorithm reconstruction of viable f(R) perturbations against DESI DR2 BAO and Pantheon+ and finds only mild allowed deviations from ΛCDM.

**Ricci Focusing Degeneracy between Dynamical Dark Energy and Matter Inhomogeneity** · I. A. Moiseev, O. S. Sazhina · [arXiv:2607.12424v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12424v1)
- Shows the ZKDR light-propagation parameter can be re-interpreted as either dynamical dark energy or weak-lensing inhomogeneity and proposes an isotropy-based test to break the degeneracy.

**Testing the Direction of Dark Sector Interaction Using Late-Time Datasets** · Anil Kandel, Phongpichit Channuie, Ertan Güdekli, Farruh Atamurotov · [arXiv:2607.12283v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12283v1)
- MCMC fits of an interacting dark-energy model to DESI DR1/DR2 BAO plus multiple SN Ia compilations weakly favor energy transfer from dark energy to dark matter but Bayesian evidence still prefers ΛCDM.

**Environmental Imprints on the Assembly of the Cool Gas around Bright Cluster Galaxies** · Runyu Zhu, Hu Zou, Taotao Fang et al. · [arXiv:2607.12361v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12361v1)
- Stacks Mg II absorption from over a million DESI background quasars around BCGs at z≈0.55 and finds cool gas is suppressed inside 200 kpc but enhanced from 200 kpc–10 Mpc relative to field galaxies.
