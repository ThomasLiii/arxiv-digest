## High priority

**Probabilistic redshift estimation of unresolved galaxies from multi-band background light maps** · Shun-Sheng Li, Hendrik Hildebrandt, Ludovic Van Waerbeke · [arXiv:2607.14525v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14525v1)
- Why it matters: Directly targets Euclid+Rubin-LSST synergy with a deep-learning framework for photo-z of unresolved sources, hitting the Euclid/SPHEREx priority and touching photometric redshifts and deblending in one go.
- TL;DR: The authors train conditional normalising flows on realistic multi-band image simulations to infer the flux-weighted redshift distribution of galaxies too faint to resolve individually, treating the diffuse background light itself as the observable. They report sub-per-cent accuracy on the mean and standard deviation for Euclid+LSST-quality imaging and show the network stays robust to missing bands when paired with data imputation. A resampling scheme is introduced to handle mismatches between simulated training sets and target survey populations, positioning the method as a practical tool for extracting cosmological information from the cosmic optical background.

## Medium priority

**The VST ATLAS Survey — IV: Galaxy, LRG and QSO bias and HODs via ACT CMB Lensing** · Harshnoor Kaur, T. Shanks, S. Bose, A. C. Edge · [arXiv:2607.14904v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14904v1)
- Why it matters: Combines wide-field galaxy/LRG/QSO clustering with high-resolution ACT DR6 CMB lensing to constrain bias, HODs, and σ8 across redshift — squarely in the LSS/weak-lensing lane.
- TL;DR: Cross-correlations with ACT lensing yield ΛCDM-consistent bias for galaxies and LRGs but hint at a lower σ8 and faster growth rate from QSOs, with HOD fits used to interpret non-linear scales.

**CAMB v2: cosmological power spectra for high-precision surveys** · Antony Lewis · [arXiv:2607.14854v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14854v1)
- Why it matters: A foundational modelling tool underlying essentially every CMB and LSS analysis, updated specifically for the precision demands of upcoming surveys.
- TL;DR: The update introduces an Olver-based treatment of hyperspherical Bessel functions and other numerical refinements to deliver fast, high-accuracy lensed CMB and matter power spectra with numerical error well below survey uncertainties.

**Analytical covariances for catalogue-based pseudo-Cℓs** · Kevin Wolz, Elyas Farah, Robert Reischke, David Alonso, Andrina Nicola · [arXiv:2607.14843v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14843v1)
- Why it matters: Catalogue-based angular power spectrum estimators sidestep pixelation effects that matter for cosmic shear and galaxy clustering at Rubin/Euclid precision, and this addresses their covariance directly.
- TL;DR: The authors derive analytic disconnected covariances for catalogue pseudo-Cℓ estimators using a Narrow-Kernel Approximation, correctly handling area overlaps between samples and shot-noise-like self-pair contributions.

**Enabling Cosmic Web Analysis at Gigaparsec Scales: A Multi Block Approach for DisPerSE** · Ankit Singh, Frazer Pearce, Meghan Gray, Gustavo Yepes · [arXiv:2607.14785v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14785v1)
- Why it matters: Filament reconstructions at gigaparsec volumes are a prerequisite for LSS-scale cosmology and environmental studies with next-generation surveys.
- TL;DR: A frozen-core block-decomposition scheme lets DisPerSE run on volumes far larger than the Delaunay tessellation memory limit while preserving the global topology of the recovered filament network.
