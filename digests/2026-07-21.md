## High priority

**Comparing explicit likelihood and likelihood-free simulation-based inference for weak lensing cosmic shear** · Simone Vinciguerra, Nicolas Martinet, Marco Gatti · [arXiv:2607.17942v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17942v1)
- Why it matters: Head-to-head test of SBI vs Gaussian-likelihood analysis on Euclid-like weak-lensing mocks that also benchmarks a map-level CNN summary, hitting both the Euclid and weak-lensing priorities.
- TL;DR: Using non-tomographic Euclid-like Gaussian random field mocks, the authors compare explicit-likelihood inference (emulator + covariance) against neural density estimators for shear two-point statistics and a CNN map-level summary, focusing on Ωm and S8. They apply LFI-style posterior calibration diagnostics (TARP) and show that the explicit-likelihood approach becomes strongly miscalibrated when the emulator is inaccurate or the likelihood is non-Gaussian, while the neural-density LFI stays well-calibrated. The compression scheme materially affects the classical analysis while barely touching LFI, and only ~30% residual disagreement remains between LFI and the CNN probe.

**Co-addition and Subtraction of Undersampled Images** · Matan Schlanger, Barak Zackay · [arXiv:2607.18054v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18054v1)
- Why it matters: A statistically optimal replacement for both coaddition and image subtraction in undersampled surveys — a direct hit on the image-mosaicking / self-calibration priority and immediately applicable to ZTF/Rubin-style transient pipelines.
- TL;DR: The paper introduces LUTRA, which performs coaddition and difference imaging on undersampled data in a way the authors prove is optimal under aliasing, in contrast to the ad-hoc treatments in current pipelines. The method delivers super-resolved outputs on which photometry and astrometry can be measured directly, and demonstrates a 1.25× SNR gain on public ZTF data with an open-source Python implementation. This translates into higher-fidelity transient detection (supernovae, GW optical counterparts) for undersampled surveys.

**Comparing the Near-infrared Spectral Energy Distributions from Different Stellar Population Synthesis Models with SPHEREx Observations** · Jeong Hwan Lee, Minjin Kim, Woong-Seob Jeong, et al. · [arXiv:2607.18048v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18048v1)
- Why it matters: A first, direct confrontation of leading SPS libraries with SPHEREx spectrophotometry across 0.7–5 µm — a mission-priority paper that quantifies systematic biases users of SPHEREx data will need to know about.
- TL;DR: The authors match SPHEREx spectrophotometry of ~3,900 SDSS compact galaxies against NIR SEDs predicted by full-spectrum fits to SDSS optical data using E-MILES, BC03, CB19, and FSPS. All four SPS models over-predict the stellar continuum by 0.1–0.3 mag between 2.4 and 5 µm, most dramatically for 1–5 Gyr populations, pointing at systematic issues around TP-AGB stars and cool-star molecular absorption. E-MILES fares slightly better beyond ~3.8 µm at older ages, and emission-line galaxies additionally show ~0.5 mag deficits attributed to non-stellar NIR emission.

## Medium priority

**Feature-driven anomaly flagging in obscured active galactic nucleus light curves with autoencoders** · Natale De Bonis, Demetra De Cicco, Stefano Cavuoti, et al. · [arXiv:2607.17728v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17728v1)
- Why it matters: Unsupervised anomaly detection on survey time-series data with SHAP-based interpretability — squarely in the rare-object/anomaly-detection lane for next-gen surveys.

**Breaking the fNL–bφ degeneracy with the time evolution of tracer number counts** · Caio B. de S. Nascimento, Neal Dalal · [arXiv:2607.18229v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18229v1)
- Why it matters: Extends the time-evolution estimator of the PNG bias parameter to realistic photometric selections using IllustrisTNG/SIMBA CAMELS, making scale-dependent bias measurements of fNL more tractable for actual LSS surveys.

**Cosmological consequences of a dynamical dark matter in the light of DESI DR2 measurements** · Abhijith Ajith, Utkarsh Kumar · [arXiv:2607.18234v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18234v1)
- Why it matters: Reinterprets the DESI DR2 dark-energy hint as a dynamical dark matter EoS, with observational LSS consequences (lower σ8/S8, better weak-lensing agreement) worth tracking.

**Simultaneous inference of isotropic and anisotropic polarization rotation angles for next-generation cosmic microwave background experiments** · Yue Zhang, Chang Feng, Sen Li, Filipe B. Abdalla · [arXiv:2607.17500v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17500v1)
- Why it matters: Bayesian framework that jointly handles cosmic birefringence, miscalibration, and higher-order foreground fluctuations for upcoming CMB polarization data.

**Intracluster light and dark matter halo shapes reflect common assembly, not mutual coupling: shape correspondence in CDM but not SIDM** · G. Martin, N. A. Hatch, W. Cui, et al. · [arXiv:2607.17792v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17792v1)
- Why it matters: Tests whether ICL genuinely traces dark matter halo shape across CDM vs SIDM in TheThreeHundred simulations, refining a widely used empirical proxy for cluster-scale weak-lensing work.
