## High priority

**Euclid: Inferring star-formation history via cross-correlations of photometric clustering and shear with the cosmic infrared background** · J. Han, S. Camera, M. Migliaccio, et al. · [arXiv:2607.19290v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19290v1)
- Why it matters: Euclid mission-priority forecast that combines photometric clustering, cosmic shear, and CIB cross-correlations to reconstruct star-formation history — hits Euclid, weak-lensing, and LSS priorities in one paper.
- TL;DR: The authors perform a tomographic Fisher forecast that cross-correlates simulated Euclid photometric galaxy clustering and weak lensing with Planck CIB maps, using an HOD-based template to measure the bias-weighted star-formation-rate density $\langle b\rho_{\rm SFRD}\rangle$ in redshift bins. Compared with prior CIB-only work on the same data, adding Euclid roughly halves the marginalized $\langle b\rho_{\rm SFRD}\rangle$ uncertainties across all bins and extends the accessible redshift range. The joint analysis also tightens HOD parameters by about 2.5× and breaks degeneracies inaccessible to either probe alone.

- Why it matters: A validated semi-analytic HMF for decaying dark matter with a transparent single-parameter fit, useful for placing DDM constraints from upcoming halo-abundance and LSS probes.

**NAPTIME: A Neural-Process Framework for Rubin Alert Classification** · Nicholas Earl, Siddharth Chaini, K. Decker French, et al. · [arXiv:2607.19236v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19236v1)
- Why it matters: Neural-process photometric transient classifier for Rubin/LSST alerts that leans on host-galaxy context and photo-z, with strong TDE ranking — squarely in the rare-object discovery lane.

**Enhanced Astrometry of the Rapid ASKAP Continuum Survey: Mid and High Frequency Epochs** · Akhil Jaini, Adam T. Deller, Yuanming Wang, et al. · [arXiv:2607.18775v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18775v1)
- Why it matters: Hierarchical crossmatch-based self-correction that drives RACS positional residuals below ~0.18″ over most of the southern sky — a survey self-calibration recipe relevant to multi-wavelength association and transient localization.
