## Medium priority

**Microlensing Detection and Inference via Learned Bayes Factors** · Nolan Smyth, Laurence Perreault-Levasseur, Yashar Hezaveh · [arXiv:2607.20260v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20260v1)
- Why it matters: ML pipeline for rare-object discovery in a wide-field survey — learned Bayes factors + transformer encoder on Roman photometry — with big gains exactly in the finite-source, free-floating-planet regime that hard cuts miss.
- TL;DR: The authors replace hard photometric cuts with an Evidence Network that outputs calibrated Bayes factors, coupled with Neural Posterior Estimation, sharing a transformer encoder over irregularly sampled Roman light curves. On simulated Roman data (without astrophysical confounders) they report 99.9% detection efficiency at a false-positive rate below 6×10⁻⁴ and lift detection of extreme finite-source events (ρ ≳ 5) from ~65% to ~95%.

**From Dark Matter to Galaxies: Halo-Free Mock Generation via Conditional Point-Cloud Diffusion** · Kana Moriwaki, Ken Osato, Naoki Yoshida · [arXiv:2607.19808v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19808v1)
- Why it matters: Diffusion-based generative model that emits galaxy point clouds (positions + SFR + stellar mass) conditioned on a coarse dark-matter density field — a halo-free alternative to HOD/SHAM for LSS mock production.

**LMC-induced Perturbations in the Milky Way Halo II: Bridging Field-level Inference and Summary-level Simulation-Based Inference** · Yanjun Sheng, Yuan-Sen Ting, Tomasz Różański, et al. · [arXiv:2607.20006v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20006v1)
- Why it matters: Uses a Conditional Flow Matching field-level likelihood as a "ground-truth" benchmark to quantify what basis-function-expansion summary statistics discard, tightening MW and LMC mass constraints by up to ~10× — a rare direct comparison of field-level vs summary-level SBI on halo tracers.

**Identifying Kilonovae in the Presence of Optical Afterglow for the Wide-Field Survey Telescope** · Huan Yang, Zhengyan Liu, Liang-Duan Liu, et al. · [arXiv:2607.20233v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20233v1)
- Why it matters: Fisher-matrix study of how well a real optical time-domain survey (WFST) can pull kilonova signals out of afterglow-dominated composite transients — informs cadence and filter strategy for rare-object discovery in kilonova follow-up.

**Modeling the HI-Halo Connection: Evolution, Scatter, and a Halo-based Prescription for 21-cm Mock Catalogs** · Mohd Kamran, Gabriella De Lucia, Marta Spinelli, et al. · [arXiv:2607.20123v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20123v1)
- Why it matters: A GAEA-calibrated HI–halo mass prescription with quantified scatter driven by halo assembly, aimed at populating N-body halos for 21-cm intensity-mapping mock catalogs relevant to post-reionization LSS analyses.
