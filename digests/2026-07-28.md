## Medium priority

**The Nodal Line of the Galaxy Correlation Function as a Geometric Cosmological Probe** · Lado Samushia · [arXiv:2607.24694](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24694v1)
- Why it matters: A fresh LSS observable applied to DESI DR1 that is nearly independent of BAO and gives a sound-horizon-free $H_0$ constraint.
- TL;DR: The author defines the sign-change locus of the anisotropic 2-point correlation function as a purely geometric probe. On DESI DR1 it pins $\Omega_m h^{3/2}$ to 3% (4.8% under a 10% bias prior), and combined with BAO yields a 4.45% constraint on $H_0$.

**Updated 1.1% Precision Values of the Hubble Constant with Corrected Pantheon+ and DES-DOVEKIE Type Ia Supernovae** · Shouvik Roy Choudhury · [arXiv:2607.24443](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24443v1)
- Why it matters: First DES-DOVEKIE distance-ladder $H_0$ measurement, directly relevant to DES-side cosmology and the Hubble tension.
- TL;DR: Applying a revised host-mass correction to Pantheon+ (114 of 1701 SNe affected) yields $H_0 = 73.264 \pm 0.806$ km/s/Mpc, still ~6.85σ from Planck. Swapping in DES-DOVEKIE with a fixed offset gives a consistent 73.267 ± 0.828. Neither correction resolves the tension.

**Measuring Cosmic Neutrino Masses Independently of Dark Energy** · Frank J. Qu, Fei Ge, Hanyue Wang, Emmanuel Schaan et al. · [arXiv:2607.24742](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24742v1)
- Why it matters: Uses Planck CMB + CMB lensing + DESI DR2 BAO, and forecasts for Simons Observatory / Spec-S5 — squarely in the observational LSS/CMB cosmology bucket.
- TL;DR: The authors build two dark-energy-robust routes to $\sum m_\nu$: a marginalization scheme over flexible $w(a)$ (bound: $\sum m_\nu < 0.152$ eV, tightening to 0.043 eV with SO+Spec-S5) and a late-Universe-free route using primary CMB + $C_L^{\kappa\kappa}$ with $A_{\rm lens}$ marginalization (0.41 eV today, 0.28 eV at cosmic-variance limit). Both trade statistical power for model independence.

**Constraining the $z \approx 1$ neutral hydrogen (HI) distribution** · Minal Chhabra, Raghunath Ghara, Somnath Bharadwaj · [arXiv:2607.24412](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24412v1)
- Why it matters: 21-cm cosmology combining CHIME power spectrum and uGMRT stacking — relevant to LSS/HI-based cosmology on next-gen surveys.
- TL;DR: A joint Bayesian fit of an HI-halo-mass relation to uGMRT $\Omega_{\rm HI}$ and CHIME 21-cm autopower yields the $z \approx 1$ HI mass function, which flattens below ~$3.6 \times 10^9\,M_\odot$ and predicts more high-mass HI galaxies than prior observations or hydrodynamical simulations.

**Evidence for Azimuthally Anisotropic MgII Absorption around DESI Luminous Red Galaxies** · Xuanyi Wu, Cheng Li · [arXiv:2607.24071](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24071v1)
- Why it matters: A statistical stacking measurement on DESI DR1 spectra — the kind of large-survey CGM/clustering analysis close to my radar.
- TL;DR: Forced MgII EW measurements on every LRG-quasar pair, with a random-control subtraction, reveal a stronger inner absorption at 0.4<z<0.75 and a ~2.5σ major-axis enhancement over the minor axis at 30-80 kpc — showing azimuthal CGM structure exists around massive quiescent halos, not only star-forming ones.

**Testing Statistical Isotropy on the Sphere with Minkowski Tensors** · Stephen Appleby, Changbom Park · [arXiv:2607.23973](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23973v1)
- Why it matters: Morphological statistics for CMB/full-sky maps; a genuine methodology contribution for large-scale isotropy tests.
- TL;DR: The authors show existing MT estimators on $S^2$ either break covariance or self-isotropize, then build correlation functions of per-component orientations that stay covariant. On simulated dipole-modulated and sheared fields, the new statistics cleanly separate coherent-alignment models from isotropic backgrounds.
