## High priority (≤5)

**Probing Parity Violation with Weak Lensing Trispectrum** · Sijin Chen, Zhengyangguang Gong, Jiamin Hou · [arXiv:2607.26046](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26046v1)
- Why it matters: A weak-lensing methodology paper that forecasts a parity-violating signal for DES Y3 and LSST Y10 source distributions, directly relevant to the survey-cosmology stack that Euclid also lives in.
- TL;DR: The authors derive a compact projection formalism for the reduced angular convergence trispectrum and use it to compute signal-to-noise for two parity-odd primordial templates (squeezed and collapsed). They forecast detectability with realistic tomographic redshift distributions and show that the Limber approximation is inadequate for the parity-odd piece, so exact line-of-sight integration is needed to preserve the signal.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**Weighted Webs: Morphology-Informed Marked Fields** · Mikel Martin Barandiaran, Jessica A. Cowell, David Alonso et al. · [arXiv:2607.26021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26021v1)
- Why it matters: Explores whether cosmic-web morphology (tidal shear, filamentarity, local fractal dimension) improves cosmological constraints beyond standard marked power spectra — relevant to LSS analyses of upcoming surveys.
- TL;DR: Density-based marks still win on their own, but combining morphology- and density-based marks tightens joint constraints on Ωm, σ8, and Mν beyond either class alone.

**Anisotropic Secondary Bias of Dark Matter Haloes in a ΛCDM Universe** · Qinglin Ma, Cheng Li · [arXiv:2607.26009](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26009v1)
- Why it matters: Extends halo secondary bias into a directional quantity, which matters for anisotropic clustering estimators used in weak-lensing/LSS pipelines.
- TL;DR: Using TNG300-Dark, they find anisotropic secondary bias is weak for concentration and formation time but strong for spin and axis ratio, driven mainly by halo–filament alignment rather than tidal anisotropy.

**Simulation-based tension quantification of the cosmic dipole** · Mali Land-Strykowski, Harry T. J. Bevins, Oliver T. Oayda, Geraint F. Lewis · [arXiv:2607.25703](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25703v1)
- Why it matters: A neural-ratio-estimator SBI framework applied to a live cosmological anomaly and explicitly pitched at LSST, Euclid and SKA — the kind of methodology I care about for large-survey inference.
- TL;DR: They train an NRE ensemble to compute Bayesian evidence ratios and validate against nested sampling, then measure ~5.7σ tension between CatWISE and Planck (~6.7σ after modelling Eddington bias).

**CNN-Based Reanalysis of Optical Turbulence at the Canary Islands Observatories (OCAN)** · Airam Salas, Begoña García-Lorenzo, Julio A. Castro-Almazán, Gelys Trancho · [arXiv:2607.25940](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25940v1)
- Why it matters: Uses frozen pretrained CNNs as feature extractors on observatory data and clusters the resulting embeddings — a light version of the representation-learning-on-astronomical-data pattern.
- TL;DR: They convert ~300 nights of SCIDAR Cn²(h) profiles into temporal-vertical heatmaps, embed them with pretrained CNNs, and cluster the embeddings unsupervised to reveal recurring turbulence regimes at Roque de los Muchachos and Teide.

**Can Deep Generative Models Reproduce Non-Stationary Gaussian Random Fields?** · Daniel Kua, Yan Song · [arXiv:2607.25929](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25929v1)
- Why it matters: A controlled benchmark of DDPM, score-SDE, flow-matching and VAE on a known non-stationary GRF — directly relevant to any use of diffusion/flow models as emulators for cosmological fields.
- TL;DR: All four families recover the mean, but only DDPM and score-SDE reproduce the covariance structure well; flow matching under-disperses and VAE fails on covariance.
