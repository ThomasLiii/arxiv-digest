## High priority (≤5)

**Shear-kSZ: A New Estimator for the Matter-Electron Power Spectrum from kSZ Tomography and Weak Lensing** · Boryana Hadzhiyska · [arXiv:2607.27149](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27149v1)
- Why it matters: A new weak-lensing × kSZ estimator explicitly forecast for LSST and Euclid Stage-IV cosmic shear analyses, addressing baryonic suppression — one of the dominant systematics I care about.
- TL;DR: The paper introduces a triple correlator of the kSZ field, reconstructed line-of-sight velocity, and lensing convergence that factorizes into a calibratable velocity kernel times the matter–electron cross-power. Its parity structure cancels CMB foregrounds, and mock analyses with SO-like CMB, DESI-like LRG velocities, and LSST-like source samples forecast a ~16σ measurement over f_sky = 0.2, translating to a percent-level constraint on the baryonic suppression S(k).

## Medium priority (≤8)

**Searching for signatures of inflationary massive fields in DESI Imaging data and Stage-V galaxy surveys** · Walter Riquelme, Santiago Avila, Lucas Pinol et al. · [arXiv:2607.27175](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27175v1)
- Why it matters: Uses DESI imaging angular clustering to constrain beyond-local PNG and forecasts Stage-V surveys — relevant to LSS analyses I follow.
- TL;DR: Systematics-driven hint of f_NL,Δ ~ 5×10³ at Δ ≈ 0.9 is not robust; Stage-V Lyman-break galaxy forecasts reach σ(Δ) ≈ 0.17–0.50 at fiducial f_NL,Δ = 4.

**Constraining the shape of dark matter haloes using only starlight II** · Jorge Sanchez Almeida, Angel R. Plastino, Sergio Guerra Arencibia et al. · [arXiv:2607.27001](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27001v1)
- Why it matters: A purely photometric diagnostic (EIM-tool) designed for large upcoming photometric surveys — fits my interest in extracting physics from imaging alone.
- TL;DR: Validation on globular clusters, dSphs, and EDGE/FIRE simulations shows the Eddington-inversion method reliably rejects NFW cusps when true DM is cored, recovering expected core radii.

**An efficient one-loop EFTofLSS framework for Vainshtein-screened Horndeski gravity** · Stan Verhoeve, Dani de Boe, Gen Ye et al. · [arXiv:2607.26945](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26945v1)
- Why it matters: An LSS analysis pipeline (PyBird extension) applied to modified gravity with Planck+BOSS+DESI DR2 — relevant to full-shape LSS methodology.
- TL;DR: The framework replaces Green's function evaluations with direct ODEs, is validated against N-body sims, and full-shape data tighten constraints on α_i-parametrized modifications.

**J-PAS & FLAMINGO: Cosmic voids and void galaxies in the gravitational landscape of photometric surveys** · J. A. Mansour, B. McCarthy, L. J. Liivamägi et al. · [arXiv:2607.26932](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26932v1)
- Why it matters: Void identification in the presence of realistic photo-z errors is directly relevant to how Euclid-like photometric surveys extract LSS.
- TL;DR: Using a quasi-potential field with a watershed algorithm on J-PAS mocks recovers dynamically dominant voids despite photo-z scatter and preserves the environmental trends of void galaxies.

**Optimizing the extraction of information from redshift probability distribution functions** · Rodrigo Duarte, Valerio Marra · [arXiv:2607.26822](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26822v1)
- Why it matters: An ML framework (turboPDZ) that optimizes photo-z point estimates and reliability scores from HSC-SSP PDZs — directly on-topic for photometric redshifts.
- TL;DR: PDZ compression via PCA plus a two-network MLP pipeline outperforms catalog z_best in σ_NMAD and outlier fraction across three HSC pipelines and improves reliability filtering, particularly for Mizuki's template-fitting pipeline.

**Probing Primordial Chirality in the Matter Distribution** · Fang-Na Shao, Hao-Ran Yu, Ming-Jie Sheng et al. · [arXiv:2607.26612](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26612v1)
- Why it matters: A helicity-based estimator for parity violation in reconstructed galaxy density fields — the observational LSS side of an anomaly I want to track.
- TL;DR: Simulations show handedness survives nonlinear structure formation; applied to SDSS DR7 the estimator sees only mild, statistically insignificant deviations.

**An Enhanced Catalog of Gaia DR3 Galaxy Candidates with Spectroscopic and ML Photometric Redshifts** · Junghyun Hwang, Ho Seong Hwang · [arXiv:2607.26558](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26558v1)
- Why it matters: A large all-sky extragalactic catalog with ML-based photo-z built from Gaia + 2MASS + unWISE — fits my interest in ML photo-z on survey imaging.
- TL;DR: An MBRNN photo-z model trained on cross-matched spectroscopic samples improves over Gaia-only photo-z; the catalog also provides outlier scores and is aimed at extragalactic and cosmological uses.
