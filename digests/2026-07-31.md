## High priority

**Multi-branch classification of diffuse cluster radio emission** · Markus Bredberg, Emma Tolley · [arXiv:2607.28349](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28349)
- Why it matters: Directly explores ML architectures (scattering transforms, squeeze-excitation attention, multi-branch CNNs) applied to survey imaging (LoTSS-DR2), with an eye toward SKA-era detection — exactly the "ML on survey imaging" beat.
- TL;DR: The authors benchmark scattering-transform encoders and SE-attention modules layered into dual-branch CNN classifiers for detecting faint diffuse radio emission in Planck-selected galaxy clusters. Beam-normalised cropping plus uv-tapering preprocessing help; stacking multiple preprocessed versions does not. The scattering-transform multi-branch design comes out as the most promising path for SKA-scale surveys.

## Medium priority

**DB-Bench: Benchmarking Deblenders for LSST DESC Using the Blending ToolKit** · Aidan Berres et al. · [arXiv:2607.28475](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28475)
- End-to-end BTK benchmark of SourceExtractor, SCARLET, and DeepDISC on simulated Rubin blends, characterising each algorithm's failure modes (especially unrecognised blends) that propagate into cosmological analyses.

**PhySR: Physics-Informed Neural Network for Super-Resolution Reconstruction in Radio Synthesis Imaging** · Hongkun Yang et al. · [arXiv:2607.27750](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27750)
- A U-Net-based super-resolution network that folds a differentiable primary-beam / PSF forward model into training, reconstructing high-res images from SKA-Mid dirty maps without needing high-res labels.

**Uncertainty-Aware Tidal Disruption Event Classification: A Host-Agnostic Probabilistic Random Forest Approach** · Vysakh Anilkumar et al. · [arXiv:2607.28510](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28510)
- A probabilistic random forest that treats photometric feature measurements as distributions to flag TDEs in the ZTF alert stream without host information, aimed at rare-transient discovery in the Rubin era.

**Growth, geometry, and early-universe split of the matter density parameter $\Omega_m$** · Felicitas Keil, Isaac Tutusaus, Alain Blanchard · [arXiv:2607.28326](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28326)
- Splits $\Omega_m$ inference into geometry, growth, and early-universe regimes across DES 3x2pt, Planck, DESI BAO, Pantheon+, and RSD; finds a ~2σ tension between the geometric and early-universe values.

**Dark Matter Constraints from Small-Scale Cosmic Structure** · Ethan O. Nadler, Keir K. Rogers, Alex Drlica-Wagner · [arXiv:2607.28564](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28564)
- Review of sub-Mpc DM probes (dwarfs, strong lensing, Lyman-α forest, streams, high-z galaxies), the associated modelling systematics, and the emerging role of probe combination and simulation-based inference.

**Measuring Distortion in the Empty Regions of Dimensionality Reduction Scatterplots with the Gap Index** · Jaume Ros, Alessio Arleo, Fernando Paulovich · [arXiv:2607.28324](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28324)
- Proposes a per-triangle "Gap Index" quality metric that scores empty-space distortion in 2D projections (UMAP/t-SNE-style layouts), giving a fast, interpretable readout that complements standard neighborhood-based metrics.

**Cosmo-SPINN: Fuzzy Dark Matter Simulations with Physics-Informed Generative Networks** · Ashutosh Kumar Mishra, Emma Tolley, Nicolas Cerardi · [arXiv:2607.28604](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28604)
- A generative U-Net with a Schrödinger–Poisson consistency loss that emulates FDM field evolution and super-resolves FDM sims, retaining accuracy with only 20% of the training data.

**A study of the large-scale formation in the environment of A3266** · J. Dietl et al. · [arXiv:2607.28140](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28140)
- eROSITA imaging out to $3R_{200}$ detects an X-ray filament linking A3266 to a nearby group at 3.6σ, characterising its temperature, density, and metallicity in the context of ongoing cluster assembly.
