## High priority (≤5)

**Foundation Models for Astrophysics** · Zhao & Ting · [arXiv:2608.02573](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02573v1)
- Why it matters: A first-principles review of foundation models framed around transferable representations for astronomy — directly on-topic for tracking where AION-1-style multimodal work is (and isn't) delivering.
- TL;DR: The chapter argues the defining property of a foundation model is representation transfer, not architecture or pretraining recipe, and audits astronomy claims against few- and zero-shot evidence. It surveys architectures, self-supervised objectives, scaling, adaptation, and cross-modal training, then offers a cautious reading of current astro papers where clear transfer across instruments and populations remains rare.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**SDSS-V Local Volume Mapper: Dithered Data Cube Reconstruction with 3DCubeGen** · Ibarra-Medel, Lugo-Aranda, Zermeño et al. · [arXiv:2608.02597](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02597v1)
- Reconstruction pipeline that coadds millions of dithered IFU spectra into flux-preserving 3D cubes with propagated uncertainties — a working example of the mosaicking/self-calibration workflow applied at survey scale.

**Discovery of the Distant, Ultra-Faint Milky Way Satellite Aquarius IV with the Vera C. Rubin Observatory Early Data Preview 2** · Cerny, Pai, Drlica-Wagner et al. · [arXiv:2608.02601](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02601v1)
- First ultra-faint MW satellite discovered from Rubin data (EDP2), independently confirmed in DECam archives at ~109 kpc — an early demonstration of the rare-object discovery regime LSST will operate in.

**The TNG50-SKIRT Atlas DR2: Spatially resolved synthetic galaxies from UV to submillimetre** · Baes, Vauterin, Abdurro'uf et al. · [arXiv:2608.01908](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01908v1)
- Public library of 1,154 z=0 TNG50 galaxies rendered with SKIRT radiative transfer to produce multi-orientation broadband images, SEDs, and physical-property maps — a ready source of paired image/label data for training imaging models.

**Testing Scale-Dependent Suppression of Structure Growth in the Linear Regime** · Oliveira, Sabogal, Avila, Nunes et al. · [arXiv:2608.02175](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02175v1)
- Joint fσ8/H(z)/SN/CMB fit prefers scale-dependent modified gravity over ΛCDM at 2.2σ while leaving S8 consistent with Planck, arguing k-dependent growth eases the reported growth suppression without worsening tensions.

**Revisiting Metastable Dark Energy in Light of DESI DR2 BAO and DESI DR1 Full-Shape Measurements** · Mukherjee, Shafieloo, Sahni · [arXiv:2608.01844](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01844v1)
- First inclusion of DESI DR1 full-shape clustering alongside DR2 BAO+SN+CMB shows metastable DE decay is compatible with data but not decisively preferred; BAO+SN alone hint at Γ/H0 > 0 at ~2σ, washing out once CMB is added.

**Environmental dependence of galaxy properties in the DESI DR1 Bright Galaxy Survey** · Kim · [arXiv:2608.01940](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01940v1)
- Uses 1.4M BGS_BRIGHT galaxies with a halo-based group catalog to show the sSFR–halo-mass trend is driven mostly by rising satellite fraction rather than intrinsic suppression, and finds Sersic index only weakly environment-dependent.
