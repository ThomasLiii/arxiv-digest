## Medium priority (≤8)

**Fisher Forecasting for the DESC with `Augur`** · Paul Rogozenski, Sankarshana Srinivasan, Javier Sánchez et al. · [arXiv:2608.03876](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03876v1)
- Why it matters: Introduces the LSST DESC's official Fisher-forecast pipeline, useful for scoping weak-lensing and 3×2pt analyses in the LSST era.
- TL;DR: The authors describe `Augur`, an LSST DESC tool that produces Fisher forecasts using the collaboration's science software stack. They cross-check it against external forecasts and nested-sampling posteriors and use it to explore modeling and hyperparameter choices for a harmonic-space 3×2pt configuration, offering diagnostics for reliable forecasting.

**Tomographer: End-to-end Redshift Distribution Estimation for Source Catalogs and Intensity Maps** · Yi-Kuan Chiang, Yu Voon Ng, Yu-Ren Lin et al. · [arXiv:2608.03415](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03415v1)
- Why it matters: A ready-to-use clustering-redshift framework directly relevant to photometric-redshift and n(z) calibration for surveys and diffuse intensity maps.
- TL;DR: Tomographer precomputes spatial "activation maps" from 3M SDSS spectroscopic tracers over 10,000 deg² so that any user-supplied catalog or intensity map yields a bias-weighted dN/dz via O(1) map multiplications instead of expensive pair counts. This removes the main technical barriers (reference assembly, pair counting, systematic corrections) that have limited broader use of clustering redshifts.

**Pixel-Level Calibration for Space-Based High Precision Astrometry Using Young's Fringes** · Hugo Rousset, Eric Thiébaut, Chloé Rey et al. · [arXiv:2608.03356](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03356v1)
- Why it matters: Sub-pixel detector self-calibration for a proposed HWO focal plane — the same class of methods used in survey astrometry and image self-calibration.
- TL;DR: The authors adapt a Young's-fringes calibration scheme to map the intra-pixel response function of a CMOS sensor for the AGATE/HWO astrometric instrument. Lab and simulation results show ~5×10⁻⁵ pix precision with 10k frames, identify inter-pixel capacitance and fringe hyperbolicity as dominant error sources, and propose an iterative inverse-problem solver with a hyperbolic fringe model to scale to gigapixel focal planes.

**Cosmology in the Einstein Telescope era: comparing traditional and simulation-based methods for population inference** · Giovanni Antinozzi, Guillermo Franco Abellán, Davide Sciotti et al. · [arXiv:2608.04005](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04005v1)
- TL;DR: A methodological comparison of hierarchical Bayesian inference against simulation-based inference (Marginal Neural Ratio Estimation) for dark-siren cosmology with O(10⁴) mock ET events, finding SBI reproduces HBI posteriors on (H₀, Ωₘ) at a small fraction of the compute once amortized.

**SCRIPT in the Cosmic Dawn: Distinguishing Redshift-Evolving Galaxy Populations with 21-cm Fluctuations** · Janakee Raste, Tirthankar Roy Choudhury · [arXiv:2608.03441](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03441v1)
- TL;DR: Extends the SCRIPT semi-numerical framework to self-consistently model spin-temperature fluctuations from Lyman-α coupling and X-ray heating, showing that global 21-cm signals from JWST-motivated evolving-galaxy models are degenerate with static models but that the 21-cm power spectrum breaks the degeneracy via clustering-bias imprints.
