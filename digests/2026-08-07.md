## Medium priority (≤8)

**photo-3x2-pt: Cosmology from cosmic shear and galaxy clustering with a single photometric galaxy catalog** · Takashi Hamana · [arXiv:2608.05530](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05530v1)
- Why it matters: A single-catalog 3×2-pt weak lensing + clustering analysis on HSC-Y3, directly on the medium-priority cosmology track.
- TL;DR: Joint angular-power-spectrum analysis of HSC-Y3 galaxy clustering, galaxy-shear, and cosmic shear tightens the S8 constraint to 0.76–0.81, roughly 25% better than the corresponding cosmic-shear-only analyses.

**SPT-3G D1: Foreground-Robust Lensing Templates for Primordial Gravitational Wave Searches** · Y. Nakato, W. L. K. Wu, Y. Omori et al. · [arXiv:2608.06343](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06343v1)
- Why it matters: A concrete, foreground-hardened CMB-lensing template pipeline for delensing — directly relevant to weak-lensing methodology in CMB cosmology.
- TL;DR: Combining SPT-3G E modes and lensing reconstruction with a Planck CIB tracer, a profile-hardened GMV estimator yields a delensing template with residual foreground bias under 10% of statistical uncertainty and the best delensing efficiency (A_lens^res ≈ 0.48) reported to date.

**NestyNet. II. Coherent Function-Space Posteriors from Scientific Neural Surrogates (or How to Avoid Expensive MCMC)** · Rodrigo Ibata, Wassim Tenachi, Foivos Diakogiannis et al. · [arXiv:2608.06274](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06274v1)
- Why it matters: Cheap function-space uncertainty for scientific neural surrogates is exactly what makes NN-based astronomy inference (e.g., simulation-based, phase-space, or emulator work) trustworthy.
- TL;DR: Linearizing a second-order-trained surrogate around its converged fit yields a low-dimensional Gauss–Newton/Laplace posterior; on an 800-parameter mock stellar-disk distribution function, just four uncertainty modes reproduce 99% of the vertical-force posterior variance and propagate to derived quantities in under a second.

**MARS: A Lightweight Morphology-Aware RFI Segmentation Network for Mask-Guided Mitigation in Radio Astronomy** · Zhaocheng Gong, Jack White, Jayanta Roy, Wesley Armour · [arXiv:2608.05546](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05546v1)
- Why it matters: A CNN applied to survey (filterbank) imagery with a signal-preservation loss — a compact case study of the kind of GPU-friendly ML that has to sit inside real-time survey pipelines.
- TL;DR: A reduced-width U-Net with directional bottleneck filters segments RFI in the frequency–time plane at F1 = 0.978, preserves ~97% of injected dispersed-pulse fluence, and runs 6–7× faster than optimized CPU baselines on a GH200 GPU.

**Reanalyzing Megamasers: a low value of H_0 from a local probe changes our view of the Hubble Tension** · Richard Watkins, Hume A. Feldman · [arXiv:2608.06247](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06247v1)
- Why it matters: A local-probe re-analysis that flips the sign of the Hubble tension for megamasers — relevant to the cosmology / large-scale-structure agenda.
- TL;DR: Using a constrained-simulation velocity-field reconstruction to correct megamaser redshifts for peculiar motions yields an H0 that agrees with CMB values and is >2σ off the distance ladder, suggesting the residual tension is a distance-ladder systematic rather than new physics.

**A space of inference spaces in the space sciences — Parametric Bayesian inference in astronomy, cosmology and particle physics** · Johannes Buchner · [arXiv:2608.06078](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06078v1)
- Why it matters: A practical benchmark suite covering the shapes of posteriors that astronomy inference actually produces — useful when picking or building samplers for cosmology/LSS pipelines.
- TL;DR: Buchner catalogs a diverse set of astronomy/cosmology/particle-physics Bayesian problems along seven axes (dimensionality, Gaussianity, tail weight, informativeness, multimodality, mixed constraint strength, phase transitions), packages them with mock data in a Docker image, and proposes them as a standard sampler test bed.
