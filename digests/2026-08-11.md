## High priority

**A bestiary of low-level electrical artifacts in SPHEREx flight data** · Fazar, Zemcov, Dowell et al. · [arXiv:2608.09862v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09862v1)
- Why it matters: Direct SPHEREx mission-instrument paper — exactly the kind of first-survey flight-data characterization that shapes what the SPHEREx pipeline can and cannot deliver.
- TL;DR: The SPHEREx team catalogs the low-level optical and electronic artifacts they see in the first survey's H2RG readouts and describes how the science-data-center pipeline mitigates them. Some effects match lab predictions, others diverge, and a few were only discovered on-orbit. The paper reads as a working reference for anyone doing photometry or spectroscopy on SPHEREx maps.

**Searching for low-surface-brightness galaxies with compact neural networks: a parameter-efficient approach to first-pass selection of diffuse galaxies in HSC-SSP imaging** · Heemann, Cecatka, Bomans · [arXiv:2608.09566v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09566v1)
- Why it matters: A concrete instance of CNNs applied to survey imaging for a hard detection problem — low-surface-brightness galaxies where selection effects dominate — and worth reading for the design choices behind the "compact" architecture.
- TL;DR: The authors build a hybrid HSC-SSP LSBG pipeline that runs permissive SEP source detection first, then uses compact CNNs as a morphological validator before physically motivated consistency checks and galfitm surface-brightness modelling. The compact-CNN stage is meant to prune contaminants cheaply so the expensive parametric fits only see plausible diffuse candidates. It is positioned as a first-pass selector, not an end-to-end classifier.

## Medium priority

**Foreground Subtraction with a Tensor-Based Oriented Singular Value Decomposition Method for HI Experiments** · Zuo, Chen, Mao · [arXiv:2608.09129v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09129v1)
- Native-tensor O-SVD foreground removal for 21 cm intensity mapping that keeps spatial-spectral correlations instead of flattening the cube — relevant to LSS methodology.

**Multi-tracer mass bias in matched cosmic voids from SDSS DR7 and the ELUCID constrained simulation** · Zhang, Yang, Guo et al. · [arXiv:2608.09086v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09086v1)
- Uses a matched-void framework between SDSS galaxies and the ELUCID constrained simulation to measure how galaxies, subhaloes, and dark matter trace underdense environments.

**Unsupervised Domain Adaptation for Multitask Image Analysis in Realistic Context with Extreme Label Shift; Application to the CTAO first Large Sized Telescope** · Dell'aiera, Vuillaume, Benoit · [arXiv:2608.09630v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09630v1)
- Combines unsupervised domain adaptation with multitask learning under extreme class imbalance on CTAO LST-1 data — a practical template for adapting simulation-trained networks to real survey imaging.

**The Environmental Dependence of Star Cluster Demographics** · Tang, Grasha, Krumholz et al. · [arXiv:2608.09439v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09439v1)
- Fits ~8300 LEGUS clusters in 12 galaxies with a Bayesian forward model coupled to a neural-network completeness estimator (c-4), folding realistic catalogue selection into the likelihood.

**A Machine Learning Based Search for Lunar Anomalies** · Kelahan, Angerhausen, Lesnikowski et al. · [arXiv:2608.09350v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09350v1)
- Applies a beta-VAE to LRO NAC imagery for unsupervised anomaly discovery — a good reference for anomaly/rare-object pipelines even though the target is planetary.

**Verifiably grounded machine interpretation of lunar geology** · Sander, Wohlfarth, Wöhler · [arXiv:2608.09276v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09276v1)
- Multimodal vision-language model that produces geologic interpretations grounded in co-registered topographic, spectral, and geologic maps — relevant as a design point for multimodal foundation-model use in survey science.
