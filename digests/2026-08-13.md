## Medium priority (≤8)

**CIBER × galaxy cross-correlations reveal a bright, low-redshift NIR background** · Cheng et al. · [arXiv:2608.12116](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12116)
- Why it matters: First tomographic cross-correlation between CIBER near-IR background maps and DESI Legacy / HSC UDS photometric galaxy catalogs — a large-scale-structure measurement that leans on the kind of survey/cross-survey pipeline my interests cover.
- TL;DR: The authors cross-correlate 1.1 and 1.8 μm CIBER imager anisotropies with photometric galaxy samples and find cross-power well above an integrated-galaxy-light model on scales ℓ<2000, concentrated at z≲0.6. Cluster members explain 15–20% of the large-angle signal; the rest points to substantial low-redshift group/halo-scale contribution to the extragalactic background light.

**Classifying Quasar Types Without a Spectrum** · anonymized author list · [arXiv:2608.11916](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11916)
- Why it matters: A photometric-only classifier for rare-object taxonomy in a wide-field survey (ZTF), directly relevant to my interest in scalable classification and rare-object discovery on survey data.
- TL;DR: Using Slepian Wavelet Variance on irregularly sampled ZTF light curves, the authors cluster 754 MILLIQUAS quasars into Type 1 vs Type 2 with 99% and 87% recovery respectively, requiring no spectra and no assumed variability model.
