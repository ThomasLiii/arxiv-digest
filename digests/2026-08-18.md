## High priority (≤5)

**Galaxy Morphology Classification: Uncertainty Modeling and Out of Distribution Detection** · Prem Prakash, Shantanu Desai, P. K. Srijith · [arXiv:2608.16654v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16654v1)
- Why it matters: Combines two of your priorities — CNN morphology on survey imaging (Galaxy Zoo DECaLS) with OOD detection for finding rare/anomalous objects and calibrated uncertainties, exactly the tooling needed for lensing searches and rare-object discovery at Euclid/LSST scale.
- TL;DR: A ResNet-34 trained with an IsoMaxPlus (distance-based) loss plus MC Dropout keeps 97% classification accuracy across nine morphology classes while boosting TNR@TPR95 for OOD detection by ~90% over the cross-entropy baseline and cutting expected calibration error by ~65–73%. Underconfident or misclassified galaxies map to higher entropy and lower distance-to-class scores, giving an interpretable "look at this" signal for unusual morphologies.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**Nonlinear velocity power spectrum: modeling the cosmological dependence on the Hubble constant and cold dark matter density** · Francesca Lepori, Ruth Durrer, Martin Kunz et al. · [arXiv:2608.16489v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16489v1)
- Semi-analytical ΛCDM model of the divergence velocity power spectrum for k<1/Mpc, accurate to <2.5% in h and ω_cdm and (surprisingly) ω_cdm-independent on nonlinear scales; public Python implementation.

**Halo mass of ULIRGs at z~2** · Jun Pan, Ming Li, Cheng Cheng et al. · [arXiv:2608.16667v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16667v1)
- Angular clustering + HOD fits for ~3000 ULIRGs in COSMOS and BOOTES place them in ~10^12.5–12.9 h⁻¹ M_☉ halos that evolve toward ~10^13.7 by z=0, with a hint of field-dependent satellite fraction.

**Brightest group and cluster galaxies as indicators of relaxation** · K. Kelder, P. Heinämäki, P. Nurmi et al. · [arXiv:2608.16481v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16481v1)
- Using IllustrisTNG, offset of the brightest halo galaxy from the potential minimum combined with the BHG–2nd-brightest magnitude gap reproduces the relaxed-halo mass function out to z~0.2, giving an observationally cheap relaxation cut for cluster cosmology.

**Supervising the Path to Fine Scales: GalerkinFlow for Scientific-Field and Image Super-Resolution** · Zikang Zhan · [arXiv:2608.16546v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16546v1)
- Equation-agnostic super-resolution that supervises intermediate states along a coarse-to-fine reconstruction path via a Galerkin-mixed velocity field, beating equation-free baselines on Navier–Stokes and Darcy and staying competitive on natural images.

**Convolution-Free Holistic Multivariance Decomposition Layer for Efficient Hyperspectral Image Classification** · Süha Tuna, Ülker Başar · [arXiv:2608.16241v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16241v1)
- End-to-end differentiable tensor-decomposition layer that separates single-mode from higher-order interactions, matching 2D/3D CNN hyperspectral accuracy with far fewer parameters — potentially useful as a lightweight substitute for CNN feature extractors on multi-band survey cubes.

**Turning spectra into images improves plant trait retrieval with 2D-CNNs** · Javier Lopatin, Teja Kattenborn, Eya Cherif et al. · [arXiv:2608.16661v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16661v1)
- Reshaping 1D spectra into 2D grids and running an EfficientNet-B0 lifts multi-trait R² from 0.587 to 0.684 vs. a 1D-CNN baseline, and a masked-autoencoder pretrained on 139k unlabeled spectral "images" beats 1D self-supervised counterparts — a transferable trick for spectroscopic or SED CNNs in astronomy.
