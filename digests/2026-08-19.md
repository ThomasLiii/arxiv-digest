## Medium priority (≤8)
**Vera C. Rubin LSST Synthetic Magnitudes derived from Gaia XP Spectra** · Anguiano et al. · [arXiv:2608.17922](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17922)
- Why it matters: A joint Gaia–LSST photometric calibration bridge is directly useful groundwork for the survey imaging pipelines and Milky Way / photo-z work I care about.
- TL;DR: The authors build synthetic LSST ugrizy magnitudes from Gaia XP low-resolution spectra by empirically correcting the spectra against SDSS Stripe 82 (with DES cross-checks), driving down systematic residuals by an order of magnitude and delivering an all-sky training/calibration set at LSST bright-end magnitudes.

**eROSITA cosmology with galaxy groups: hot gas budget out to the virial radius** · Popesso et al. · [arXiv:2608.17735](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17735)
- Why it matters: Directly measures baryonic feedback signatures at group scales — the same nuisance physics that dominates weak-lensing and cluster-cosmology systematics.
- TL;DR: Bayesian fits to eROSITA surface-brightness profiles of 25 2MRS-selected groups yield sub-cosmic hot gas fractions and scaling relations out to R_200, quantifying how much baryonic mass is expelled beyond the virial radius.

**Large eROSITA X-ray sources as 2MRS galaxy groups** · Finoguenov et al. · [arXiv:2608.17732](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17732)
- Why it matters: A methodologically interesting wavelet + Hausdorff-distance cross-match of X-ray extended sources with optical groups — the kind of catalog-building trick that generalizes to Euclid / LSST cluster finding.
- TL;DR: A 619-group, 80%-pure X-ray catalog is constructed from eROSITA-DE DR1 using multi-scale wavelet decomposition and a novel ranked-partial-Hausdorff identifier against 2MRS group membership, with its X-ray luminosity function agreeing with prior work down to ~5×10^41 erg/s.

**A stochastic forward model for the intergalactic dispersion-measure distribution of Fast Radio Bursts** · Feldbrugge et al. · [arXiv:2608.17658](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17658)
- Why it matters: Provides a fast, physically decomposed forward model of DM_IGM tied to IllustrisTNG — a useful pipeline for using FRBs as cosmological probes (H0, missing baryons).
- TL;DR: `turbofrb` calibrates only four parameters against ray-traced TNG sightlines yet reproduces the mean DM at the percent level and Jensen–Shannon divergences ≤5×10⁻³ over z=0.5–2.5, disentangling diffuse IGM, halo, and filament channels and recovering an injected H0 within 1σ on localised FRBs.

**NMMA-Astro-COLIBRI: An Automated Light-Curve Supernovae Classification Service in the Multi-Survey Era** · NMMA/Astro-COLIBRI collab. · [arXiv:2608.17568](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17568)
- Why it matters: Real-time Bayesian transient classification from sparse photometry is squarely on the "rare-object discovery in survey data" axis and pre-figures LSST alert-stream tooling.
- TL;DR: The service ingests filtered photometric alerts and runs nested-sampling fits against 11 SN templates plus kilonova models, returning per-model log Bayes factors within minutes and correctly re-classifying SN 2021ugl as Type IIb from only its first ~6 days of ZTF g/r data.
