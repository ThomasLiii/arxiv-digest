## Medium priority (≤8)

**SPT-3G+: A Cosmic Microwave Background Experiment for the South Pole Telescope** · T. Natoli et al. · [arXiv:2608.20236](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20236v1)
- Why it matters: The delensing program and cluster/transient discovery outputs from SPT-3G+ intersect directly with weak-lensing and large-scale-structure science.
- TL;DR: The paper describes the survey concept for a 6,020-pixel dichroic 90/150 GHz camera to be deployed on SPT in 2029, delivering roughly an order-of-magnitude mapping-speed gain over SPT-3G. Six years of observations should reach 0.5 μK-arcmin combined depth, enabling deep CMB lensing maps, cluster catalogs, and — in combination with BICEP — a target of σ(r)≈0.001 on the tensor-to-scalar ratio.

**Cluster finding with outskirt stellar masses and percolation** · Pablo Avalos et al. · [arXiv:2608.19768](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19768v1)
- Why it matters: A cleaner, projection-effect-free cluster selection is directly relevant to optical cluster cosmology work on surveys like Euclid.
- TL;DR: Using IllustrisTNG300 at z=0.4, the authors quantify satellite contamination in a cluster sample defined by the outer stellar mass (50–100 kpc annulus) of central galaxies and show a simple proximity-based percolation step suppresses it. With a M_out cut around 4×10^10 M_sun and a 2 R_200c percolation radius they recover complete, pure samples above ~10^14 M_sun, offering a red-sequence-independent selection route.

**Far from the Crowd: Scalable Self-Supervised Learning via Geographic Isolation** · Daniele Rege Cambrin, Francesco Rossi, Mattia Varile · [arXiv:2608.19766](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19766v1)
- Why it matters: A cheap, metadata-only curriculum for MoCo/MAE pretraining that translates naturally to sky surveys where position on the sky can serve as an isolation proxy.
- TL;DR: The authors rank remote-sensing tiles by geographic isolation (from geolocation metadata alone) and feed that ordering as a curriculum into MoCoV2 and MAE. On CopernicusBench tasks the schedule matches or beats visual-complexity curricula while cutting pre-computation by ~140×, and it hits baseline final-epoch accuracy on 20–40% of the training budget, with CKA analysis showing higher effective-rank embeddings.

**A Prediction for DESI Full-Shape: Increasing Tomographic Ω_m(z) Trend** · Eoin Ó Colgáin, M. M. Sheikh-Jabbari · [arXiv:2608.19883](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19883v1)
- Why it matters: Consistency tests across independent DESI probes are central to interpreting apparent dark-energy dynamics in the LSS program.
- TL;DR: The authors combine DESI DR2 Lyman-α Full-Shape constraints at z_eff=2.33 with DR1 Full-Shape results at lower redshifts and report an Ω_m(z) slope of 0.022±0.012 (1.8σ from constant), which maps to phantom dark energy — opposite in sign to the trend from DESI BAO, implying an unavoidable phantom crossing if the trends persist.

**A blind spot in transverse BAO calibration** · Domenico Sapone · [arXiv:2608.20296](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20296v1)
- Why it matters: A model-independent systematics diagnostic for angular BAO measurements matters for any LSS pipeline using DESI/Euclid-era transverse clustering.
- TL;DR: A constant calibration error in transverse BAO is fully degenerate with r_d h and invisible in transverse-only fits, but combining with radial BAO reduces the consistency test to a straight line whose slope gives the relative calibration ε. Applied to two SDSS transverse-BAO compilations the test yields ε=1.073±0.021 (3.8σ from unity) and 1.021±0.029, with the two datasets differing at 3.9σ.

**Transient Early Dark Energy-Like Dynamics as a Mechanism for Enhanced Early Structure Formation in the JWST Era** · Abhik Bhattacharjee et al. · [arXiv:2608.20288](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20288v1)
- Why it matters: An alternative structure-formation scenario constrained by CMB/BAO/SN data is context for interpreting high-z galaxy abundances and future lensing/clustering probes.
- TL;DR: A sub-percent dark-matter component briefly behaves like early dark energy during radiation domination, producing a window of negative effective sound speed that boosts small-scale perturbation growth. The enhanced halo abundance lowers the star-formation efficiencies needed to match JWST galaxy counts at z≳10 while remaining consistent with CMB, BAO, and SN Ia constraints.
