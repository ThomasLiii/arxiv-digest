## High priority (≤5)

**Decoupling candidate dual AGN from chance superpositions in the GOTHIC survey via a deep-learning framework** · Mukheja, Saha, Bhattacharya et al. · [arXiv:2608.24164](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24164)
- Why it matters: Directly on-topic — a CNN-family object detector applied to SDSS survey imaging to sift a large candidate list, with a validation-set score that beats the deterministic pipeline it revisits.
- TL;DR: The authors re-examine ~46k dual-AGN candidates that the GOTHIC pipeline had rejected and train a YOLOv11 oriented-bounding-box detector on annotated SDSS cutouts to separate real dual nuclei from foreground stars and blends. The model reports precision/recall of 0.92/0.91 on the dual-nucleus class and yields ~29.6k candidates, of which visual inspection suggests roughly 55–62% are genuine, giving a conservative refined catalogue of ~13.7k pairs down to ~0.56″ separations.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**First application of weak lensing peak steepness statistics to HSC Y1 data: effectively probing halo density profiles** · Li, Liu, Zhang et al. · [arXiv:2608.24591](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24591)
- Combining peak height and steepness statistics on HSC Y1, the authors obtain S_8 = 0.76 (+0.08/-0.07) and infer notably low halo concentrations, consistent with strong baryonic feedback at cluster scales in the HMcode2016 framework.

**The Effect of Slitless Spectroscopic Redshift Uncertainty and Interlopers on BAO Measurements from CSST-like Samples** · Ding, Yu, Zhang · [arXiv:2608.24276](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24276)
- Using FastPM mocks of CSST-like slitless spectroscopy, the study models how Gaussian redshift errors and [OIII]–Hβ interlopers damp and distort the BAO signal and shows that reconstruction still recovers α_⊥ and α_∥ with only ~0.1–0.2% systematic bias.
