## Medium priority (≤8)

**Revealing Galactic dust beneath the cosmic infrared background anisotropies with Wavelet Phase Harmonics** · Sinha, Ghosh, Allys et al. · [arXiv:2608.25617](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25617v1)
- A generative-statistical (WPH) component-separation algorithm cleans Planck 353 GHz maps into a dust-only signal at high Galactic latitudes across low, intermediate, and high S/N patches, without HI-template leakage — a useful non-parametric alternative to the linear dust–HI regressions that pipelines like Euclid's rely on.

**Finding and using interpretable latents in a neutrino foundation model with sparse autoencoders** · Bonnet-Guerrini, Ioannou-Nikolaides, Timiryasov et al. · [arXiv:2608.26090](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26090v1)
- Sparse-autoencoder mechanistic interpretability on an IceCube-pretrained foundation model surfaces physical concepts the direction head ignores, and an event-level uncertainty head trained on those latents cuts median angular error from 20.2° to 3.2° at 20% selection — a compelling template for extracting hidden capability from survey/imaging foundation models.

**Constraining primordial non-Gaussianity and energy injection with the thermal Sunyaev–Zeldovich and integrated Sachs–Wolfe cross-correlation** · Ibitoye, Ma, Tiwari · [arXiv:2608.25809](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25809v1)
- The ISW×tSZ cross-spectrum jointly bounds scale-independent (f_NL = −358⁺¹⁴⁰₋₁₁₄) and scale-dependent PNG plus early-Universe energy injection, with the authors flagging Simons Observatory and Euclid as the near-term tightening path.

**Distinguishing cold and self-interacting dark matter through topological analysis** · Szpilfidel, Laigle, Boldrini et al. · [arXiv:2608.25632](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25632v1)
- Genus statistics on the Darkium DM-only simulations separate CDM from four SIDM cross-section variants at intermediate scales (≲0.05 Mpc/h) around 10¹²–10¹⁴ M⊙/h halos, including a redshift-projection setup meant to mirror observational realities.
