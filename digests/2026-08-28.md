## Medium priority (≤8)

**ELUCID-DESI II. Revealing dark matter mass, tidal, and velocity (MTV) fields using galaxy group phase information** · Li, Yang, Hong et al. · [arXiv:2608.26668](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26668v1)
- A DESI-BGS-oriented reconstruction of dark matter density, tidal, and velocity fields at z<0.6 that swaps the usual analytic bias correction for a simulation-calibrated mapping from galaxy-group phase information, achieves ~120 km/s velocity residuals, and iteratively corrects Kaiser distortions to build the mass field — directly relevant to LSS reconstruction on spectroscopic surveys.

**Optimal transport regularized dynamic radio interferometric reconstruction** · Nilipour, Moriyama, Ikeda et al. · [arXiv:2608.27192](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27192v1)
- Proposes an OT-distance regularizer inside the regularized-maximum-likelihood framework for time-resolved VLBI imaging, tested on EHT-scale SgrA* simulations (geometric and GRMHD) where it recovers orbital motion and dynamic cross-correlations noticeably better than existing priors — a new regularization tool for interferometric image reconstruction.
