## High priority (≤5)

**DINOspec: Efficient Multimodal Alignment of Vision and Spectral Foundation Models for Astronomy** · Lastufka, Drozdova, Schaerer, Voloshynovskiy · [arXiv:2608.30503](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30503v1)
- Why it matters: Directly extends the AION-1 line — composing a frozen DINOv3 image encoder with the AION-1 spectral tokenizer for astronomy, exactly the multimodal-foundation-model direction you track.
- TL;DR: The authors keep two pretrained encoders frozen and learn small contrastive adapters over ~20k image–spectrum pairs to bring their representations into a shared space. Alignment lifts galaxy-morphology F1 from 0.72 to 0.78 and spectral-class F1 from 0.70 to 0.74 while training at most 21M parameters, though spectroscopic redshift regression is unaffected. The takeaway is that scientific foundation models can be composed post-hoc rather than jointly retrained.

**Learning Radio Astronomical Representations with LeJEPA and Very Small Models** · Lastufka, Drozdova, Kinakh, Holotyak et al. · [arXiv:2608.30594](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30594v1)
- Why it matters: Self-supervised in-domain pretraining for survey imaging, with an explicit argument that small domain-native models can match big transferred foundation models — a useful counterpoint for the CNN-vs-ViT and foundation-model-scale debates.
- TL;DR: The paper pretrains ~6M-parameter vision encoders on Radio Galaxy Zoo images using LeJEPA's latent-space regularization objective and benchmarks against other self-supervised recipes. Across three radio-galaxy morphology datasets it matches a much larger foundation model while producing more consistent representations across train/eval splits. The authors argue that objective choice, not scale, is what unlocks compact scientific vision models.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**Broad-band host measurements of little red dots and similar compact nuclei require source-conditioned polychromatic PSFs** · Bonaque-González · [arXiv:2608.30459](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30459v1)
- A polychromatic-PSF analysis shows that using field-star PSFs to model compact high-z sources whose SEDs differ from stars can spuriously invent or inflate an extended host, biasing inferred sizes, luminosities, and masses of LRDs — a PSF-modelling caveat relevant to any broad-band deblending on survey imaging.

**Photometric redshifts for active galactic nuclei with LePHARE for the Vera C. Rubin Observatory** · Shirley, Salvato, Cohen-Tanugi, Ilbert et al. · [arXiv:2608.30494](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30494v1)
- Benchmarks LePHARE SED-fitting on six X-ray/radio/IR/variability/colour/spec-selected AGN samples in COSMOS with HSC-CLAUDS photometry as an LSST-depth proxy, releases per-selection configuration recipes plus AGN photo-z posteriors, and warns that point-like AGN processed with galaxy templates get badly biased redshifts.

**The Analysis, not the Aperture: End-to-End Transformer Reconstruction for Imaging Atmospheric Cherenkov Telescopes** · Jobst, Heckmann, Heinrich, Paneque · [arXiv:2608.31148](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.31148v1)
- Replaces four decades of hand-crafted IACT image parameterization with a factorised spatio-temporal video vision transformer trained end-to-end for joint γ/hadron classification, energy, and arrival-direction regression, dropping the effective energy threshold from 0.22 to 0.07 TeV and roughly tripling collection area at 0.2 TeV.

**SNAD: enabling discovery in the era of big data** · Pruzhinskaya, Ishida, Malanchev, Lavrukhina et al. · [arXiv:2608.30477](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30477v1)
- Overview of the SNAD anomaly-discovery pipeline for wide-field surveys — the Coniferest active-learning library, the SNAD Transient Miner, and a ZTF viewer — which has already produced 100+ supernova candidates and rarer classes (red-dwarf flares, SLSNe, RS CVn, YSOs) from ZTF data releases.

**Not all those missing are lost: leveraging galaxy clustering in incomplete catalogs to unleash dark sirens cosmology** · Barbieri, Kalomenopoulos, Tagliazucchi, Gair et al. · [arXiv:2608.30599](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30599v1)
- Replaces the uniform-in-comoving-volume assumption for galaxies missing from dark-siren host catalogues with a ξ(r)-driven repopulation, tightening posteriors by 2–4× for catalogues at 1–10% completeness without introducing bias.

**Measuring peculiar velocity and tomographic redshift dipole with DESI DR1 catalogs** · Wu, Xia · [arXiv:2608.30914](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30914v1)
- Applies the Doppler-modulated redshift-dipole estimator to four DESI DR1 tracer populations (BGS, LRGs, ELGs, QSOs) over 0.1<z<2.1; the QSO sample recovers v ≈ 358 km/s in agreement with the CMB kinematic dipole, while number-count dipoles are inflated by power leakage and footprint incompleteness.

**SPT-3G D1: Quadratic-Estimator CMB Lensing Reconstruction and Cosmology** · Omori, Wu, Nakato, Bianchini et al. · [arXiv:2608.31136](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.31136v1)
- Presents a polarization-dominated CMB lensing map over 1500 deg² from SPT-3G's 2019–2020 seasons and derives σ₈Ω_m^0.25 = 0.6046 ± 0.0096; combined with ACT DR6, Planck PR4 and DESI DR2 BAO it tightens Σm_ν < 0.072 eV and eases prior 2σ hints of curvature and w₀wₐ deviations.

**On-sky demonstration of a vector Zernike wavefront sensor in a cascaded adaptive optics system** · Motte, Chambouleyron, Fétick, Oyarzun et al. · [arXiv:2608.30666](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30666v1)
- Adds a 400 Hz v-ZWFS + 97-actuator DM second AO stage (OZIRIIS) downstream of PAPYRUS's pyramid first stage, closing the loop on sky for +16 pts Strehl and identifying low-Strehl optical-gain effects — an AO ingredient for future high-contrast imaging.
