## High priority (≤5)

**The fraction of clumpy star-forming galaxies in nearby galaxies from CLAUDS and HSC-SSP** · Popp, Dickinson, Serjeant, Fortson et al. · [arXiv:2609.01152](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01152v1)
- Why it matters: Deep-learning object detection on a wide-field imaging survey using the ZOOBOT astronomy foundation model as a feature-extractor backbone — a concrete example of the foundation-model-for-survey-imaging direction you track, applied to clump/deblending-style morphology.
- TL;DR: The authors run an improved DL detector with ZOOBOT as the backbone on a mass-complete sample of 5,395 star-forming galaxies at z ≤ 0.32 in HSC-SSP+CLAUDS fields, extracting 12,790 off-centre clumps. Requiring a ≥8% u-band flux contribution, the clumpy fraction falls from ~31% at z~0.3 to ~23% at z~0.1, matching low-redshift extrapolations of high-z measurements; imposing an M_cl ≥ 10⁷ M_⊙ cut instead pushes the clumpy fraction to ~60% and flips its stellar-mass dependence.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**Baryonic feedback suppression of the matter power spectrum: a three-parameter fitting formula and its single-parameter reduction** · Zhang · [arXiv:2609.00807](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00807v1)
- Calibrates a physically interpretable three-parameter fit for P_hydro/P_DMO across FLAMINGO, IllustrisTNG, Illustris, and CAMELS-TNG/SIMBA at k ≤ 3 h/Mpc, z ≤ 2 (max error <0.03), and shows a one-parameter reduction suffices for most simulations — directly addressing the dominant systematic in weak-lensing cosmology.

**First tomographic measurements of the angular clustering and bias of photometric quasars from S-PLUS** · Lopes, Avila, Bernui, Nakazono · [arXiv:2609.01238](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01238v1)
- Measures the two-point angular correlation of 23,402 S-PLUS QuCatS photometric quasars in four redshift bins over 1.1 ≤ z_phot ≤ 2.6, recovering a bias evolution consistent with prior work and validating the photo-z pipeline for future S-PLUS large-scale-structure analyses.

**Optical Colors as Compressed Spectral Information for Trans-Neptunian Objects** · Lin, Markwardt, Napier, Adams et al. · [arXiv:2609.01169](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01169v1)
- Builds a low-dimensional optical–NIR spectral manifold from JWST spectroscopy of 48 outer solar system bodies and treats broadband colors as compressed representations on it, ranking filter combinations by KL information gain — a representation-learning framing aimed at scaling to Rubin's millions of objects.

**The MWA Interestingly Deep AStrophysical (MIDAS) Survey: Survey Description and First Data Release** · Paterson, Seymour, Hurley-Walker, Galvin et al. · [arXiv:2609.01405](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01405v1)
- Releases the deepest MWA survey to date at 215.68 MHz over the GAMA9/GAMA23 fields (~2100 deg² total, sub-mJy sensitivity, 133k catalogued components) along with an updated open-source GLEAM-X-derived deep-imaging pipeline, a useful reference for wide-field survey mosaicking practice.

**Addressing the low-wind effect with the Lyot-based low-order wavefront sensor on SCExAO** · Singh, Vievard, Lozi, Deo et al. · [arXiv:2609.00737](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00737v1)
- Reports on-sky measurement and correction of low-wind-effect differential piston with a machine-learned Lyot-based low-order WFS on SCExAO, keeping the correction compatible with coronagraphic imaging modes — a data-driven fix for a persistent NCPA-adjacent problem on segmented pupils.

**Exploring reinforcement learning to enhance focal-plane wavefront control for vortex coronagraphs** · Taskin, Nousiainen, Orban de Xivry, Absil et al. · [arXiv:2609.01199](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01199v1)
- Tests an RL-based focal-plane wavefront control algorithm (PO4NCPA, using sequential phase diversity for phase-ambiguity handling) in METIS-representative simulations with scalar and vector vortex coronagraphs, extending learned-control experiments beyond classical AO.

**Investigating the Young Stellar Populations and Hierarchies in Nearby Galaxies with the UVIT. III.** · Shashank, Subramanian, Menon, Mondal et al. · [arXiv:2609.01065](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01065v1)
- Runs two-point correlation analysis on ~25,000 UV-selected star-forming clumps across 17 nearby galaxies to derive galaxy-specific correlation lengths (100 pc–3.4 kpc) and fractal dimensions (0.71–1.73), showing SF hierarchies depend on host morphology rather than being universal.
