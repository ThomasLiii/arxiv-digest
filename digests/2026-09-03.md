## High priority (≤5)

**Empirical Near-Infrared Spectral Templates from SPHEREx: Disentangling AGN and Host-Galaxy Emission** · Ren, Guo, Zheng et al. · [arXiv:2609.02167](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02167)
- Why it matters: A SPHEREx-native, data-driven template dictionary for AGN/host decomposition — directly relevant to your interest in SPHEREx methods and representation learning on survey spectra.
- TL;DR: The authors use weighted non-negative matrix factorization plus forward modeling on the SPHEREx NEP field to learn a 14-component empirical near-infrared template set that separates seven galaxy and seven AGN components. The templates recover redshifts to |Δz|<0.01 for ~93% of sources and classify galaxies vs. narrow- and broad-line AGNs at 81% accuracy in the deep field (73% at all-sky depth), with host-fraction estimates that agree with SDSS spectral and HSC imaging decompositions.
