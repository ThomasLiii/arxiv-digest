## High priority (≤5)

**Euclid preparation. The shape of halo profiles in ΛCDM and non-standard cosmologies** · Euclid Collaboration, L. Pizzuti, G. Y. Ferron et al. · [arXiv:2609.04192](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04192)
- Why it matters: Direct Euclid preparation paper quantifying how modified-gravity, neutrino, and dynamical-dark-energy signatures show up in cluster weak-lensing profiles — squarely in your Euclid mission-methods interest and adjacent to WL cosmology.
- TL;DR: Using the DUSTGRAIN-PF and DEMNUni N-body suites, the team measures 3D and projected halo profiles out to 5 r_500c for f(R) gravity, massive neutrinos, and dynamical dark energy, and compares them to ΛCDM. f(R) drives ~10% deviations in low-mass halo outskirts at z=1.1 while neutrinos and w0-wa induce only few-percent shifts, and they estimate that catching such profile differences with Euclid weak lensing needs stacks of ~10⁵ halos at z=1.1 (a few thousand at z≲0.5), arguing these effects must be modelled in any Euclid mass-inference likelihood.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**Morphology of Radio Sources in Representation Space** · Nicolas Baron Perez, Marcus Brüggen, Luisa Lucie-Smith · [arXiv:2609.03779](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03779)
- Applies a self-supervised classifier and clustering-in-representation-space to LoTSS-DR3 (>13M sources), recovering the 12 DR2 morphological classes plus 6 rarer ones (winged, FR-ambiguous, …) and framing it as open-set discovery rather than pointwise anomaly flagging.

**The moving lens effect: analytical modelling and foreground suppression** · Amy Wayland, David Alonso, William Coulton · [arXiv:2609.04133](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04133)
- Builds a bispectrum-based theory of the moving-lens CMB×galaxy-momentum estimator and shows analytically that the leading small-scale astrophysical foreground vanishes after angular averaging in the squeezed limit, leaving a clean spin-1 E-mode signal from the transverse velocity field.

**Consistency between cosmological and standard siren observations in evolving dark energy** · Macarena Lagos, William J. Wolf · [arXiv:2609.04112](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04112)
- Maps a non-minimally-coupled scalar-tensor model that already fits DESI's time-varying dark energy onto GW-friction parametrisations (c_M and Ξ₀, n), finding it consistent with GWTC-5 at ≲1σ but in 2–4σ tension with the conventional c_M×Ω_Λ / w0-wa parametric analysis, signalling that those parametrisations impose implicit priors incompatible with physical scalar-tensor dark energy.

**Prospects for probing dark matter with filamentary 21cm emission** · Yizhou Liu, Yingjie Jing, Haoran Shi et al. · [arXiv:2609.03649](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03649)
- Forecasts that although CDM vs. WDM leaves clear imprints on filament morphology in intrinsic 21cm maps at z=4, SKA1-Low and even SKA2-Low beams (≳6″) wash out the small-scale structure needed to distinguish the models by direct imaging.
