## High priority (≤5)

**LensFactory.jl: A general-purpose strong lens modeling package** · Ashish Kumar Meena · [arXiv:2609.08649](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08649)
- Why it matters: A new open-source strong-lensing modeling framework that scales cluster-scale reconstructions to desktop hardware — directly useful infrastructure for the strong-lensing detection and follow-up work at the top of your list.
- TL;DR: The author releases a Julia package supporting single- and multi-plane strong lensing with a library of analytic profiles and pluggable inference methods, exploiting multiple dispatch and multi-threading so full cluster reconstructions finish in hours on a laptop-class machine. Validation runs recover convergence and magnification for the simulated Ares and Hera clusters and reproduce published results on the JWST-observed cluster SMACS J0723 to 0.46″ image-plane RMS. As a diagnostic they also introduce "singularity maps" that probe higher derivatives of the lensing potential, complementing the usual image-position, time-delay, and flux-ratio observables.

**Harnessing stellar kinematics to constrain dark energy with the double-source-plane gravitational lens SDSS J0946+1006** · Daniel J. Ballard, Wolfgang J. R. Enzi, Thomas E. Collett et al. · [arXiv:2609.08573](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08573)
- Why it matters: A concrete case study of using strong lensing plus complementary data to break degeneracies and constrain cosmology — sits squarely in the strong-gravitational-lensing analysis lane, and shows an approach that will scale to Euclid- and Rubin-era lens samples.
- TL;DR: The team jointly fits HST near-IR/near-UV imaging and VLT-MUSE stellar kinematics of the well-studied double-source-plane lens SDSS J0946+1006, using the deflector velocity dispersion to lift the multi-plane mass-sheet degeneracy that would otherwise spoil a free-cosmology multi-plane model. Combined with Planck they infer w = −1.01(+0.08,−0.13), and with Pantheon w = −0.99(+0.13,−0.15); interestingly, adding this lens to DESI BAO removes the ΛCDM tension seen in other combinations, yielding (w0,wa) = (−0.87,−0.22). They also show that substructure and multipolar halo shape perturbations only modestly shift the inferred cosmology.

**Euclid: Quick Data Release (Q1) — Exploring the detailed visual morphology of galaxies in clusters** · P. Mas-Buitrago, S. Kruk, D. O'Ryan et al. · [arXiv:2609.08962](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08962)
- Why it matters: A double hit — Euclid mission science and a deep-learning foundation model (Zoobot) applied to survey imaging — using Q1 data to measure T–Σ and T–R relations out to 3 R_500c, the kind of Euclid + foundation-model result you're tracking.
- TL;DR: Using the Euclid Q1 visual-morphology catalogue, whose smooth / featured / barred classifications come from the Zoobot deep-learning foundation model, the authors identify 1,754 members across 71 clusters at 0.2 ≤ z ≤ 0.5 and confirm the classical morphology–density and morphology–radius trends, now extended to large cluster-centric distances. Beyond ~1.5 R_500c the morphological segregation weakens and featured-or-disc galaxies overtake smooth ones at the lowest densities, consistent with field interlopers dominating in the cluster outskirts. They also see a tentative drop in the bar fraction toward lower densities, most pronounced for the most massive galaxies.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**Sizing the Universe with DESI Galaxy Sizes: Plain Fundamentals of Fundamental-Plane Lensing** · Kun Xu, Jiaqi Wang, Ravi K. Sheth et al. · [arXiv:2609.09151](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09151)
- Uses Fundamental-Plane size residuals of 3.26M DESI LRGs behind DESI-BGS lenses as a spectroscopic magnification estimator, recovering surface-density profiles at Stage-III shear precision with a very different systematics budget than shape-based cosmic shear.

**Baryonification IV: Constraining baryonic feedback with X-ray gas fractions** · Jozef Bucko, Andrina Nicola, Aurel Schneider et al. · [arXiv:2609.09144](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09144)
- Extends the baryonification framework to forward-model X-ray surface brightness of CHEX-MATE and eFEDs groups/clusters, deriving joint constraints on hot-gas fractions and matter-power-spectrum suppression that suggest strong feedback (6% at k=1, 23% at k=5 h/Mpc).

**Mitigating baryonic effects in weak lensing with higher-order statistics** · Andreas Tersenov, Sacha Guerrini, Jean-Luc Starck et al. · [arXiv:2609.09131](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09131)
- Runs simulation-based inference on cosmoGRID convergence maps to show that starlet peak counts and the starlet ℓ1-norm can still nearly double the power-spectrum figure of merit even after imposing "baryon-safe" scale cuts, and quantifies how the BNT transform localizes baryonic sensitivity.

**TILING I: Field-level Bayesian reconstruction of cosmological initial conditions during the epoch of reionization** · Nikolaos Triantafyllou, Andrei Mesinger, Steven Murray et al. · [arXiv:2609.09102](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09102)
- Introduces a hybrid ML pipeline in which a point estimator seeds a score-based diffusion network that samples the posterior over EoR initial conditions from mock 21cm and galaxy maps, recovering k ≲ 0.2 cMpc⁻¹ modes and even filling in modes lost to 21cm foreground wedges.

**Optical Depths from the Thermal Sunyaev-Zel'dovich Effect with ACT DR6 and DESI DR1 Spectroscopic Galaxies and Optically-Selected Clusters** · J. E. Moore, C. Popik, Y. Gong et al. · [arXiv:2609.08939](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08939)
- Stacks ACT DR6+Planck Compton-y and coadded temperature maps on DESI LRGs, BGS galaxies, and eROMaPPer optical clusters to derive Compton-y–optical-depth scaling relations that agree with pairwise kSZ, delivering the first SZ-derived ȳ–τ̄ calibration for an optically selected sample.

**Why Azimuthal Averaging Works in Halo Lensing: Symmetry and Power Counting for Nonlinear Shear and Magnification** · Keiichi Umetsu · [arXiv:2609.08825](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08825)
- Quantifies, via symmetry arguments and triaxial NFW tests at six mass–redshift grid points, why radial-profile compression in cluster weak lensing keeps sub-2% biases in reduced shear, magnification, and magnification bias — a nice sanity check for stacked cluster WL pipelines.

**Finding the distribution of matter using lenses — II: deconvolution-based reconstruction with 3x2pt measurements** · Jun-Qian Jiang, Ajoy Dawn, Dhiraj Kumar Hazra et al. · [arXiv:2609.08460](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08460)
- Builds a regularized Richardson–Lucy deconvolution over 3x2pt kernels to reconstruct scale-dependent modulations A(k) of the matter power spectrum, showing that Rubin/LSST Y10 mocks can pick out ≳1% oscillatory departures across 0.1 ≲ k ≲ 0.5 Mpc⁻¹ without assuming an emulator.

**The Roman eXtreme Deep Field (RXDF)** · Haojing Yan, Anton M. Koekemoer, Yue Shen et al. · [arXiv:2609.08145](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08145)
- Describes a 386-hour Roman program to reach AB=30 over 678 arcmin² in seven bands, embedded inside the Euclid Ultra Deep Field near the NEP, that will provide the deepest Roman imaging plus a time-domain baseline for reionization, LSS, high-z SMBHs, LRDs, and SNe.
