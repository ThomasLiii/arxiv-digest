## High priority (≤5)

**Beyond the BLUE I: the advantage ceiling - how much can any estimator beat the matched filter in mm/submm survey data?** · Kaustuv Basu · [arXiv:2609.10475](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10475)
- Why it matters: Puts a principled Fisher-information bound on how much a CNN source-extractor can actually gain over a matched filter in survey maps — directly relevant to any ML-based imaging pipeline where teams claim to "beat" classical estimators.
- TL;DR: The author defines a single dimensionless "advantage ceiling" η that quantifies the maximum variance improvement any unbiased estimator can achieve over the matched filter given a noise covariance, and computes it from noise-only simulations before any network is trained. Across a taxonomy of mm/submm noise, Gaussian noise pins η to 1, spectral-tilt covariance mixtures allow up to ~10×, and source confusion — because its non-Gaussian part is the noise itself — gives the largest headroom (η ≥ 3.8 for extended sources). Read as extra integration time, η tells you when hunting for a smarter estimator is worth it and when it is not.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**Learning cosmic web environments with diffusion models** · Mehdi Noor, Tony Bonnaire, Nabila Aghanim et al. · [arXiv:2609.09950](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09950)
- Trains a diffusion model on Quijote N-body sims and shows via Dice coefficients and cross-power spectra that its self-attention maps encode overdensities and cosmic-web environments at intermediate-to-large scales — a step toward interpretable representation learning for LSS.

**Enabling New Discoveries with Machine Learning** · Michelle Lochner · [arXiv:2609.10109](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10109)
- Reviews the Astronomaly active-anomaly-detection framework, recent deep-learning extensions, and the human-in-the-loop workflow for surfacing rare or novel sources in SKA/Rubin-scale surveys.

**Associating binary black holes with galactic centres using lensed gravitational waves** · Laura Uronen, Tian Li, Qiuhan He et al. · [arXiv:2609.10285](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10285)
- Shows that strong-lensing sky localisation combined with Euclid- or HST-class imaging can pin LVK BBH sources to sub-galactic scales and reject light-tracing spatial priors, tying observational strong lensing to BBH formation-channel discrimination.

**Likelihood-free inference with nuisance parameters through normalizing flows** · Phil Assheton · [arXiv:2609.10534](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10534)
- Decomposes a normalizing-flow posterior to expose a near-pivotal test statistic in the presence of nuisance parameters, potentially useful as a simulation-based-inference building block for cosmology analyses that need to marginalize systematics.
