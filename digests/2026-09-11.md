## High priority (≤5)

**Learning JWST. I. A Foundation Model for New Population Discoveries and Morphology-Aware Photometric Redshift Measurements in the JADES Survey** · Jiani Ding, Minghao Yue, Yongda Zhu et al. · [arXiv:2609.11879](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11879)
- Why it matters: A self-supervised multi-band foundation model built on JWST/JADES that does exactly what the AION-1-style multimodal-for-astronomy program targets — a shared embedding space usable for both rare-population discovery and morphology-aware photo-z, with an explicit path to Roman, Euclid, and Rubin/LSST.
- TL;DR: FM-JADES-v1 is trained without labels on 482k JADES DR5 sources, combining multi-band imagery with the photometric catalog into a single embedding space. In blind experiments the embedding surfaces high-z galaxies and Little Red Dots as isolated islands with no prior selection cuts, and a three-band photo-z probe on the learned features reaches σ_NMAD ≈ 0.16 versus 0.44 for template fitting. The authors frame it as a scalable discovery layer for upcoming wide-field surveys and a base for downstream property regression.

**SMART: Spherical Mass ApeRture Toolkit** · Igor Bezmaternykh, Sandrine Pires, Nicolas Dagoneau et al. · [arXiv:2609.11181](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11181)
- Why it matters: Direct methods contribution for weak-lensing analysis on Euclid-scale footprints, bypassing convergence reconstruction and flat-sky projection — the kind of pipeline component that will sit under Stage-IV cluster and cosmology work.
- TL;DR: The authors extend the shear-based aperture-mass formalism to the sphere and release a package that computes spherical aperture-mass maps directly from the shear field, avoiding both planar projections and κ reconstruction. Galaxy-grouping strategies deliver up to ~40× speed-ups over brute force at NSIDE 4096 while improving precision, and in weak-lensing cluster detection the spherical shear-based maps dominate projected shear approaches in purity–completeness with a simpler selection function. Code is on GitHub.

**TDCOSMO XXXI: New techniques in line-of-sight studies of time delay lenses** · Daniel Johnson, Devon Williams, Chris Fassnacht et al. · [arXiv:2609.11652](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11652)
- Why it matters: Core methodology update for strong-lensing time-delay cosmography, and the first standardised weighted-number-counts pipeline built on the Euclid Flagship simulation — sits at the intersection of strong lensing and Euclid mission science.
- TL;DR: κ_ext along each lens's line of sight is a dominant systematic in H0 measurements from time-delay lensing, and this paper reworks the weighted-number-counts machinery to run against the Euclid Flagship Simulation and to break out observer–lens, lens–source, and source-side contributions separately. Applying the updated pipeline to the 11 TDCOSMO 2026 time-delay lenses shifts the median κ_ext from −0.002 to −0.006 and stays within 1σ of prior estimates for 10 of 11 systems. It is presented as the largest standardised line-of-sight analysis of time-delay lenses to date.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**Constraints on the fuzzy dark matter mass using globular clusters in dwarf galaxies from Euclid ERO data** · Lisa Besnard, Pierre Boldrini, Paola Di Matteo et al. · [arXiv:2609.11328](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11328)
- Uses Euclid Early Release Observation globular-cluster candidates in Fornax and Perseus dwarfs to rule out m22 ≲ 1 for fuzzy dark matter via a dynamical-friction orbital-decay analysis, and forecasts sharper limits from later Euclid releases.

**No Detectable One-halo Galactic Conformity Signal with Halo-mass Estimates Consistent with Weak-lensing Constraints** · Dingyi Zhao, Yingjie Peng, Luis C. Ho et al. · [arXiv:2609.11313](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11313)
- Shows in SDSS that the standard AM-based one-halo conformity signal (~6–10σ) vanishes (+0.00±0.03 dex) when halo masses are drawn from an ML SHMR calibrated against weak-lensing constraints, suggesting prior detections trace halo-mass estimation bias.

**Improving Faint Object Detection for Space Situational Awareness with Variational Autoencoders** · Angela Cratere, Luca Ghilardi, Vishnu Reddy et al. · [arXiv:2609.11269](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11269)
- Combines a Tiny-U-Net stellar-mask segmentor with a partial-convolution VAE that inpaints astronomical backgrounds, yielding a preprocessing stage that lifts faint moving-target detectability in real cislunar imagery — a transferable recipe for source removal on structured survey backgrounds.
