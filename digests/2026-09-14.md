## High priority (≤5)

**Probabilistic characterization of blending with LSST and application to cluster lensing cosmology** · Manon Ramel, Cyrille Doux, Marine Kuna, Michel Aguena et al. · [arXiv:2609.12158](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12158)
- Why it matters: A general-purpose deblending diagnostic tuned for Stage-IV static probes: it quantifies match ambiguity between detections and truth catalogs, feeds directly into weak-lensing systematics, and is explicitly designed to plug into overlap analyses with Euclid and Roman reference catalogs.
- TL;DR: The authors define a per-object "blending entropy" $S_b$ over the posterior of assignments between detections and true galaxies, run it through the DESC DC2 simulation, and show that a $S_b < 0.2$ cut removes about a quarter of the LSST galaxy sample near the depth limit — the same population that carries the noisiest shapes and photo-z's. When they propagate that cut through cluster weak-lensing profiles around cosmoDC2 halos, blending-induced mass and $\sigma_8$ biases drop substantially. The framework is presented as a template for using Euclid- or Roman-quality imagery as an external reference to police blending in LSST.

**The Infrared Glow of Galactic Outskirts: A New Window with NASIM** · Elham Saremi, Ignacio Trujillo, Mohammad Akhlaghi, Zohreh Ghaffari et al. · [arXiv:2609.12934](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12934)
- Why it matters: Concrete self-calibration and mosaicking pipeline for VISTA/VIRCAM in the Euclid Deep Field South — exactly the "clean the sky, keep the diffuse light" plumbing that survey imaging teams need alongside Euclid, and the deep NIR view it opens is directly complementary to Rubin/LSST and Euclid work in the same footprint.
- TL;DR: NASIM is a fully automated, reproducible near-infrared low-surface-brightness reduction pipeline built on Gnuastro inside a Maneage workflow. On $K_s$-band VISTA data in the Euclid Deep Field South (KEDFS), it removes large-scale flat-field patterns and per-detector systematics while preserving diffuse emission, reaching $\mu_{K_s} \approx 27.7$ mag arcsec$^{-2}$ at 3$\sigma$ over 100 arcsec$^2$ — roughly 67$\times$ deeper than 2MASS and 11$\times$ deeper than VHS. Comparisons with public VISTA products show the extra depth in the low-surface-brightness regime does not visibly cost compact-source sensitivity.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**Clustering-based halo mass assignment for high-redshift galaxies: method, validation, and application to JWST** · Seunghwan Lim, Sandro Tacchella, Sara Saleh, Roberto Maiolino et al. · [arXiv:2609.12040](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12040)
- Uses two-point clustering in stellar-mass bins, validated on COLIBRE+HOMA, to assign halo masses to JADES galaxies at $z=6$ and $z=10$ with <0.3 dex scatter, and forecasts what a Roman Deep Tier would add for constraining bursty versus non-bursty star formation.

**E-INSPIRE II: Finding relics from wide-sky multi-band surveys — a proof-of-concept ML regression** · Charles Rosen, Chiara Spiniello, John Mills, Alexey Sergeyev et al. · [arXiv:2609.12608](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12608)
- Trains a Support Vector Regression on ~430 ultra-compact massive galaxies to reconstruct a spectroscopic "degree of relicness" from photometric and structural features, and shows a DoR$\geq0.6$ cut yields a clean, high-purity relic-candidate selector for future wide-area surveys — a workable pre-selection recipe for rare-object hunts in survey imaging.

**Impact of galaxy intrinsic alignments on non-Gaussian weak lensing statistics for modified gravity** · Mehar Chawla, Christopher T. Davies, Joachim Harnois-Déraps et al. · [arXiv:2609.12038](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12038)
- On MGLenS mocks with a non-linear linear-alignment IA model, lensing void profiles and abundance emulators improve Stage-IV constraints on $S_8$–$f_{R0}$ and $S_8$–$H_0 r_c$ by up to an order of magnitude over two-point functions, but unmodeled IA can bias cosmology while leaving the modified-gravity sector largely intact.

**The Role of Big Bang Nucleosynthesis in Joint Cosmological Analyses** · Mehmet Akharman, Nicholas DePorzio, Cara Giovanetti, Hongwan Liu · [arXiv:2609.12065](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12065)
- Runs Planck + DESI DR2 BAO+BBN and BAO+BBN+CMB joint fits with BBN nuisance parameters marginalized for the first time, quantifies how investigator choices around BBN shift the inferred $h$ and $N_\text{eff}$, and lays out a pipeline that keeps BBN prediction uncertainties honest in joint analyses.

**Identification of Cosmic Chronometers in the GAMA Survey** · Laura J. Hunt, Kevin A. Pimbblet, David M. Benoit · [arXiv:2609.12711](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12711)
- Assembles a 231-galaxy pure-passive cosmic-chronometer sample from GAMA between $0.03<z<0.4$ using NUVrJ colours, Balmer/OII/Ca H&K spectral features and stellar-mass cuts, and studies how sample purity feeds into $H(z)$ and $H_0$ statistical uncertainty.
