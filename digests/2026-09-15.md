## High priority (≤5)

**Gnuastro: removing extended/diffuse outliers while coadding** · Akhlaghi, Vives Arias, Renard, Vázquez Ramió et al. · [arXiv:2609.15529](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15529v1)
- Why it matters: Directly targets image-stacking / mosaicking methodology, one of the tracked high-priority topics.
- TL;DR: A new Gnuastro 0.23 release adds arithmetic operators that use every exposure jointly to flag diffuse outliers by exploiting pixel contiguity, so leftover artifacts (satellite trails, scattered light) can be masked before the final coadd. The result note is packaged as a reproducible Maneage build.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**MUSHY: Multimodal Flow-Based Amortized Bayesian Inference for Spectroscopic Data Fusion** · Jeff Shen · [arXiv:2609.15945](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15945v1)
- A rectified-flow model takes spectra from multiple surveys (APOGEE, GALAH, DESI) as separate modalities and learns a well-calibrated posterior over stellar physical parameters, with fusion outperforming any single-survey input on synthetic ATLAS12/SYNTHE benchmarks.

**PYRA and MYTHRA: new statistical analysis for image reconstruction** · Drevon, Abello, Millour, Meilland et al. · [arXiv:2609.15365](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15365v1)
- Ensembles of MiRA reconstructions are swept over hyperparameters and then aggregated statistically, delivering an averaged image plus uncertainty and winning the 2024 Interferometric Imaging Contest — a general recipe for pushing sparse-Fourier imaging past its parameter-choice sensitivity.
