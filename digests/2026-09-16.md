## High priority (≤5)

**Pushing HST to the limit: Detection completeness and morphology robustness of faint galaxies in the EGS field** · Jia, Zhang, Wang et al. · [arXiv:2609.16982](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16982v1)
- Why it matters: Directly addresses detection completeness and blending on deep survey imagery, with an explicit hand-off to Euclid, CSST, and Roman — the surveys and tasks you track.
- TL;DR: The authors run mock galaxy-pair injection into a CANDELS/EGS F160W mosaic and cross-match with CEERS to quantify how faint sources are missed, how Sérsic morphology parameters bias under blending, and how to separate real faint galaxies from spurious detections. They present the framework as a template that can be replayed on upcoming Euclid, CSST, and Roman fields, and derive an empirical criterion for cleaning faint catalogs before statistical galaxy-evolution work.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**Cosmological information from field-level analysis in redshift space** · Kokron, Nishimichi, Baumann et al. · [arXiv:2609.16719](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16719v1)
- Field-level EFT forward-modeling of a DESI LRG-like mock quantifies how much extra cosmological precision is actually available beyond the joint power spectrum + bispectrum in the perturbative regime.

**The Equivalence Principle in the Dark Sector in light of DESI** · Perivolaropoulos, Skara, Nesseris · [arXiv:2609.17423](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17423v1)
- Fitting DESI DR1 BAO+FS (and DR2 BAO) jointly with CMB, a long-range dark-sector force at ~0.5% of gravity relieves the mild Λ-CDM tension at ~2.5σ, providing a concrete dark-sector target for clustering analyses.

**Assessing ionospheric effects on image quality in the LOFAR Decametre Sky Survey** · de Gasperin, Groeneveld, Edler et al. · [arXiv:2609.17382](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17382v1)
- Three overlapping LoDeSS pointings observed under different ionospheres are used to measure the diffractive scale, decorrelation time, and transfer angle of direction-dependent calibration solutions — quantitative inputs for self-calibration pipelines at decametre wavelengths.

**Optimal exposure time for satellite imaging? A trade-off between residual tip-tilt and registration accuracy** · Ferrari, Fétick, Mugnier et al. · [arXiv:2609.17023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17023v1)
- Derives an analytical optimum for ground-based exposure time that balances speckle freezing against SNR-limited registration jitter after post-processing, applicable well beyond satellite imaging to any short-exposure AO/lucky pipeline.

**From Foundation Embeddings to Cropland Maps: Label Efficiency, Temporal Transferability and Independent Human Validation** · Estes, Elmes, Ye et al. · [arXiv:2609.17138](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17138v1)
- Frozen AlphaEarth geospatial-foundation-model embeddings feed a linear classifier that reaches 93.7% accuracy on binary cropland mapping in Maine, a clean case study of frozen-encoder-plus-linear-probe on satellite imagery relevant to the AION-1 style of transfer.
