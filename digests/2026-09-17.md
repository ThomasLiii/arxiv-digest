## High priority (≤5)

**Galaxy-Galaxy Strong Lensing simulation with the GPU acceleration across surveys and multi-bands** · Fucheng Zhong et al. · [arXiv:2609.18180](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18180v1)
- Why it matters: A GPU/PyTorch, fully auto-differentiable strong-lensing image simulator that jointly targets KiDS, LSST, and Euclid observing conditions and is exercised end-to-end with a deep learning deblender — squarely on the strong-lensing-with-ML / Euclid axis.
- TL;DR: The authors build a tensor-based simulator that renders galaxy-galaxy strong lenses with matched SEDs, PSFs, and photometric systems for multiple surveys, and use it to train and evaluate a neural network for deblending lensed sources. Because everything runs on GPUs with autodiff, the pipeline is ~10³× faster than CPU baselines and can plug into gradient-based lens modeling rather than only forward-only training data generation.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**Follow-up of SN 2025wny VI: The Rate and Detectable Population of Strongly Lensed SLSNe-I in ZTF** · Jacob O. Hjortlund et al. · [arXiv:2609.18912](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18912v1)
- Why it matters: First confirmed strongly lensed superluminous supernova; forward-modeled discovery rate is directly useful context for lensed-transient detection strategies in ZTF and successors.
- TL;DR: A ZTF-tuned forward simulation shows one lensed SLSN-I in seven years is consistent with predictions, and after correcting for magnification and Malmquist bias SN 2025wny sits near the top of the luminosity function; its wide 4.9″ image separation hints that wide-separation lensed-transient rates are underestimated.

**Void probability function in the Quijote simulations** · Snehasish Bhattacharjee · [arXiv:2609.18221](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18221v1)
- Why it matters: A large-scale-structure summary statistic evaluated across ΛCDM extensions in a widely used simulation suite — a low-cost probe for upcoming galaxy surveys.
- TL;DR: The VPF of Quijote haloes and galaxies is shown to be sensitive to massive neutrinos, modified gravity, and primordial non-Gaussianity, and to shift between geometric-hierarchical and negative-binomial regimes with redshift, dilution, and galaxy morphology.

**Characterization of the CSST Survey Camera CCDs: I. basic electro-optical performance** · Zun Luo et al. · [arXiv:2609.19105](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19105v1)
- Why it matters: CSST is a peer of Euclid/SPHEREx for wide-field multiband imaging; the measured detector response feeds directly into image simulations and calibration priors relevant to survey-imaging methods.
- TL;DR: Ground testing of all 31 flight CCDs yields a full performance baseline (>80 ke full well, ~3.6 e read noise, <0.02 e/pix/s dark current, >99.9994% CTE, high QE over 255–1000 nm), all within spec and intended as inputs to the CSST image-simulation and pipeline stack.

**The Spitzer Data Fusion — A Far-Ultraviolet to Far-Infrared Multi-Wavelength Database in Spitzer Extragalactic Survey Fields** · Mattia Vaccari · [arXiv:2609.18694](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18694v1)
- Why it matters: A ready-to-use FUV–FIR photometric and photo-z catalog over Spitzer legacy fields explicitly designed as a bridge to Euclid and Rubin survey work.
- TL;DR: The release band-merges 4.4M IRAC-selected sources over 65 deg² (plus a deeper 2.8M-source SERVS companion) with matched photometric and spectroscopic redshifts, distributed via Zenodo and VizieR for photo-z calibration, SED fitting, and cross-matching.
