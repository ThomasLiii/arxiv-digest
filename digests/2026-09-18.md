## High priority (≤5)

**Euclid: Galaxy cluster detection through the weak lensing effect — algorithm assessment and selection** · A. Manjón-García, G. Leroy, S. Pires et al. · [arXiv:2609.20571](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20571v1)
- Why it matters: Blind head-to-head of nine cluster-finding algorithms on Euclid-like weak-lensing simulations — directly a Euclid methodology paper on weak-lensing survey imaging.
- TL;DR: The team runs nine cluster-detection pipelines over 1200 deg² of DEMNUni-Cov mocks matched to Euclid's expected depth, matches recoveries to synthetic clusters above S/N > 2, and down-selects four complementary methods for the upcoming Euclid data releases. The paper reports how completeness and purity trade against z–M selection and matching choices, and lays groundwork for a combined detection strategy.

**Physical properties of star-forming clumps in nearby galaxies from CLAUDS and HSC-SSP** · Jürgen J. Popp, Hugh Dickinson, Stephen Serjeant et al. · [arXiv:2609.20079](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20079v1)
- Why it matters: A Faster R-CNN object detector trained on wide-field HSC-SSP imaging producing a 710k-clump catalog — a concrete CNN-on-survey-imaging pipeline at scale.
- TL;DR: The authors run FRCNN over ~210,000 low-z galaxies in the HSC-SSP Wide/Deep + CLAUDS fields and extract ~710,000 giant star-forming clumps with (u)grizy photometry. Fitting SED models yields stellar mass, age, dust, and SFR per clump; the young-clump stellar mass function follows a power law (α ≈ 1.98) consistent with in-situ formation via violent disk instabilities.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**Probing submillimeter number counts below the confusion limit: extreme-value statistics of the P(D) distribution and its modulation by gravitational lensing** · Kaustuv Basu, Andrea Guerrero, Frank Bertoldi · [arXiv:2609.19689](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19689v1)
- Why it matters: Novel forward-model-free statistic on submm survey maps whose lensing modulation directly reads out count curvature — a fresh handle on lensed-galaxy population statistics.
- TL;DR: Peaks-over-threshold applied to Planck 857 GHz and Herschel/SPIRE 350 μm P(D) tails constrains the count slope and its lensing-induced curvature without an instrument forward model.

**Predictions on the abundance of primordial black holes: results from the SMILE VLBI milli-lensing sample** · Xinyue Jiang, Shuo Cao, Marek Biesiada et al. · [arXiv:2609.20329](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20329v1)
- Why it matters: A concrete large-sample milli-lensing pipeline forecast tightening intermediate-mass PBH bounds by ~10× — lensing-detection methodology on radio survey data.
- TL;DR: Assuming no confirmed milli-lensing among ~5000 SMILE flat-spectrum radio sources, the projected 95% upper limit on f_PBH in the 10⁶–10⁹ M⊙ range reaches ≲0.12%, an order of magnitude tighter than prior VLBI constraints.

**Constraints on the Pop III Sky Surface Brightness from High-Redshift Caustic Transients in MACS0416** · Jakob P. Perivolotis, Pietro Bergamini, Rogier A. Windhorst et al. · [arXiv:2609.19757](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19757v1)
- Why it matters: Strong-lensing caustic search in a cluster field with an updated lens model — the classic recipe for finding highly magnified transients at the edge of detectability.
- TL;DR: Difference imaging across three MACS J0416 epochs, guided by an updated strong-lensing model of the caustics for 7 ≤ z ≤ 17, yields no new caustic transits and tightens the null-result bound on the Pop III sky surface brightness at high redshift.

**Topological Signatures of AGN Feedback in the Simba Simulations** · Ryusei R. Kano, Romeel Davé, Tsutomu T. Takeuchi et al. · [arXiv:2609.19993](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19993v1)
- Why it matters: Persistent-homology summary statistics for large-scale structure that pick up feedback signatures the 2-point function misses — a complementary handle for survey-scale analyses.
- TL;DR: TDA (Betti curves, persistence diagrams, 2-Wasserstein distance) on Simba galaxy catalogs isolates AGN-feedback-driven changes in loop-scale topology at 0.1–0.4 h⁻¹ Mpc, strongest for satellites in quenched ≳10¹² M⊙ halos.

**Offline vs Real-Time Validation and Performance Assessment of Heimdall Detection Pipeline for Fast Radio Bursts** · Valentina Cesare, Giovanni Naldi, Francesco Fiori et al. · [arXiv:2609.20635](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20635v1)
- Why it matters: Practical benchmark of a real-time single-pulse detection pipeline on Northern Cross data — directly relevant to scaling transient-detection stacks to SKA-era data rates.
- TL;DR: Heimdall is exercised in offline and PSRDADA ring-buffer real-time modes on 16-cylinder Northern Cross data to compare detection performance and resource use ahead of SKA-class throughput.
