## High priority (≤5)

**Data-driven Galaxy Population Prior for Photometric Redshifts** · Nikolas Frediani, Daniel Gruen, Luca Tortorelli et al. · [arXiv:2609.26594](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26594)
- Why it matters: Photo-z priors are the accuracy bottleneck for Stage-IV weak-lensing cosmology (Euclid in particular), and this paper attacks the SED/luminosity/redshift prior itself with a data-driven model rather than the usual template shortcuts.
- TL;DR: The authors learn the joint galaxy population prior over SEDs, luminosities, and redshifts directly from data, replacing the ad-hoc template libraries and Gaussian mixtures that current photo-z pipelines lean on. They argue this prior can be fed into standard photo-z inference and thereby close roughly the order-of-magnitude gap between existing photo-z error models and what Stage-IV surveys need for weak lensing.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**Fitting Moving Objects in Up-The-Ramp Data with Applications to the Roman Space Telescope and JWST** · Timothy D. Brandt · [arXiv:2609.25632](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25632)
- Derives the per-pixel likelihood for a point source traversing a nondestructively read detector and shows how to jointly fit its trajectory and flux — a self-calibration-flavored primitive for Roman and JWST imaging that is also directly applicable to Euclid-class wide-field ramp data.

**A Multi-Mission Detection Framework for High-Energy Transients: Application to a 13-year Fermi/GBM and Swift/BAT Sample** · Ariel Perera, Barak Zackay, Tejaswi Venumadhav · [arXiv:2609.25672](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25672)
- Combines Fermi/GBM and Swift/BAT streams into a joint significance test that pushes below the single-instrument detection floor, an anomaly/rare-transient discovery template that transfers naturally to combining survey imagers.

**Bayesian Galaxy Asymmetry** · Mathieu Perron-Cormier, Kristine Spekkens, Nathan Deg et al. · [arXiv:2609.26472](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26472)
- Recasts the squared-difference galaxy-asymmetry statistic as a Bayesian posterior on 21 cm data, recovering meaningful values in the low-SNR regime where the existing noise correction fails and giving usable uncertainties for morphological population studies.
