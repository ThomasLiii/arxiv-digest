## High priority (≤5)

**SN Helios: A Multiply Imaged Type II Supernova Opening Time-Delay Cosmography beyond redshift of 3** · Seiji Fujimoto, Conor Larison, Justin D. R. Pierel et al. · [arXiv:2609.30440](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30440)
- Why it matters: A fresh strong-lensing detection with a multiply imaged supernova at z=3.34 behind a cluster, opening H0 time-delay cosmography from a genuinely high-z source — directly on the strong-lensing detection interest.
- TL;DR: The authors identify a red F150W-dropout transient in JWST/NIRCam VENUS-survey imaging of RXC J0018.5+1626, then confirm it with second-epoch NIRCam and a NIRSpec prism spectrum showing a broad H-alpha line with P-Cygni absorption that classifies it as a Type II supernova at z=3.34. Cluster lens models predict magnifications of ~10-20 and a companion image reappearing in 2-6 years, which would deliver a rare time-delay H0 measurement anchored beyond z=3, and the host is undetected across deep rest-UV/optical, emission-line, and ALMA continuum data.

**Breaking the AGB Color Degeneracy with Variability and H2O: A Consistent Picture of AGB Chemistry, Dust, and the J-Region Across Environments** · Caroline D. Huang, Massimo Marengo, Antonio Capodagli et al. · [arXiv:2609.30551](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30551)
- Why it matters: A concrete SPHEREx science application — using SPHEREx low-resolution spectroscopy to break AGB C/O photometric ambiguity, which feeds the JAGB distance ladder relevant to the H0 program.
- TL;DR: Combining SPHEREx spectroscopy of LMC AGB stars with JWST/NIRCam photometry and HST time-series in the SN Ia host M101, the authors build an F182M index that resolves the 1.9 micron H2O feature and cleanly separates O-rich from C-rich AGB stars that are degenerate in broadband colors. They show that pulsation and infrared dust excess are asymmetrically coupled — nearly every dusty star is variable, but many variables show little dust — and that M101 O-rich AGB variables run ~0.7 mag redder than LMC analogs in dust-sensitive colors, implying environment-dependent circumstellar extinction that biases JAGB distances if uncorrected.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**Selective state space model for photon energy estimation in microwave kinetic inductance detectors** · Benjamin A. Mazin · [arXiv:2609.30382](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30382)
- TL;DR: Venom replaces the standard coordinate-transform + Wiener optimal filter for MKID pulse-height estimation with a compact 3,314-parameter Mamba selective state-space model that operates directly on raw IQ timestreams and targets on-FPGA deployment.

**FPCA-Enhanced Simulation-Based Inference for Robust Type Ia Supernova Cosmology** · Moonzarin Reza, Lifan Wang · [arXiv:2609.30312](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30312)
- TL;DR: Swapping SALT2 light-curve summaries for functional-PCA components inside a simulation-based inference pipeline yields SN-Ia cosmology constraints comparable to explicit-likelihood analyses but noticeably more robust on out-of-domain light curves, and applied to DES-Y5 recovers the collaboration's Omega_m/Omega_Lambda to within 0.12-0.23 sigma.

**Tails and Trails: Comets and NEOs Survive Co-addition in Rubin Early Data Preview 2** · Colin Orion Chandler, Joseph Murtagh, Ian Chow et al. · [arXiv:2609.30361](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30361)
- TL;DR: The team mines Rubin EDP2 for shallow coadd regions built from one or two visits, then uses the Ponder/Sorcha orchestrator on the Rubin Science Platform to locate known comets and NEOs whose extended morphology survives stacking, delivering 59 activity detections across 32 objects (including newly recognized activity in 2025 NC6) and hundreds of NEO trail cutouts as an early demonstration of what DP2 will enable.

**Self-Supervised Representation Learning: From Spectral Foundation Models to Auroral Emission Spectra** · Matthieu Le Lain, Gael Cessateur, Sebastien Lefevre · [arXiv:2609.31206](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31206)
- TL;DR: The paper pretrains a 1D masked-autoencoder Vision Transformer on 223k unlabelled auroral spectra and benchmarks whether existing astronomy spectral foundation models (SpectraFM in the IR, SpecFormer in the optical) transfer, finding that transfer tracks the spectral window and that in-domain self-supervised pretraining matches expert-engineered features and beats a supervised baseline with 10% of the labels.

**FOTO: Figure-Level Semantic Search for Astronomy** · Hurum Maksora Tohfa, Francisco Villaescusa-Navarro · [arXiv:2609.30536](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30536)
- TL;DR: FOTO indexes 482,750 figure titles/captions from 53,839 astro-ph papers with a locally-runnable 110M-parameter embedding model and uses an LLM only to verify shortlist figures, more than doubling recall@20 over Pathfinder and Semantic Scholar on figure-level queries while avoiding hallucinated references.
