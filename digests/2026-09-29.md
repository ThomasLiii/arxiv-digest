## High priority (≤5)

**Improving Lens Modelling from Ground-Based Imaging with Deconvolution** · Galan et al. · [arXiv:2609.34305](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34305)
- Why it matters: Direct hit on strong lensing detection and modelling — applies the STARRED wavelet deconvolution to HSC-SSP ground-based imaging so the ~1000-lens HSC sample can be modelled without waiting for space-based follow-up.
- TL;DR: The authors preprocess seven HSC-like mock lens systems with starlet-regularised deconvolution, sharpening the effective PSF from ~0.6" to ~0.15" before feeding the images into standard lens modelling. On these mocks they recover the Einstein radius and axis ratio to sub-3% precision, arguing the pipeline can unlock accurate lensing measurements from the full HSC sample.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**Diverse Metallicities in Protoclusters at z ~ 3.4 detected by Deep Learning** · Toshikawa et al. · [arXiv:2609.35471](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35471)
- Why it matters: Downstream science of a deep-learning survey detector (PCFNet) applied to protocluster identification, with follow-up validating both the ML predictions and the resulting environmental measurements.

**Field-level inference of growth rate from DESI BGS galaxy and ZTF supernova simulations** · Ravoux et al. · [arXiv:2609.34508](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34508)
- Why it matters: Large-scale-structure cosmology using a joint density-plus-peculiar-velocity field-level likelihood on realistic DESI BGS + ZTF SN simulations, projecting a ~10% fσ8 constraint from the combination.

**KiDS-DR5: Exploring dust transport in dark matter halos of galaxies** · Wang et al. · [arXiv:2609.34615](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34615)
- Why it matters: Weak-lensing survey analysis on KiDS-DR5 that jointly models galaxy–galaxy lensing signals and multiband magnitude shifts to disentangle halo mass from circumgalactic dust.
