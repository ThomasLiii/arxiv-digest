## High priority (≤5)

**LUMA: A CNN for Strong Gravitational Lens Searches in Astronomical Imaging** · G. V. Donatiello, A. A. Nucita, F. De Paolis, et al. · [arXiv:2609.36857](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36857v1)
- Why it matters: Direct hit on the strong-lensing-detection-with-ML high-priority interest, and the authors flag transformer-based follow-ups as the next step.
- TL;DR: The authors pair a physics-motivated arc/ring enhancement preprocessor with a compact three-block CNN and train it with class reweighting on simulated survey images. On held-out simulations they report ~96% accuracy and ROC AUC ≈ 0.99, with high completeness and purity for lens candidates, arguing that even lightweight CNNs are a viable baseline before scaling up to real data and transformers.

**An Ionized Superstructure at Cosmic Dawn Revealed by a Foundation Model for Astrophysical Research** · M. Yue, Y. Zhu, J. Ding, et al. · [arXiv:2609.36656](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36656v1)
- Why it matters: An astrophysics foundation model (AION-1-adjacent) is used to make a genuine discovery in JWST data, which is exactly the multimodal-foundation-models-for-astronomy target.
- TL;DR: A foundation model trained on JWST datasets flags a z=7.567 galaxy that shows strong Lyα transmission at z≈6.80 extending over ~210 comoving Mpc, implying a hydrogen neutral fraction of ~2×10⁻⁶ inside an epoch where the cosmic mean is ~0.4. The authors find no matching galaxy overdensity, in tension with inside-out reionization pictures, and pitch the result as the first previously-unknown astrophysical phenomenon uncovered by deep learning.

**A Full-Sky SPHEREx Map of Integrated 3.3 and 3.4 μm PAH Emission** · R. Cepeda-Arroita, C. Dickinson, J. A. Rubiño-Martín, et al. · [arXiv:2609.37878](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37878v1)
- Why it matters: First full-sky PAH product from SPHEREx QR2 data — squarely inside the SPHEREx-mission-methods high-priority bucket, and a reusable public data release.
- TL;DR: Using SPHEREx QR2 Level-2 spectra, the team builds inverse-variance-weighted HEALPix maps (Nside=4096, 2.6' FWHM) of the 3.3 μm aromatic and 3.4–3.5 μm aliphatic PAH bands after zodiacal subtraction and PSF-based point-source removal. Per-pixel band fits deliver the first all-sky spectrally-resolved PAH tracers, directly targeting the smallest grains that broadband proxies only reach indirectly.

**Morphological Identification of Dynamical Regimes in MHD Turbulence with ScaleAware-JEPA** · M. Zhao, G.-X. Li, K. Qiu · [arXiv:2609.36833](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36833v1)
- Why it matters: A JEPA-style self-supervised representation applied to astrophysical imaging maps — the same family of techniques as AION-1 and other astronomy foundation models.
- TL;DR: The authors train ScaleAware-JEPA on gas-density morphology alone and show that its multiscale latent coordinates separate clump-, filament-, and diffuse-like regions along an ordered turbulent-velocity-scaling axis. With velocity and magnetic fields held out during training, MHD diagnostics fall on coherent regions of the learned latent atlas, arguing that morphology-only representation learning captures dynamical state beyond simple density thresholding.

**Detecting HI Self-Absorption using Neural Networks** · E. G. M. Muller, N. M. McClure-Griffiths, H. Nguyen, et al. · [arXiv:2609.38042](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38042v1)
- Why it matters: CNN applied directly to 21-cm survey data with SKA-scale throughput — a concrete instance of the "CNNs applied to survey imaging" high-priority theme.
- TL;DR: A lightweight CNN, trained on synthetic emission spectra, flags HI self-absorption features and infers their velocities without needing an emission model or ancillary tracers, reaching ~96–97% accuracy/precision/recall in held-out tests. On real Riegel–Crutcher and Galactic-plane data it reproduces the spatial and velocity structure of known cold-HI features, at ~15,000 spectra/second on a laptop GPU — fast enough for real-time processing at SKA-era data rates.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**Weak Lensing Measurements from the Lyman-α Forest** · A. Slosar · [arXiv:2609.36949](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36949v1)
- Why it matters: Direct hit on weak-lensing methodology, applied to a new cosmological tracer (Lyα forest with DESI DR1).
- TL;DR: A quadratic estimator on DESI DR1 forest and quasar–forest correlations, projected onto galaxy/quasar deflection templates, yields a combined lensing amplitude A_L = 0.62 ± 0.33 — consistent with both ΛCDM and null, i.e. the current data are not yet decisive.

**A halo-based intrinsic-alignment model for simulation-based inference** · M. Gatti · [arXiv:2609.37254](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37254v1)
- Why it matters: An IA model specifically built for field-level and SBI weak-lensing analyses, addressing one of the harder nuisances in that pipeline.
- TL;DR: A halo-based IA prescription with separate central/satellite components matches FLAMINGO intrinsic shapes across two-point and higher-order statistics better than NLA-family models, retains most of its power in a portable four-parameter form, and transfers to an independent N-body run.

**Cross-correlating Squared kSZ and HI Intensity Fields: A Map-level ACT–MeerKAT Forecast** · Y.-E. Jiang, Y. Liu, Y. Gong, et al. · [arXiv:2609.36863](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36863v1)
- Why it matters: An LSS-style map-level cross-correlation forecast between two live surveys, relevant to the large-scale structure / clustering interest.
- TL;DR: Mock ACT-plus-MeerKAT map-level analysis of a Jiutian-1G lightcone predicts a cumulative S/N of ~3–4 for the kSZ²×HI² cross-correlation once realistic beams, ILC noise, and MeerKAT scanning are folded in.

**Discovery of the Lyman-α Forest in a Star-Forming Galaxy at z ≈ 7.57: Implications for the Timing and Topology of Cosmic Reionization** · J. M. Helton, J. Witstok, M. Laska, et al. · [arXiv:2609.38080](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38080v1)
- Why it matters: Companion observational paper to the foundation-model discovery above; relevant to the LSS/reionization side of the medium-priority cosmology bucket.
- TL;DR: JWST NIRSpec+NIRCam data reveal ~7σ transmission of the Lyα forest through a massive z≈7.57 galaxy, implying an unusually transparent sightline (x_HI ≪ 0.1%) coincident with high-z overdensities and suggesting luminous galaxies may contribute more to the ionizing-photon budget than usually assumed.
