## High priority (≤5)

**Stacked strong and weak lensing united: Improved measurement of the stellar and dark matter distributions in massive early-type galaxies at $z\sim 0.5$** · Momoka Fujikawa, Masahiro Takada · [arXiv:2609.39040](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39040v1)
- Why it matters: Directly combines strong and weak lensing on Subaru HSC imaging — strong lensing detection and precision survey-imaging lensing analyses are the top-priority topic.
- TL;DR: The authors stack weak lensing profiles from HSC with enclosed-mass measurements from 13 strong lens systems to pin down the central dark matter and stellar density profiles of massive ellipticals at z≈0.5. They recover an NFW-consistent inner halo, a bottom-heavy IMF, and a near-isothermal total slope, and show the stacked combination tightly constrains an internal mass-sheet transformation parameter. The result is pitched as a general recipe for joint strong+weak lensing of galaxy populations.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**An LSST-DESC Precursor Project: Hyper Suprime-Cam Year 1 $3\times2$pt in Harmonic Space** · D. Sanchez-Cid et al. · [arXiv:2609.39603](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39603v1)
- First end-to-end run of LSST-DESC's harmonic-space 3×2pt pipeline on HSC Y1 (cosmic shear, galaxy clustering, galaxy-galaxy lensing) with mode deprojection against observing systematics, reproducing the official HSC cosmic shear cosmology and extending to joint ΛCDM / wCDM constraints.

**Effects of a central dark matter core on time-delay cosmography with galaxy clusters** · Yuting Liu, Masamune Oguri · [arXiv:2609.39796](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39796v1)
- Re-analyses two cluster-scale strong-lens time-delay systems (SDSS J1004+4112 and the Refsdal supernova in MACS J1149) with a cored NFW main halo, finding that H₀ and the inferred core size are sensitive to how the BCG stellar mass is modeled.

**Is DESI Seeing Dynamical Dark Energy, or a Cosmic Glitch in Gravity?** · Robin Y. Wen et al. · [arXiv:2609.40176](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40176v1)
- Shows that a one-parameter model letting the cosmological gravitational coupling differ from Newton's G (G_cosmo/G_N = 0.9920 ± 0.0025) fits Planck+ACT+SPT+DESI DR2 as well as the two-parameter w₀wₐ dynamical dark energy, and uniquely predicts DESI distances from CMB alone.

**Two bits about lossy compression: On the limits of compression in cosmology** · Hurum Maksora Tohfa, Lehman H. Garrison · [arXiv:2609.40239](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40239v1)
- Benchmarks SZ3 against an autoregressive-transformer neural compressor on cosmological fields including weak-lensing convergence maps, showing the neural coder reaches 9–27% below the Gaussian-optimal rate and transfers across fields without retraining.

**A machine learning-based method for populating dark matter halos in N-body simulations with substructure** · Milad Noorikuhani, Jeremy Tinker · [arXiv:2609.40066](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40066v1)
- Introduces a decision-tree classifier plus nearest-neighbor subhalo transplant that copies substructure from a high-resolution N-body box into lower-resolution ones, cheaply producing many realizations with resolved subhalos for covariance work.

**When Streams Curve Away: a Test of Dark Matter from Extragalactic Stellar Stream Populations** · Nathaniel Starkman et al. · [arXiv:2609.40057](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40057v1)
- Proposes a model-independent geometric statistic (fraction of "convex" stream segments curving away from the host centre) that lets upcoming survey catalogues of extragalactic streams discriminate CDM vs SIDM halo shapes at up to 5σ without per-stream orbit modeling.
