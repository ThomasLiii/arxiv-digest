## High priority

**Weighing Galaxies Inside-Out: Small-Scale Lensing and the Stellar Mass Problem** · Divya Rana, Henk Hoekstra, Konrad Kuijken et al. · [arXiv:2610.01541v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01541v1)
- Why it matters: Direct forecast for Euclid wide-survey weak lensing — hits both "Euclid mission science" and "strong/weak gravitational lensing" from the high-priority list.
- TL;DR: The authors build realistic mocks of a DESI-like Bright Galaxy Survey inside the Euclid wide area and show that small-scale galaxy-galaxy lensing can constrain the matter distribution inside galaxies, probing the stellar initial mass function. Foreground lens light introduces strong scale-dependent biases in source detection and shape measurement; subtracting it with Galfit shrinks those biases by nearly an order of magnitude down to a few half-light radii, yielding roughly 8% constraints on a stellar-mass "mismatch" parameter.

**Weak-Lensing Shear Response for Photometric Redshift-Based Tomographic Binning** · Xiangchong Li, Tianqing Zhang, Rachel Mandelbaum et al. · [arXiv:2610.00999v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00999v1)
- Why it matters: Couples weak lensing shear calibration with photo-z tomography (two named interests) and uses Rubin Data Preview 1 — i.e., survey-imaging methodology at the frontier.
- TL;DR: Tomographic weak lensing bins are built from noisy photo-z estimates, and the correlation between those photo-zs and shear induces redshift-dependent selection biases that distort the measured growth of structure. The authors extend the AnaCal analytical self-calibration framework to propagate shear responses through the photo-z selection, dropping the usual dependence on external image simulations, and validate the AnaCal-based photo-zs on Rubin DP1 data where they match the quality of standard pipelines.

**Radio Interferometric Calibration with the Exponential Map** · L. Cabral, M. Fernandez-Corazza, G. Gancio et al. · [arXiv:2610.01895v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01895v1)
- Why it matters: Self-calibration is explicitly on the high-priority list; this is a new, fast ML estimator for interferometer gains validated on real data.
- TL;DR: Antenna gains in a radio interferometer sit on a Lie group, so the authors build a maximum-likelihood calibrator that parameterizes gain perturbations via the exponential map and estimates them in that geometry. The estimator reaches the Cramér-Rao lower bound in simulation and runs about 40× faster than an Expectation-Maximization baseline, and the authors apply it to real Submillimeter Array observations of the protoplanetary disk AS 209.

## Medium priority

**Bayesian Image Reconstruction with Spatially Variant PSFs in X-ray Astronomy** · Vincent Eberle, Matteo Guardiani, Margret Westerkamp et al. · [arXiv:2610.02062v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02062v1)
- TL;DR: Information-field-theory deconvolution with a patch-based spatially variant PSF outperforms Richardson-Lucy variants on synthetic data and is demonstrated on Chandra Cassiopeia A observations.

**Spurious sources in high-resolution VLA surveys** · Eric F. Jiménez-Andrade, Emmanuel Momjian, Eric J. Murphy et al. · [arXiv:2610.02028v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02028v1)
- TL;DR: Using the GOODS-N 10 GHz mosaic, the authors trace the excess of spurious sources in sub-arcsecond VLA continuum images to residual dirty-beam sidelobes from sparse uv coverage, not to primary-beam effects or Gaussian noise statistics.

**Cosmological inference from a joint DESI DR1 full-shape power spectrum and bispectrum analysis** · Caroline Guandalin, Prakhar Bansal, Pedro Carrilho et al. · [arXiv:2610.01836v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01836v1)
- TL;DR: A one-loop power spectrum plus tree-level bispectrum EFT-of-LSS analysis of DESI DR1 LRGs and quasars tightens the cold dark matter density and matter-fluctuation amplitude by 9-20% over the power spectrum alone.

**Non-Gaussian Parameter Bias: Formalism and Validation** · Nikolina Šarčević, Matthijs van der Wild, Elena Sellentin · [arXiv:2610.02104v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02104v1)
- TL;DR: A DALI-based framework analytically maps systematic data mismatches to non-Gaussian parameter shifts and posterior deformations, agreeing with MCMC to a fraction of a percent in a controlled benchmark where Fisher gets even the direction of the shift wrong.

**Do Submillimeter Galaxies Trace Megaparsec Large-scale Structures?** · Canze Deng, Daizhong Liu, Helmut Dannerbauer et al. · [arXiv:2610.01136v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01136v1)
- TL;DR: Applying a Poisson Probability Method protocluster finder to 449 A3COSMOS submm galaxies recovers 15 known protoclusters and flags 26 new high-significance candidates, with 11-71% of SMGs sitting in overdense structures depending on the sample cut.

**hyprfine: simulating the 21-cm signal from the Dark Ages through to the Epoch of Reionization on a GPU** · Harry T. J. Bevins · [arXiv:2610.01865v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01865v1)
- TL;DR: A JAX-based, GPU-native, differentiable analytic simulator of the sky-averaged 21-cm signal from z≈1100 to z≈6 that runs in a fraction of a second — a candidate forward model for differentiable cosmological inference.

**ibUMAP: Coherent and Scalable Field Evaluation for UMAP Optimization** · Bin Chen, Yumeng Xue, Patrick Paetzold et al. · [arXiv:2610.01445v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01445v1)
- TL;DR: A field-based reformulation of UMAP's repulsive sampling using FFT-evaluated moments gives 3-6× speedups over umap-learn/cuML and more reproducible embeddings across reruns — directly relevant for UMAP on astronomical representations.
