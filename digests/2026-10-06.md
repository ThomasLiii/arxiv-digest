## Medium priority

**JESSNet: Joint dEconvolution and Sparse Separation Network for HI 21 cm foreground cleaning** · Athanasia Gkogkou, Victor Bonjean, Jean-Luc Starck et al. · [arXiv:2610.06199v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06199v1)
- TL;DR: A learned sparse-separation framework for 21 cm intensity-mapping cubes that bakes in a chromatic beam, a multi-scale "learnlet" wavelet regularizer, and survey-footprint masks, and recovers HI angular and frequency power spectra on simulated SKA-MID-like observations.

**Optimizing Color Selection for Cluster-Member Identification with SuperBIT** · Leila Ohashi, Sayan Saha, Jacqueline E. McCleary · [arXiv:2610.06635v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06635v1)
- TL;DR: Using spectroscopically confirmed members in SuperBIT's 2023 flight data, the (b–g) color gives the tightest red-sequence scatter and highest purity for red-sequence cluster-member picks — the baseline needed before weak-lensing richness maps.

**Testing Refracted Gravity with the kinematics of stacked galaxy clusters** · Dario Primignani, Lorenzo Pizzuti, Antonaldo Diaferio et al. · [arXiv:2610.06841v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06841v1)
- TL;DR: Stacked kinematics of 74 CIRS clusters are fit with Refracted Gravity using MG-MAMPOSSt, giving vacuum-permittivity estimates ε₀≈0.04–0.37 that are consistent across stacks but still noticeably sensitive to how the hot gas is parameterized.

**Testing ΛLTB as an alternative to dynamical dark energy** · Yukang Zhang, Pengjie Zhang · [arXiv:2610.05729v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05729v1)
- TL;DR: Confronting a mildly inhomogeneous ΛLTB cosmology with DESI DR2 BAO, Planck CMB, four SNe Ia compilations and the kSZ effect leaves a ~300 Mpc/h, ~10% density excess as a viable alternative to dynamical dark energy, with analytic distance and kSZ expressions provided for follow-up tests.

**Cusps, cores, and one acceleration: dark-matter haloes versus modified dynamics across the full SPARC sample** · Mikhael Da Silva · [arXiv:2610.05871v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05871v1)
- TL;DR: Uniform MCMC fits of NFW and MOND to all 175 SPARC rotation curves give a near-draw once ΛCDM concentration priors are imposed, with 53% of fitted halos lying >2σ below the expected concentration–mass relation — a population-level restatement of the core-cusp tension.
