## Medium priority

**A Graph Attention Network Framework for Simultaneous Prediction of Galaxy and Dark Matter Halo Properties in GAMA DR4** · Sandeep Aashish, Abhay V Hegde, Aditya Prakash Gupta et al. · [arXiv:2610.07836v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07836v1)
- TL;DR: GAMANet builds a 19,469-galaxy graph on GAMA DR4 using spatial proximity and FoF group membership as edges, then uses GATv2 attention over photometric/spectroscopic/morphological/environmental node features to jointly regress halo mass, stellar mass, and an sSFR proxy (R² ≈ 0.96 for halo mass).

**Mitigating prior-volume effects in galaxy clustering by adopting Mpc over Mpc/h units** · Andrea Pezzotta, Alejandro Pérez-Fernández, Giosuè Gambardella et al. · [arXiv:2610.08751v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08751v1)
- TL;DR: Switching EFT/VDG∞ clustering likelihoods to Mpc units removes a hidden h-induced prior correlation, pulling Stage-IV synthetic-data posteriors on w0waCDM back inside 1σ and matching invariant-prior or σ12 reparameterization fixes.

**Fast and accurate differentiable code of the galaxy power spectrum based on Eulerian and Lagrangian one-loop perturbation theories** · Yosuke Kobayashi, Kazuyuki Akitsu · [arXiv:2610.08023v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08023v1)
- TL;DR: `elf` is a JAX implementation of EPT and LPT one-loop galaxy power spectra that runs in a few ms on GPU, enables gradient-based inference (HMC), and holds ~0.01% error up to k ≲ 1 h/Mpc — the first auto-diff LPT one-loop code.

**The impact of feedback and cosmology on Cosmic Infrared Background cross-correlations** · Sara Maleubre, David Alonso, Reggie Dunsdon et al. · [arXiv:2610.08436v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08436v1)
- TL;DR: Running FLAMINGO hydro variants through tomographic ⟨b·ρSFR⟩ and CIB×shear predictions shows that current data already rule out strongly stellar-feedback and jet-driven-AGN models, demonstrating CIB cross-correlations as a feedback probe.

**The Role of the Cosmic Web in Galaxy Evolution Across 0.4<z<4** · Sina Taamoli, Bahram Mobasher, Hossein Hatamnia et al. · [arXiv:2610.07710v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07710v1)
- TL;DR: Multiscale Morphology Filter + FoF group assignment on COSMOS2020 maps stellar-mass and SFR dependence on cluster/filament/field environment across four redshift bins, with the environmental SFR trend reversing sign above z ≈ 2.

**Boosted Odds: Lower-Point Probes of Parity Violation with Momentum Fields** · Yong Sheng Yap, William R. Coulton, Jiamin Hou, Steven Gratton · [arXiv:2610.08671v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08671v1)
- TL;DR: Fisher-forecasts the density-density-momentum bispectrum (including a line-of-sight kSZ projection) as a parity-violation probe competitive with four-point density statistics but with a far smaller data vector, piggy-backing on existing galaxy×kSZ pipelines.
