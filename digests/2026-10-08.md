## High priority (≤5)

**A Gravitationally Lensed Low-Luminosity AGN From The Cosmic Noon** · Mićić, Holmes, Dai et al. · [arXiv:2610.10244](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10244v1)
- Why it matters: Directly leverages strong gravitational lensing — a core interest — to study an otherwise inaccessible population of faint AGN at high redshift.
- TL;DR: Combining HST imaging, Chandra X-ray data, and published cluster lens models for SDSS J2243−0935, the authors identify a triply-imaged faint X-ray source and show it is likely a low-luminosity AGN at z≈2.14, magnified by μ≈78. The intrinsic X-ray luminosity (~10⁴² erg/s) and low host stellar mass make it one of the first strongly lensed LLAGN at cosmic noon, demonstrating lensing as a route into the faint-AGN regime that X-ray surveys cannot otherwise reach.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**Constraints on Primordial Oscillatory Features from the Power Spectrum and Bispectrum of Redshift-Space Galaxy Clustering in DESI DR1** · Chen, Chudaykin, Ivanov et al. · [arXiv:2610.10530](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10530v1)
- Why it matters: Large-scale structure cosmology with DESI DR1, pushing the bispectrum into primordial-feature searches.
- TL;DR: First consistent inclusion of the galaxy bispectrum alongside the power spectrum in a DESI DR1 search for linear and logarithmic primordial oscillations, bounding amplitudes at ~3% and showing that a mild ~3σ preference for one logarithmic feature disappears when cosmology is marginalized — a direct demonstration of why marginalization matters for feature hunts.

**Angular Baryon Acoustic Oscillations with DESI DR1** · Paopiamsap, Mena-Fernández, Gil-Marín, Verde · [arXiv:2610.10215](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10215v1)
- Why it matters: Complementary cosmology-free BAO analysis of DESI DR1 using the 2D angular correlation function.
- TL;DR: Measures the transverse BAO scale α⊥(z) in tomographic redshift shells across DESI DR1 LRGs, ELGs, and QSOs to 2.5–4.2% precision, finding broad agreement with the 3D analysis but a ~2σ offset in the z≈1.49 QSO bin that pulls Ωm low — a pipeline that will tighten with future DESI data releases.

**The Fast Transient Foreground Fog of LSST** · Megias Homar, Kulkarni, Lage et al. · [arXiv:2610.09394](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09394v1)
- Why it matters: Characterizes a key contaminant for sub-minute transient searches with the Rubin/LSST survey.
- TL;DR: Uses pairs of 30-s LSST exposures covering 4,755 deg² to measure the rate of satellite/debris glints down to magnitude ~23, finding ~10⁷ events sky⁻¹ day⁻¹ and identifying a previously unreported medium-Earth-orbit mm-scale debris population; argues parallax from paired distant telescopes is the robust fix for distinguishing celestial bursts from this foreground.

**Scalable Logistic Gaussian Process Density Regression with Kinetic Langevin Sampling** · Paulin, Jung, Benczúr · [arXiv:2610.09591](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09591v1)
- Why it matters: A scalable, well-calibrated conditional-density method benchmarked explicitly on per-galaxy photometric redshifts.
- TL;DR: Bayesian logistic-GP photo-z estimator that trains on 3.9M galaxies on a single GPU using kinetic Langevin sampling and matches state-of-the-art tabular foundation models on density/calibration metrics.

**Effective Neutrino Mass and Apparent Phantom Crossing** · Rodrigues, Rodrigues, Santos da Costa, Alcaniz · [arXiv:2610.10195](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10195v1)
- Why it matters: Observational cosmology paper tying the DESI/Planck neutrino-mass tension to late-time dark energy phenomenology.
- TL;DR: Shows that a pNGB-coupled mass-varying-neutrino model fit to CMB+BAO+SN data can push the inferred effective neutrino mass below the normal-ordering floor while producing an apparent w=−1 crossing near z≈0.9 — without invoking a true phantom field.
