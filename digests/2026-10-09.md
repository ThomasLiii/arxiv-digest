## High priority (≤5)

**Data Representation Matters: Optimizing Machine Learning for X-ray Source Classification by Leveraging Spatio-Spectral Information** · Maggie Lieu, Ivan Valtchanov, Marguerite Pierre et al. · [arXiv:2610.12317](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12317v1)
- Why it matters: Direct benchmark of 1D/2D/3D CNNs applied to survey imaging (XMM-Newton event cubes) with GradCAM interpretability — squarely in the "CNNs applied to survey imaging" bucket.
- TL;DR: The authors compare CNN architectures on simulated XMM-Newton observations to classify AGN versus galaxy clusters, varying whether the input is spectral, imaging, or a full 3D spatio-spectral event cube. The 3D CNN on event cubes is most robust and, per 3D-GradCAM, learns to separate diffuse ICM thermal emission from localized non-thermal AGN signatures while implicitly handling background, bypassing manual background subtraction for large catalog pipelines.

**An analytical model for non-local stochastic field-level galaxy bias on the sphere** · Maximilian von Wietersheim-Kramsta, Nicolas Tessore, Benjamin Joachimi et al. · [arXiv:2610.12259](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12259v1)
- Why it matters: Field-level bias methodology aimed explicitly at Euclid, Rubin LSST, and Roman — a methods paper for the mission-science list.
- TL;DR: BOLTS treats galaxy bias as a statistically isotropic random field on the sphere, decomposing it into linear, non-local stochastic, and deterministic components in harmonic space. Fit to projected spectra from FLAMINGO hydrodynamical simulations, it recovers the equilateral bispectrum within sample variance down to ~10 Mpc scales for halo masses above 10^11.5 solar across 0<z<3, outperforming local deterministic bias models and enabling simulation-based inference on upcoming projected galaxy surveys.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**HI intensity mapping with MeerKAT: A measurement of the 21-cm auto-spectrum on cosmological scales** · MeerKLASS Collaboration, Matilde Barberi-Squarotti, José L. Bernal et al. · [arXiv:2610.12158](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12158v1)
- Why it matters: Observational large-scale-structure clustering measurement with a near-term pathway to SKA — matches the LSS/clustering medium bucket.
- TL;DR: The MeerKLASS team reports a 6.2-sigma direct detection of the 21-cm HI power spectrum at z=0.42 on cosmological scales from 236 deg^2 of 2021 MeerKAT data, using subset cross-spectra to suppress noise and residual systematics and constraining the HI clustering amplitude Ω_HI b_HI.
