## High priority (≤5)

**Mapping Dark Matter in Merging Clusters with Weak Lensing for CSST: Morphology and Filament Recovery** · Chen, Li, Wang et al. · [arXiv:2610.17857](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17857v1)
- Why it matters: Deep-learning mass reconstruction applied to realistic survey imaging — a direct hit on the "CNNs/ViTs on survey imaging" and weak-lensing interests, with a cluster-scale physics payoff.
- TL;DR: The authors run the MIU2Net deep-learning mass-reconstruction pipeline on mock CSST-like reduced-shear maps built from N-body simulations and show it recovers halo centres to ~0.1 h⁻¹ Mpc, pins down elongated-halo orientations, and traces cosmic-web filaments connecting halo pairs with sub-percent stability against shape noise. The reconstruction preserves low-contrast anisotropic structure better than Kaiser–Squires, pointing to statistical studies of cluster assembly and anisotropic accretion once CSST data arrive.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**Testing the 4-Point Correlation Function as a Probe of Parity Violation with the Quijote-Odd Simulations** · Ortolá Leonard, Slepian, Krolewski et al. · [arXiv:2610.18707](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18707v1)
- Why it matters: Clarifies how BOSS/DESI parity-violation searches respond to noise in large-scale structure clustering — core LSS methodology.
- TL;DR: Running BOSS- and DESI-style 4PCF parity statistics on Quijote-Odd halo catalogues yields no detectable parity-odd signal; the measurement is Poisson-noise-dominated and distinguishable from the expected signal response, giving a controlled baseline for interpreting real-survey results.

**The ePESSTO+ census of ZTF nuclear transients. I. What flares in galactic nuclei?** · Concepción, Petrushevska, Leloudas et al. · [arXiv:2610.18633](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18633v1)
- Why it matters: Prototype rare-transient filter for the LSST-era alert deluge — anomaly/rare-object discovery in survey streams.
- TL;DR: A real-time Lasair filter on 16 months of ZTF alerts, selecting nuclear transients without photometric cuts, isolates 10 confirmed TDEs plus one Bowen fluorescence flare out of 476 candidates, with g−r colour and rise time cleanly separating TDEs from the supernova background and recovering 40% of known TDEs in the window.

**Structure and evolution of cosmic voids within DESI DR1** · Monllor-Berbegal, Planelles, Quilis · [arXiv:2610.18348](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18348v1)
- Why it matters: New public void catalogue from DESI DR1 — straightforward large-scale-structure deliverable aligned with stage-IV survey analyses.
- TL;DR: After redshift-space-distortion preprocessing and velocity-field reconstruction, the AVISM finder identifies 1,114 BGS and 216 LRG voids in two redshift bins, with sizes up to 65 cMpc. Size functions and density profiles match theoretical expectations, the linear void bias is 1.31 (BGS) and 2.14 (LRG), and both catalogues are released.
