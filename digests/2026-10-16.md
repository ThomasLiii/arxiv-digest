## High priority (≤5)

**CHARIZARD: A High-throughput Generic Pipeline for Present and Upcoming Interferometers** · Pal, Kale · [arXiv:2610.21158](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21158v1)
- Why it matters: An end-to-end pipeline whose central design choice is distributing self-calibration and imaging — a direct hit on the self-calibration / imaging-pipeline interest for upcoming interferometric surveys.
- TL;DR: CHARIZARD takes raw measurement sets through flagging, cross-calibration, self-calibration and full-Stokes imaging while sharding the work by sub-band and target field across SLURM/PBS clusters inside a rootless container. On a 1.5 TB MeerKAT polarisation track it reaches 4.11 μJy/beam with σ_I/σ_V ≈ 1.010 and residual on-axis instrumental polarisation of 0.13% linear / 0.06% circular, finishing 48.6 core-hours of work in 18.4 wall hours. The authors position it as a template for the scheduling layer that next-generation radio arrays will need, independent of the underlying algorithms.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**Model-independent parameters from DESI DR1 LRG2 with an explicitly varying power-spectrum shape** · Song, Icaza-Lizaola, Oh et al. · [arXiv:2610.21235](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21235v1)
- Why it matters: Joint fitting of model-independent distance/growth parameters with primordial-shape parameters on DESI DR1 — core LSS/clustering methodology.
- TL;DR: A hybrid perturbative + N-body-template emulator lets the authors vary primordial shape parameters alongside α_⊥, α_∥ and fσ₈ on the DESI DR1 LRG2 sample, finding that with a tight Planck shape prior the result shifts by less than 1/20σ relative to fixed-shape fits and projects consistently onto flat ΛCDM with h = 0.724 ± 0.017.

**Minkowski Functionals of CII Line Intensity Maps: Probing Astrophysics and Primordial Features** · Acharya, Chingangbam · [arXiv:2610.21710](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21710v1)
- Why it matters: Non-Gaussian LSS statistics applied to a line-intensity map, with a direct forecast for an upcoming survey (CCAT).
- TL;DR: Semi-numerical CII intensity-map simulations at z ≈ 6 and 7.6 show that Minkowski functionals constrain the halo-luminosity astrophysical model under CCAT-like noise and can disentangle a bump-like primordial-power-spectrum feature from those nuisance parameters when combined across smoothing scales and redshifts.

**Properties and limitations of linear models for mutual coupling in 21 cm arrays** · MacKay, Pascua, Rath et al. · [arXiv:2610.22197](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22197v1)
- Why it matters: Mutual-coupling mitigation is the gating systematic for 21 cm cosmology, and this paper reframes the common linear-coupling model in terms of what it can and cannot absorb.
- TL;DR: Full-wave HERA-like simulations split mutual coupling into an antenna mode (translated isolated responses) and a structural remainder; the antenna mode holds only ~1% of the beam-perturbation power, so linear re-radiation models alone are near-useless, but sky-weighted beam correlations plus relaxed coefficient constraints and holographic back-projection recover order-of-magnitude suppression in idealised cases.

**Thermal Sunyaev–Zel'dovich Effect in the circumgalactic medium — III: does richness matter?** · Das, Chiang, Mathur · [arXiv:2610.21628](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21628v1)
- Why it matters: A 7-million-galaxy ACT+Planck × DESI Legacy stacking analysis — the kind of large-scale cross-correlation that feeds Stage-IV cosmology inference.
- TL;DR: Stacking tSZ on DESI Legacy groups and galaxies down to 10¹²·² M⊙ at 5.8σ, the authors find that mass–richness bins span a zoo of thermal-pressure profiles from standard GNFW to flattened and miscentered shapes, and that CGM thermal energy scales below self-similarity by 7.3σ, arguing that mass alone is an inadequate axis for calibrating energy–mass relations.
