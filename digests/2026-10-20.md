## High priority (≤5)

**Plate-YOLO: An End-to-End Object Detection Framework for Robust Astrometric Registration of Historical Plates** · Xu, Shang, Shen et al. · [arXiv:2610.25677](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25677v1)
- Why it matters: A CNN detector adapted for degraded survey imaging — direct hit on the ViT/CNN-for-survey-imaging interest, with a self-calibration-adjacent angle (Gaia-guided fine-tuning to rescue plates the classical pipeline refuses).
- TL;DR: The authors retrofit YOLOv5 with Space-to-Depth stem, SimAM attention, a high-resolution P2 head and a Normalized Wasserstein Distance loss so that it can both detect sources and distinguish stars from artifacts on scanned historical plates. A Gaia-anchored fine-tuning loop produces target-archive labels on the fly, and on 150 UBAI plates where the conventional pipeline had failed, Plate-YOLO plus fine-tuning recovers 146/150 astrometric solutions while running source extraction in 6.6 s/plate against SExtractor's 16.7 s.

**Neutrino self-interactions in light of DESI and CMB-SPA** · Noriega, De-Santiago, Garcia-Arroyo et al. · [arXiv:2610.26654](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26654v1)
- Why it matters: Combined DESI DR1 full-shape + Lyα + Planck/ACT/SPT constraints on a BSM scenario — representative of current LSS+CMB joint analyses.

**Cosmological implications of two mock TianQin standard siren catalogs generated under different background cosmological models** · Li, Wang, Zhu · [arXiv:2610.26727](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26727v1)
- Why it matters: Forecast showing that the fiducial model used to generate siren mocks can push the inferred dark-energy preference by over a sigma — a mock-design issue worth flagging.
