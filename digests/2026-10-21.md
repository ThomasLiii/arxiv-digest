## High priority (≤5)

**The "Rubin 2036: Opportunities for Discovery" Workshop: A Community Roadmap for the Future of the Vera C. Rubin Observatory After the LSST** · Plazas Malagón, Allen, Anand et al. · [arXiv:2610.28482](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28482v1)
- Why it matters: The continued-surveying working group explicitly discusses a machine-learning foundation model pretrained on Rubin imaging for detection, deblending, and classification — a direct hit on the foundation-models-for-survey-imaging interest, plus photometric-redshift and high-z LSS angles.
- TL;DR: The paper summarizes a 100-participant KIPAC/SLAC workshop that laid out a post-LSST roadmap for the Rubin Observatory across five working groups: continued surveying (including an imaging foundation model), cadence (transits, SN Ia, time-delay cosmography), filters (broad/medium/narrowband for photo-z, high-z LSS, AGN reverberation), multi-messenger follow-up (standard sirens, kilonovae), and technology upgrades (fast readout, new shutters, summit edge computing). Cross-cutting themes include AI/ML, Chilean co-leadership, and satellite-megaconstellation mitigation.
