## High priority (≤5)

**Self-Supervised Representation Learning for JWST Galaxy Images and Low Resolution Spectra** · Yang Cheng, Morgan Fouesneau, Ivelina G. Momcheva et al. · [arXiv:2610.31078](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31078v1)
- Why it matters: A multimodal foundation-model-style approach on JWST imaging plus PRISM spectra — exactly the AION-1-adjacent direction I follow for astronomy foundation models.
- TL;DR: The authors train a joint embedding over single-band F150W cutouts and 1–2 μm PRISM spectra, then use the learned representation to predict stellar masses at 1<z≤3 (R²≈0.9, σ_NMAD=0.24 dex) even when only one modality is available at inference. A CLIP-style cross-modal alignment lifts single-modality performance by ΔR²=+0.04 for images and +0.09 for spectra, closing much of the gap to joint inference.

## Medium priority (≤8)

**Photometric classification and out-of-taxonomy detection of ZTF transients with FLARE** · Argyro Sasli, Felipe Fontinele Nunes, Xuanrui Zhang et al. · [arXiv:2610.30656](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30656v1)
- Why it matters: Anomaly / rare-object discovery in a time-domain survey stream, with a working review loop in production — the kind of pipeline-level system that matters for LSST-era discovery.
- TL;DR: FLARE classifies ZTF transients across six classes and flags out-of-taxonomy objects; a simulated reviewer loop recovers 83% of out-of-class objects after inspecting 15% of the stream, and feature-space retraining with 11 labelled novel examples recovers 85% of hidden TDEs.

**SpecLess: A Multi Survey Python Package for Machine Learning Classification of Galaxy Morphology and Activity from Photometry** · Uzay Aydin, Mehmet Tanriver · [arXiv:2610.31545](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31545v1)
- Why it matters: Quantifies how well photometric classifiers transfer across SDSS, KiDS, and DESI Legacy imaging — a useful baseline for the cross-survey generalization question that representation-learning methods have to beat.

**Sound-Horizon-Agnostic Forecast for Constraints on H₀ and Σmν from BAO, CMB Lensing, Supernovae and LSST-like Galaxy Lensing** · Seyed Hamidreza Mirpoorian, Helena García Escudero, Levon Pogosian et al. · [arXiv:2610.31660](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31660v1)
- Why it matters: Weak-lensing-forward forecast that treats r_d as a free parameter and carefully marginalizes over photo-z, shear bias, IA, and baryons for an LSST-like survey.

**A cosmic filament catalog for COSMOS-Web Data Release 1 across 0.5 < z < 3.5** · Qiaoyang Hao, Tao Wang, Weishan Zhu et al. · [arXiv:2610.31168](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31168v1)
- Why it matters: Builds a large-scale-structure product by propagating photo-z PDFs into stacked DisPerSE filament probability maps, calibrated against TNG mocks — relevant to the LSS/photo-z pipeline side.

**Assessing the consistency of the CPL dark-energy model with cosmological data: Simulation-calibrated neural posterior disagreement** · Tian-Yang Sun, Tian-Nuo Li, Guo-Hong Du et al. · [arXiv:2610.31044](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31044v1)
- Why it matters: A simulation-based inference model-check using disagreement between NPE and flow-matching posteriors, with simulation-calibrated p-values — a methods-level take on SBI diagnostics for cosmology.
